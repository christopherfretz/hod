## Decisions, and why

- **A vector is a `Val::Agg`**, not a new `Val` variant: other crates'
  tests match `Val` exhaustively (`hod_a64/tests/common/aggr_gen.rs`), and
  "a value is its image" is already what `Agg` is.
- **Poison per lane, observed late.** Scalar poison traps at once; a
  vector lane cannot (vectorized code computes lanes it never reads), so
  a lane is poison until something makes a scalar of it.
- **A poison divisor lane traps**, as LangRef's undefined behavior;
  **`freeze` keeps poison** (§12).
- **Vector loads are lenient per lane** (an unwritten byte makes its lane
  poison) and stores of poison lanes clear the written flags, so poison
  round-trips through memory and a scalar read of it still traps.
- **Odd lanes are refused**: `llc` 19 and 22 store `<4 x i3>` in a way
  that is not LangRef's packing, so there is no proven image.

## Validation

- `tests/vector_oracle.rs`: 3,000 functions, 10 files, against `llc -O0
  -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` of LLVM 22.1 and 19.1, AArch64, native:
  2,917 agree byte for byte with both (3,940 poison bytes wildcarded),
  83 trap in the interpreter and are not run (68 `nan-result`, 13
  `poison-lane`, 2 `division-by-zero`). About 20 s.
- `vector_programs_agree_with_llvm`: 66 generated programs with `vectors`
  per major at `-O0` (SelectionDAG) and `-O2`.
- Existing seeds unchanged: hashes of three option sets fixed before the
  switch, and `existing_seeds_keep_their_programs`.
- Mutations, each reverted:
  1. mask lanes most significant bit first (`vector.rs` `get`/`set`):
     `layout_in_memory`; 67 of 293 oracle functions differ;
  2. a lane shifted by the width or more traps instead of being poison
     (`int_lane`): `integer_lanes_and_poison`, `compares_and_selects`,
     `reductions`, `vectors_as_values`;
  3. stored poison lanes left written (`vector_store`):
     `element_access_and_shuffles`; 17 of 293 oracle functions differ.

## LLVM bugs found (steered around in the oracle, §10.3)

- `trunc <3 x i64>` / `<3 x i128>` to `<3 x i8>`: SelectionDAG of llc 19
  and 22, at `-O0` and `-O2`, gives wrong lanes (`ff ff 00` for
  `ff 0b ff`); GlobalISel is right. Only these two of a 144-case matrix.
- `llvm.vector.insert` in llc 19: "Don't know how to widen the operands
  for INSERT_SUBVECTOR" for non-power-of-two lengths, one-lane
  subvectors and masks.
- `<4 x i3>` stores not packed as LangRef describes (llc 19 and 22).
