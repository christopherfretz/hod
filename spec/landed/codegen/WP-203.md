# WP-203 — vectors wider than 128 bits across calls on x86-64

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/abi.rs` (`Class::Wide`, the `ymm` pieces of
§3.2 of `spec/abi-x64.md`, `Features::assumed`, the `preserve_*`
refusal), `features.rs` (which CPUs and features make AVX known,
`FnFeatures::vzeroupper`), `call.rs` (the 32-aligned argument area, the
`vzeroupper` plan), `frame.rs` (where `vzeroupper` is written),
`vector.rs` (`load_wide`, `store_wide`), `target.rs` (their dispatch),
`spec/abi-x64.md` §1.3, §3.2, §4.3, §5.1, §8.7, §9.1, §10, §11.1. Tests:
`crates/hod_x64/tests/abi/wide.rs`, `vzeroupper.rs`,
`wide_vector_abi.rs`, and rows in `abi/errors.rs`, `abi_followups.rs`,
`features_census.rs`.

## What is built

Under AVX (`+avx`, or a CPU `llc` 19 and 22 both give AVX), a vector of
17 to 32 bytes is one `ymm` (`ymm0`..`ymm7` in, `ymm0`..`ymm3` out,
shared with the `xmm` count); a power-of-two vector over 32 bytes is
32-byte `ymm` pieces; a non-power-of-two vector widened past 32 bytes
travels element by element as without AVX. On the stack a wide piece
takes a 32-byte slot aligned to 32; in a variadic signature it always
goes on the stack and is not counted in `al`. Aggregates (clang's
`struct{__m256i}`, a pair, a union, `std::array<__m256,2>`) reach hod as
clang writes them, by value in `ymm` or `byval` aligned to 32.
`vzeroupper` goes where `llc -O0` writes it (§8.7).

Without AVX nothing changed: the split form stays byte-identical.

## What is refused

`x64:wide-vector-abi`, with the function's features: under AVX-512 a
vector of more than 32 bytes (`zmm`, which needs EVEX moves hod does not
encode) or of `i1`; under AVX that is only assumed (a CPU or a feature
`llc` 19 or 22 may read without AVX: an unknown CPU, `+avx10.1-256`,
`+avx10.2-256`, `+amx-avx512`) any vector AVX would put in `ymm`; a
`preserve_mostcc` or `preserve_allcc` signature with a `ymm` piece.
Coroutines need no refusal: a clang C++20 coroutine with `__m256i`
parameters and a `co_yield` of one runs.

## Evidence

- `abi::wide_vector_grid`: 1,119 signatures with `ymm` pieces (every
  element type, mixed with integers, floats, 128-bit vectors, aggregates,
  more than eight vector arguments, `byval`/`sret`, variadic calls) under
  `+avx`, `+avx2`, `sandybridge`, `haswell` and `znver3`, each executed
  both ways against `llc` 19 and 22 (`-O0`, `-O0` SelectionDAG, `-O2`):
  zero disagreements. Under `skylake-avx512`, 635 accepted signatures
  have 5,080 `llc` conventions equal to `+avx`'s (`qemu` has no AVX-512
  to run them); 391 refused as `x64:wide-vector-abi`.
- `wide_vector_abi`: 162 runs per test (C++ from clang 19 and 22, and IR
  shapes), each unit built by `llc`, hod and hod under `Stress::ALL`,
  mixed all nine ways, linked by bfd, lld and mold, run under
  `qemu-x86_64 -cpu max` for `-mavx2`, `-march=haswell` and
  `-march=znver3`: all print what `llc`/`llc` prints.
- `vzeroupper::vzeroupper_where_llc_writes_it`: 15 functions under nine
  machines against `llc` 19 and 22, `FastISel` and `SelectionDAG`; 11 of
  15 write one on the AVX machines, none on the others.
- The six distinct simdjson Haswell modules saved by WP-201 compile with
  `hod --strict -mcpu=haswell` (three were refused on
  `x64:wide-vector-abi` before).
- Every 10th entry of the x86-64 corpus (646 modules), the base commit's
  hod against this one's: 603 objects byte-identical, 43 refused by both
  with the same message, none different.
- A stack protector, inline probes (a 70,000-byte frame), and
  `cf-protection` together with nine `__m256i` arguments (one on the
  stack) run correctly, linked by bfd and lld.
- gdb (`gdb-multiarch` against `qemu-x86_64 -g`) prints a `__m256i`
  parameter of a hod-built function as `{11, 22, 33, 44}`.

## Where hod differs from `llc`, by rule

§8.7 of `spec/abi-x64.md`: one shared `ret` for the `vzeroupper` before
it; intrinsics lowered to library calls are no calls; the rule is
`FastISel`'s where `SelectionDAG` splits 256-bit integer arithmetic
without AVX2. §1.3: `llc` 19 does not know `diamondrapids`, `novalake`
and `wildcatlake`, and gives `avx10.1`, `avx10.2` and `avx10.2-512` no
AVX; hod follows 22 for both.

## Found on the way

kiln (21caaf874) with `-mavx2` passes `struct{__m256i}`, a union of one
and `__m256d` `byval` and returns them through `sret`, where clang passes
and returns them in `ymm`: kiln's objects do not link with clang's at
those signatures.
