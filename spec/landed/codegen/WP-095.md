# WP-095 — the short tail, second part

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/tail2.rs`, the hooks below,
`crates/hod_a64/tests/tail2.rs`, `crates/hod_bc/tests/annotations.rs`,
the heading "The short tail, second part (WP-095)" in `spec/codegen.md`
§5.5.1, `spec/ir.md` §4.2/§4.3, `spec/ll.md` §5.9, `spec/bc.md` §5.5, and
the support-matrix rows.

## What is lowered

- Scalar `bfloat`: `fpext`, `fptrunc` (from `float` with `llc`'s NaN arm,
  from `double` through `FCVTXN`), `fadd`/`fsub`/`fmul`/`fdiv` in single
  precision, `frem` through `fmodf`, `fneg`, every `fcmp` predicate,
  `fptosi`/`fptoui`, `sitofp`/`uitofp` from 1 to 64 bits (round to odd for
  more than 16), `extractvalue`/`insertvalue`; as `llc` without
  `FEAT_BF16`.
- `extractelement` from `<N x i128>` (`<1 x i128>` in a `Q` register,
  wider through memory), constant or variable index.
- Label differences in initializers (`trunc (sub (ptrtoint blockaddress
  (@f, %a)), (ptrtoint blockaddress(@f, %b)))`, any mix of `add`, `sub`,
  `trunc` and numbers with one block added and one subtracted, of one
  function): `ConstPool::block_diff`, `FixupTarget::BlockDiff`, written as
  a number by module emission (no relocation, as the assembler folds
  `llc`'s `.word .La-.Lb`); `hod_interp` gives `a - b` of its block
  addresses.
- `blockaddress` in data of a function whose only `indirectbr` is
  unreachable: the function gets its `block_offsets`.
- `bitcast` of a constant vector of numbers (at most 128 bits) to a
  scalar: folded to its image by `fold_cast`.
- `preserve_mostcc` as caller and callee: assigned as `ccc`; the callee
  saves `x9`..`x15` after the frame record, with `DW_CFA_offset` rows,
  and reloads them with `DW_CFA_restore` rows in every exit sequence.
- `@llvm.global.annotations` in `llvm.metadata`: dropped by `hod_ll` and
  `hod_bc`. `"fmv-features"`: a dropped attribute.

## Hooks and edits outside `tail2.rs`

- `hod_a64/src/float.rs`: `flags`, `compare_format`, `binary`, `cast`
  call `tail2`. `target.rs`: the `ExtractElement` arm, `assign`.
  `frame.rs`: `begin`, `end`, `body_rows`, `teardown` (one call each).
  `constaddr.rs`: a `BlockDiff` arm (refused). `lib.rs`.
- `hod_ir`: `flatten.rs` (`BlockDiff`, `block_diff`; `Fixup` is 40 bytes,
  was 32), `fold.rs` (`vector_bits`), `classify/walk.rs` (label
  difference rows, `block_address` factored out), `classify/matrix.rs`,
  `attrs.rs` (`fmv-features`, and `PREFIXED_STRING_ROWS` moved by one).
- `hod_cg/src/driver.rs` (`mark_reachable`), `hod_cg/src/emit/mod.rs`
  (`relocate_data`), `hod_interp/src/interp.rs` (`fixup_value`).
- `hod_ll/src/globals.rs`, `hod_bc/src/module.rs`: the annotations global.
- Tests that changed with the behavior: `hod_a64/tests/float.rs` (`bfloat`
  compiles now), `hod_ir/tests/{classify_matrix.rs, classify_support/
  mod.rs, flatten_diff.rs, fold_diff.rs, print_data.rs}`,
  `hod_ll/tests/differential/{objects.rs, canonical.rs}`.

## What stays refused, by name

- `a64:bfloat-fp128`: `bfloat` to or from `fp128`.
- `operand:block-diff`: a label difference as an operand (in an aggregate
  too). Other shapes keep `const-expr:sub` / `const-expr:trunc`.
- `const-expr:sub` of a number minus an address (`spec/ir.md` §4.2).
- `metadata-arg:llvm.read_volatile_register.i64`: needs metadata operands
  of intrinsics in the IR (WP-064) and the body reader.
- `debug:dbg-value`: no shape in the corpus maps to a whole-function
  location (`spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1 "WP-095").
- `abi:bfloat-array` (`hod_a64::abi`, WP-082's file).

## Evidence

- `crates/hod_a64/tests/tail2.rs`, 15 tests, under bfd, lld and mold, hod
  plain, `Stress::ALL` and far, against `llc -O0 -global-isel=0
  -fast-isel=0`, and `hod_interp` for `i128` lanes, label differences
  and `preserve_mostcc`: 9 `bfloat` tables (≈600 calls with ties, NaNs,
  infinities, the round-to-odd edges) and `frem` linked with `-lm`;
  `FCVTXN` disassembled; extractelement 66 calls; label differences of 2,
  4 and 8 bytes and with an addend, and no relocation; vector bitcasts;
  the unreachable `indirectbr`; `preserve_mostcc` 30 calls with a stack
  argument, and an assembly caller with sentinels in `x9`..`x15` whose
  callee unwinds the stack with `_Unwind_Backtrace`: the unwinder finds
  the sentinels in the caller's frame and the registers hold them after
  the return (hod in three modes and `llc`).
- `crates/hod_bc/tests/annotations.rs`: both rules, LLVM 19 and 22
  bitcode.
- Mutations, each caught and reverted: (1) the rounding bit taken from
  bit 17 (`bfloat_agrees_with_llc`); (2) the `x9`..`x15` CFI rows one slot
  off (`preserve_most_saves_and_describes_x9_to_x15`); (3) the label
  difference written `b - a` (`label_differences_agree_with_the_interpreter`).
- No `tests/ir` seed: the label-difference and `preserve_mostcc` cases
  need `indirectbr` tables and assembly the tests above already run.
