# WP-067 — AArch64 floating point, the leftovers of WP-039

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/float.rs` (`load`, `store`, the `fp128`
compares, conversions and `frem`, `half` `frem`), the two hooks in
`crates/hod_a64/src/mem.rs`, 18 entries appended to `runtime_fns!` in
`crates/hod_cg/src/sym.rs`, `common::image_to_fpr` (appended) and the
non-scalar arm of `common::materialize_fpr_const`,
`crates/hod_a64/tests/float.rs`, the heading "Floating point, the
leftovers (WP-067)" in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1, the `WP-067` line of
`tests/common/groups.rs`, and the support-matrix rows.

## What is lowered

- `load` and `store` of every `Fpr`-class type (`half`, `bfloat`,
  `float`, `double`, `fp128`, 64- and 128-bit vectors, bit-packed
  vectors of narrow elements included), through every addressing form
  of `mem.rs`, one access of exactly the store size. Atomic accesses
  stay refused by `mem.rs` (`a64:atomic`).
- `fcmp` of `fp128`, all 16 predicates, by `__eqtf2`, `__lttf2`,
  `__letf2` and `__unordtf2` (two calls for `ueq` and `one`); fused
  into `select` like the other formats.
- `fpext` to and `fptrunc` from `fp128` for `half`, `float` and
  `double`; `fptosi`, `fptoui`, `sitofp` and `uitofp` between `fp128`
  and every integer width 1..64; `frem` of `fp128` (`fmodl`) and of
  `half` (`fmodf` in single precision, exact).
- Non-zero vector constants and every other non-scalar `Fpr` constant,
  symbol addresses in them included, from the constant's image.
- Matrix: `inst:load`, `inst:store`, `inst:bitcast`, `inst:select`,
  `inst:freeze` gain the float classes (`Half`, `BFloat`, `Float`,
  `Double`, `Fp128`; the `Mem` class of the shared rows is not this
  package's); `fadd`, `fsub`, `fmul`, `fdiv`, `fneg`, `fptrunc`,
  `fpext`, `fcmp`, `fptoui`, `fptosi`, `uitofp`, `sitofp` gain `Fp128`;
  `frem` is on for `Half`, `Float`, `Double`, `Fp128`; `type:half` and
  `type:fp128` are on.

## What is refused, by name

- `a64:bfloat`, `a64:vector-op`, `a64:vector-fcmp`, `a64:vector-icmp`,
  `a64:vector-select`: unchanged (the vector compares wait for
  WP-040's `<N x i1>`).
- `a64:fp-cast`: a cast between `fp128` and a vector, and the vector
  casts WP-039 refused.
- `a64:constant-relocation`: a constant image with a relocation that is
  not an aligned 8-byte symbol address.
- `a64:fp128-libcall` and `a64:half-frem` are no longer produced; the
  names stay as constants.

## Evidence (`tests/float.rs`)

- `loads_and_stores_agree_with_the_model_and_llc`: 1,477 functions and
  8,862 calls over 23 types and every addressing form (pointer, constant
  offsets in and out of the scaled range, register and scaled indices,
  `ptradd` chains, GOT and `dso_local` globals), into a buffer that ends
  at a guard page. hod plain, hod under `Stress::ALL` and
  `llc -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` print the loaded bits and every
  changed byte; all equal a Rust model of exact-size accesses.
- `every_form_agrees_with_llc_and_the_interpreter`: now 724 functions
  and 46,749 calls; 20,809 compared with `hod_interp`, 11,076 with a C
  oracle (`long double` and `_Float16` expressions compiled by the
  system compiler: every `fp128` operation, compare and conversion, and
  `half` `frem`). New special values for `fp128`: the largest subnormal,
  the integer boundaries around 2^31, 2^32, 2^63, 2^64. 4 results differ
  from llc in the NaN only. It also covers `fp128` compares fused into
  `select` of `double`, `float`, `half` and `<4 x i32>`, vector
  constants (in arithmetic, returned, stored, with symbol addresses),
  and loops carrying 17 `Fpr` values each through `phi`s.
- `atomic_fp_accesses_stay_refused`.

## Mutations (each caught, then reverted)

1. A store always of the 16-byte view: the guard page kills the run.
2. `ogt` without swapping the `__lttf2` operands: 368 calls differ (C
   and llc).
3. The `ccmp` of `ueq`/`one` with flags 0 instead of Z: 371 differ.
4. `uitofp` to `fp128` sign-extending odd widths: 43 differ.

## Follow-ups

- `crates/hod_a64/src/mem.rs`: the "# Errors" docs of `load` and `store`
  still name `LOAD_FPR` / `STORE_FPR`, which are now unused.
- `crates/hod_a64/src/control.rs:71` (the `condbr` fusion point): when
  it is filled, `float::flags` of an `fp128` compare makes calls, which
  displace every register; nothing may be pinned across it.

## Corpus (82 files with an `fp128` compare, conversion or `frem`)

`hod --strict` with `HOD_CENSUS=1` on this branch: no reason of any of
them names a float construct any more. 34 are now refused only for
`inst:call` and `attr:target-features` (calls are WP-038's); the other
48 also need variadic functions, aggregates or intrinsics; 2
(`builtins/complex-1.c`, both LLVM versions) are malformed input to
`hod_ll` ("metadata !0 is defined twice").
