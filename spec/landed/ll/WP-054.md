# WP-054 — hod_ll: debug information

Landed: `spec/ll.md` §7.4 and §11.9 (LLVM's debug information read into
`hod_ir`'s tables), the debug names of §3.4, and `spec/ir.md` §9.7 (the
printer writes it back). `classify` still refuses `debug:*`: code
generation from these tables is another work package.

## What it did

| where | contents |
|---|---|
| `crates/hod_ll/src/debug.rs` (new) | every `DI*` node, distinct or uniqued, forward references and cycles (reserve, then define); tuples, expressions, locations in place; LLVM's parser checks (a field once, of its shape, the required ones); `debug:invalid` where LLVM's verifier would drop or reject what the module uses |
| `crates/hod_ll/src/body.rs`, `call.rs` | `!dbg` through `set_loc_since`; `#dbg_declare`, `#dbg_value`, `#dbg_assign`, `#dbg_label` and the `llvm.dbg.*` calls into the body's slot variables, declarations and records; `BodyDebug` and `DebugRecordText` are gone |
| `crates/hod_ll/src/globals.rs`, `meta.rs`, `parser.rs`, `lib.rs` | a function's subprogram, a variable's global-variable expressions, `!llvm.dbg.cu`; `symbol_dbg()` is gone |
| `crates/hod_ir/src/debug/` | `DiShape`, `DiFieldOut`, `field_shape`, `each_field`; a subprogram's LLVM default is a definition; `define_distinct_loc`, `is_distinct_loc` |
| `crates/hod_ir/src/print/debug.rs` (new), `body.rs`, `module.rs` | nodes, locations, `!llvm.dbg.cu`, `!dbg` on functions and variables, records in both dialects; `#dbg_assign` refused |
| `crates/hod_ll/examples/ll_print.rs` (new) | `ll_print <major> in.ll`: read and print back, by hand |

Outside the listed files, mechanically: `crates/hod_ir/src/lib.rs`
(re-exports), `crates/hod_ir/src/builder.rs` (`set_loc_since`),
`crates/hod_ir/src/verify.rs` (`Body::verify_debug`, the debug checks of
`Body::verify` alone).

## The body form

What the bitcode reader (WP-055) fills, by `FunctionBuilder`
(`spec/ir.md` §6.6): the location of each instruction (`set_loc` /
`set_loc_since`), `slot_var` for an entry `alloca`'s declaration,
`dbg_declare` for any other, `dbg_value` / `dbg_value_list` /
`dbg_assign` / `dbg_label` before the next instruction. Nodes and
locations are in `Module::debug`, by `DiId` and `LocId`.

## Evidence

- `tests/debug.rs`: clang 19 and 22 `-g` (C++ and C, `-O0`, and `-O2`
  without assignment tracking) and kiln++ `-g` output: read, printed,
  `llc -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` on both; `llvm-dwarfdump
  --debug-info --debug-line` alike, offsets aside (and, for LLVM 22
  `-O2`, `is_stmt` and repeated rows: Key Instructions are dropped). 57
  malformed snippets against `llvm-as` 19 and 22.
- By hand (`ll_print`, the same comparison): the 26 corpus files with
  debug information, both majors, alike.
- `cargo xtask roundtrip`: the 14 `debug-info` lines of
  `tests/roundtrip/expected-aarch64.tsv` pass now and are removed.
- Cost (`tests/perf.rs`, release, best of 5): clang 22 `stl.cpp`
  without `-g`, module level 4.35 ms (4.4 before); with `-g` (9.68 MiB,
  53,896 nodes) module level 33.5 ms, 289 MiB/s, module and bodies
  51.3 ms, 189 MiB/s, peak 6.3 MiB above the text; `llvm-as` 22 375 ms,
  25.8 MiB/s, 104 MiB resident.

## Open

- `#dbg_assign` is read and not printed: `hod_ir` does not keep the
  `DIAssignID`. Clang at `-O2 -g` writes them.
- Key Instructions (`atomGroup`, `atomRank`) are dropped.
- `DIImportedEntity`'s `elements`, and the `DiOther` kinds, stay
  unsupported.
- `placed_after` (`crates/hod_ir/src/print/debug.rs`) is O(instructions ×
  declarations) for declarations of instruction results.
