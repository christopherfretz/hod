# WP-059 — `hod_dwarf`: globals, namespaces, imports, template parameters

The design is `spec/dwarf.md`. The contract of what landed is §6.11 (with
its refusal table), the family rows 81 to 86 of §6.1 and the template
paragraph of §6.8. This file records what the work package did, what it
measured, and what it left.

## What landed

- New modules in `crates/hod_dwarf/src`:
  - `scope.rs`: the refusal names of §6.11 and `ScopeDies`;
  - `global.rs`: globals and static member definitions of every shape
    (`DW_AT_location` with `addrx`, `DW_AT_const_value` for a constant
    expression, AArch64 TLS without a location, `DW_AT_specification`,
    linkage name, alignment), in their namespace or class scope;
  - `template.rs`: template parameters (type, value, template template,
    packs) on classes, subprograms, variables and template aliases;
    value parameters that are addresses take a `.debug_addr` slot
    (`place_symbols`); `unsigned_di_type` follows `llc`'s
    `isUnsignedDIType`, an enumeration through its base type;
  - `import.rs`: imported modules and declarations at unit, namespace and
    function scope, written at seal before the definitions are finished;
  - `spec.rs`: member declarations on demand, `DW_AT_specification` for
    out-of-line definitions, and a definition's own `DW_AT_type` when its
    return type differs from its declaration's.
- Hooks, one call each at the site that refused: `die.rs` (discovery of
  a global, the `.debug_addr` count, the DIE kinds), `func.rs`
  (`subprogram_parent`, `seal`, `subprogram_die`), `types.rs` (the
  template task and its call sites) and `hod_cg/src/emit/lines.rs`
  (`place_symbols` after seal).
- `abbrev.rs`: families 81 to 86 appended (after WP-085's 72 to 80; `CODE_END` 10631); 35 and 42
  refined.
- Refusals removed: `debug:specification`, `debug:specification-type`,
  `debug:out-of-line-definition`, and `debug:die-unsupported` for
  globals. Added: `debug:template-address` (an address parameter of a
  symbol that is not defined) and `debug:declaration-scope`, among the
  rows of §6.11.

## Evidence

- `hod_dwarf/tests/scope.rs`: plan-level refusals and base cases.
- `hod_a64/tests/dies_scope.rs`: trees of `globals.cpp`, `templates.cpp`
  and `scope.cpp` by clang 19 (`llc` 19 and 22), clang 22 and kiln++
  (`llc` 22), by default, with `-fstandalone-debug` and (clang) with
  `-gtemplate-alias`: 66 objects, 4,317 DIEs equal to `llc`'s, all
  verified. Plus a hand-written definition with its own return type.
- `hod_a64/tests/dies_scope_debugger.rs`: 72 gdb and lldb sessions under
  GNU ld, lld and mold, hod's output equal to `llc`'s.
- `hod_a64/tests/dies_scope_throughput.rs`: 157 ns per DIE at 20,000
  instances, 168 at 40,000 (ratio 1.07).
- Survey per function (functions whose DIEs are written): STL 282 → 330
  of 384, kiln++ 274 → 322 of 377, corpus 28 → 38 of 50.
  `debug:specification` 58 → 0. `debug:inlined-subroutine` 34 → 52:
  functions now reach WP-085's refusal.
- `.debug_info` is 5–10% larger than `llc`'s, from the core's
  ULEB `strx`/`udata` forms; the forms match once those are folded.
- Mutations caught: a signed template value written as `udata`
  (`--verify`), a function's imports dropped (tree), a definition's own
  return type dropped (the hand-written case).

## Left open

- `crates/hod_dwarf/src/types.rs:613` treats every enumeration as signed
  for static member constants (`static_member_of_unsigned_enumeration`,
  ignored).
- kiln emits no `DIImportedEntity`.
- `debug:template-address`: `llc` relocates against an undefined
  symbol; hod refuses.
- x86 TLS locations are refused.
