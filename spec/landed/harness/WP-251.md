# WP-251 — the full gate runs L6 and L7 on both targets

Landed: `cargo xtask ci` without `--quick` runs what `docs/VALIDATION.md`
says a release candidate needs of kiln's corpus (L6, strict) and of the
debugger layer (L7) for both target architectures, from one host, with
an explicit list of what is expected not to pass
(`spec/harness.md` "ci", "corpus" "Expected results", "debug" "In the
gate"). Follow-ups 2, 3 and 6 of `spec/landed/harness/WP-244.md`.

## The list of expected results

`docs/status/corpus-expected.tsv`, beside `corpus-x64-differences.tsv`:
the one table of the repository the corpus commands already read
(`corpus compare`). It cannot live in the corpus: entries are
`capture`'s, checked byte for byte against the manifest, and the corpus
is build output. `corpus verify` reads it by default (`--expected
<file>` names another) and applies the rows of the corpus's target
(`xtask/src/corpus/expected.rs`). Its rows at landing:

| target | kind | test | name | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| x86-64 | refused | `kiln tests/conformance/dcl.attr.cconv/cc-run.cpp` | `cc:x86_regcallcc` | refused by design (WP-141); behind it `cc:x86_vectorcallcc` |
| x86-64 | refused | `kiln tests/conformance/dcl.asm/asm-goto.cpp` | `x64:inline-asm` | a `callbr` with a template (`spec/asm.md` §11) |
| x86-64 | undefined | `gcc-torture builtins/strncmp.c` | - | uninitialised `c1 - c2` at `n == 0` (WP-244, Findings 1) |

AArch64 has no row. The two modules WP-244 found refused there
(`cc-run.cpp`, `cc:win64cc`; `exclusive-sysreg-run.cpp`,
`intrinsic:llvm.aarch64.ldxr.p0`) were run through `irdiff --strict` with
this branch's release hod, LLVM 22 and 19, four linkers: 16 PASS of 16.
On x86-64 `cc-run.cpp` now stops at `cc:x86_regcallcc` (`cc:win64cc`
landed), `asm-goto.cpp` at `x64:inline-asm`, both majors.

Semantics: a listed refusal under its name is UNSUPPORTED and listed as
EXPECTED; another name, a test hod builds (stale), or a row for a test
the corpus does not have is a list problem (exit 1); an unlisted refusal
fails as before. An undefined entry is built by both sides in strict
mode; its PASSes and its run or comparison FAILs become SKIP, listed as
UNDEFINED; a refusal or a failed build or link still fails. No wildcard,
no directory rows, one row per target and test.

## The gate

`STEPS` gains, after `roundtrip`, the full gate's own steps
(`xtask::ci::FULL_ONLY`), cheapest first: `debug-x86_64`,
`debug-aarch64` (`debug::gate(target)`: the debugger tests, then `debug
verify` of that target's corpus), `corpus-aarch64`, `corpus-x86_64`
(`corpus verify --strict -j 4` of that target's default corpus with the
list). `debug` as a step name is gone. The corpora are found as `corpus
verify` finds them (`corpus::default_dir_for`). A missing corpus skips a
corpus step with a note, or fails it under `HOD_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1`. Every
step prints its wall time; the gate ends with a table of the selected
steps, their result and wall time, the steps after a failure as `not run
(an earlier step failed)`, and under `--quick` the full gate's steps as
`not run (full gate only)`. The orchestration moved from `xtask/src/main.rs`
into `xtask::ci::run`. The quick gate's steps and `HOSTED_STEPS` are
unchanged.

`debug verify --target x86_64` without paths takes `corpus-x64` (it took
the host's corpus and skipped every module), and a run that verifies
nothing is exit status 3.
