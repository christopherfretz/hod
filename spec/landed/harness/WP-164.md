test. Rules: `spec/harness.md` "CI", rule 6.

## What changed

- **`.github/workflows/sweep.yml`**: `build` per runner (`ubuntu-24.04`,
  `ubuntu-24.04-arm`); `libcxx <arch> <backend> K/6`, 24 jobs: hod (the
  shim, strict unless dispatched hybrid, per-test logs) and `llc` (kiln++
  alone), `--timeout 60` (was 20); `summary` runs
  **`scripts/libcxx-sweep.py`** and uploads `sweep-summary`, one table.
  The summary fails when a test passes with `llc` and not with hod on the
  same runner; XFAIL, XPASS and UNSUPPORTED differences are listed only.
- **Pages**: `docs/status/libcxx-hod-x64.md` "Native x86-64 runner",
  `docs/status/libcxx-hod.md` "Native AArch64 runner";
  `docs/VALIDATION.md` "Hosted CI"; `spec/harness.md` "CI" rule 6, with
  the cost.
