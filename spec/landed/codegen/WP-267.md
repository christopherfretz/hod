# WP-267: the WAITPKG intrinsics on x86-64

Spec: `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.31 "The WAITPKG intrinsics (WP-267)";
encoder forms in `spec/mc-x64.md` "Processor state and extensions".

The real-project lane built mold for x86-64 through hod: 2 of its 93
translation units, both in its bundled oneTBB (`src/tbb/arena.cpp`,
`task_dispatcher.cpp`), were refused as `intrinsic:llvm.x86.tpause`,
called by `<waitpkgintrin.h>`'s `_tpause` (`_ZL7_tpausejy`, compiled
with `-mwaitpkg`). WP-190 had listed the three WAITPKG names as refused
by name. No module of the captured corpus calls any of the three.

## What `llc` does (`-O0`, LLVM 19 and 22)

- With `+waitpkg`: `tpause`/`umwait` are `mov edx, hi; mov eax, lo`
  (either order), the instruction on the control operand's 32-bit
  register, `setb al`; `umonitor` is the instruction on the address's
  64-bit register. Bytes, checked with `llvm-mc` 22: `tpause edi`
  `66 0F AE F7`, `tpause r8d` `66 41 0F AE F0`, `umwait r15d`
  `F2 41 0F AE F7`, `umonitor rdi` `F3 0F AE F7`, `umonitor r8`
  `F3 41 0F AE F0` (the 32-bit address form `umonitor eax` takes `67`,
  which hod never writes).
- Without it: each of the three aborts, "Cannot select" (`X86ISD::TPAUSE`,
  `X86ISD::UMWAIT`, "intrinsic %llvm.x86.umonitor"), in both versions.
  So hod refuses each by its own name, as for its WP-190 siblings.

## What was built

- `crates/hod_ir/src/intrinsic.rs`: `X86Tpause`, `X86Umwait`,
  `X86Umonitor`; `classify/matrix.rs`: their rows (`never(X64_ONLY)`,
  `on(X86_SYS)`); `docs/status/support.md` blessed (3 rows).
- `crates/hod_mc_x64/src/state.rs`: `tpause_r`, `umwait_r` (the 32-bit
  register form), `umonitor_r` (64-bit).
- `crates/hod_x64/src/x86/sys.rs`: `Feature::Waitpkg`, three rows, two
  `Op` arms (`Wait`, `Umonitor`) and three refusal names. Edited
  existing items: the `Feature` enum and `Feature::name`, the `Op`
  enum, `row`, `refusal` and `lower` (one new arm each, additive: no
  existing arm changed). A module that calls none of the three gets the
  same code as before.

## Refused, by name

- `intrinsic:llvm.x86.tpause`, `intrinsic:llvm.x86.umwait`,
  `intrinsic:llvm.x86.umonitor`: a function whose `target-features` do
  not enable `waitpkg` (none, a list without it, `+waitpkg,-waitpkg`).
- `x64:x86-intrinsic-signature`: a declaration with other types.
- On AArch64 every `llvm.x86.*` name, as before.

## Evidence

- `crates/hod_mc_x64` `tests/l1/state.rs`: the three forms on all 16
  registers against `llvm-mc` 19 and 22; `tests/l1/table.rs` rows.
- `crates/hod_x64/tests/x86_intrinsics.rs`:
  `waitpkg_sequence_is_llcs` (hod plain and under every stress option,
  `llc` 22 and 19: the same shape for each call, the deadline in `edx`
  and `eax` immediately before, the 32-bit control register, `setb`
  immediately after; `umonitor` on a 64-bit register; parameters and
  constant operands); `system_intrinsics_only_with_their_feature`
  (refused without the feature and after `-waitpkg`, built with it,
  only its own instruction); `system_rows_and_refusals_by_name` (rows);
  `system_intrinsics_agree_with_llc` (the three compiled into the
  linked execution object by hod and both `llc`s, never called).
- Mutations, each caught and reverted: `hi` and `lo` swapped
  (`waitpkg_sequence_is_llcs`: `@tp`'s `shr` result no longer reaches
  `edx`); `setae` for `setb` (the same test); `umonitor` without its
  feature check (`system_intrinsics_only_with_their_feature`).
- `crates/hod_ir/tests/body_llvm.rs` `intrinsic_names_are_llvms`: the
  three declarations round-trip through LLVM 22.
- The lane's reproducer (`1.ll`, `arena.cpp`, 1.1 MB) compiles with
  `hod --strict -O0 -relocation-model=pic`: 0.21 s, 12 MB peak. Its
  `_ZL7_tpausejy` holds `llc`'s sequence (`mov edx, hi; mov eax, lo;
  tpause ctrl; setb`); the function differs from `llc`'s around it as
  every hod function does (register choice, callee-saved pushes,
  `endbr64` and the stack protector under the function's
  `sspstrong`, §5.6.24/§5.6.25). The 14-line reduction `min.ll` has no
  `target-features`: `llc` 22 aborts on it and hod refuses it by name;
  with `"target-features"="+waitpkg"` added both compile it.
