# WP-052 — `spec/dwarf.md` and `hod_dwarf` part 1: the line table

The design is `spec/dwarf.md`, and the contract of what landed is its §3
to §5. This file records what the work package did, what it measured, and
what it left.

## What landed

- `spec/dwarf.md`: the DWARF 5 design for M3:
  - the sections, relocations, units and COMDAT handling (§1);
  - where each DIE comes from and what `hod_ir`/`FuncRecord` lack (§2);
  - the interface (§3), the line table (§4) and the line policy (§5);
  - DIEs (§6, planned), speed and memory with the budget (§7), and tests
    (§8);
  - the bridge (§9), follow-on rows (§10) and open questions (§11).
- `crates/hod_dwarf` (depends on `hod_obj` only):
  - `src/line.rs`: `Row`, `flags`, `Sequence`, `FileEntry`,
    `LineHeader`, `LineSections`, `write_debug_line` (with
    `.debug_line_str` pooling) and `FileTable` (llc's directory and file
    rules);
  - `src/policy.rs`: `SourceLoc`, `Transition`, `FunctionLines` and
    `function_rows`;
  - `src/target.rs`: `LineTarget`, `AARCH64`, `X86_64` and `for_arch`;
  - `src/leb.rs`: LEB128;
  - `Error` in `src/lib.rs`.
- Tests:
  - `tests/lines.rs` (§8.1) and `tests/llc_oracle.rs` (§8.2), with the
    module generator `tests/common/generate.rs`;
  - `tests/debugger.rs` (§8.3), with the program
    `tests/data/lines/{main,second}.{cpp,ll}` and `inc/util.h`;
  - `tests/throughput.rs` (§8.5).
- The `dwarf.md` row of `spec/README.md`.

## Evidence

- `cargo test -p hod_dwarf`: 3 lib units, 17 in `lines.rs`, 3 oracle
  tests, 2 debugger tests, 1 doctest, and 1 ignored measurement.
- §8.2 runs on LLVM 19 and 22, which give identical results. With
  `HOD_DWARF_CORPUS=.build/corpus` it covers the 2 data modules, the
  generated module with and without `-function-sections`, and the
  corpus's `-g` modules, on AArch64 and x86-64:
  - 18 objects, 164 sequences and 19,387 rows are byte-identical in
    `.debug_line`, `.rela.debug_line` and `.debug_line_str`, in both
    input orders;
  - the decoded rows are equal, and `llvm-dwarfdump --verify` from 19
    and 22 is clean on every spliced object;
  - the policy over 212 functions: 60 come out exactly as `llc` writes
    them. The other 152 are all in the generated module, and every
    difference there is the named one (1,476 rows made statements at a
    file change at the same line); no duplicates were merged. Every clang
    and kiln function matches exactly;
  - one sequence per function takes 102,211 bytes against `llc`'s
    103,031 (0.992x).
  - Without the corpus there are 8 objects, 126 sequences and 19,199
    rows. One corpus module asks for DWARF 4 and is skipped with a note
    (§11 question 6).
- §8.3, on an AArch64 host for LLVM 19 and 22 × bfd 2.44, lld and mold
  2.37.1:
  - gdb 16.3 stops 11 times over a 40-line transcript, and lldb 19
    stops 20 times over a 224-line transcript;
  - hod's tables, built from the policy's rows as one sequence per
    function in both objects, give transcripts identical to the originals;
  - the off-by-one table gives a different transcript every time.
- §8.5 was measured in release with
  `cargo test --release -p hod_dwarf --test throughput -- --ignored
  --nocapture`, on the shared host at load about 25 on 24 cores. The input
  was 20,000 functions and 1,985,902 rows:
  - the policy took 6.1 ns/row (12.2 ms);
  - the writer took 12.4 ns/row (24.6 ms, 336 MB/s), with 4.16 bytes/row
    (8.27 MB of `.debug_line`);
  - checking takes about 1.7 ms of the writer's time, and the program
    loop the rest;
  - neither a larger reservation nor a per-row stack buffer (the trick
    that halved `hod_eh`'s time) changed it, so neither was kept.

## Decisions

1. One sequence per function, always (§5.6). `llc` instead ends a
   sequence at every section switch. One per function costs 14 bytes plus
   one relocation for each function that would have shared a sequence,
   but it makes COMDAT and per-chunk emission trivial. It came out
   smaller than `llc` here (0.992x).
2. The policy reproduces `llc`'s rows with two named differences (§5.1
   and §5.2). A change of file at the same line is a statement. A row that
   repeats the position with no new flag is merged.
3. `hod_dwarf` has its own row types and no dependency on `hod_ir` or
   `hod_cg`. The bridge in `hod_cg::emit` maps `LocId` and `DiFile` to
   them (§5.7, §9).
4. DWARF 5 only. A module asking for version 4 is ignored for now (§11
   question 6).
5. `minimum_instruction_length` is 1 on both targets, as in `llc`. This
   keeps byte identity as the oracle; 4 on AArch64 is §11 question 2.
