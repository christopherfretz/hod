# WP-246 (part 1): `win64cc` on x86-64

Landed (part 1): `win64cc` (`__attribute__((ms_abi))`) definitions,
direct and indirect calls and `invoke`s on x86-64, in both directions
with the other conventions, as LLVM 19.1.7's `CC_X86_Win64_C` and
`CSR_Win64` say and `llc` 19 and 22 confirm (`spec/abi-x64.md` §13). Not
yet: AArch64 (`cc:win64cc` is `not-implemented` there), and ten
constructs refused by name (§13.7).

## What it did

- `hod_x64::win64`: the four shared positions (`rcx`/`xmm0` to
  `r9`/`xmm3`), the 32-byte shadow area, one position per piece (an
  `i128`'s halves too), returns as under `ccc` with a memory return's
  address in `rcx`, `sret` in its position, a variadic call's `double`s
  copied to the position's general register; `abi::assign_signature`
  hands it every `win64cc` signature.
- `frame`: a `win64cc` function saves `rsi`, `rdi` (pushed after `rbp`)
  and `xmm6`..`xmm15` (`movups`), with CFI rows, through the
  `preserve_*` save plan; `musttail`, coroutine bodies and variadic
  definitions are refused by name, `llvm.eh.return` as
  `x64:eh-return-convention`.
- `hod_ir::classify`: the `cc:win64cc` row is `supported` on x86-64 and
  `not-implemented` on AArch64 (was rejected on both);
  `docs/status/support.md` blessed.
- The `tests/abi` harness: a `win64cc` callee's poison leaves `rsi`,
  `rdi` and `xmm6`..`xmm15` alone; LLVM 19 is no longer skipped on
  `win64cc` cases (it agrees with 22 on all of them).

## Test evidence

- `tests/abi/win64.rs`: five table tests and `win64_grid`, 81 `win64cc`
  signatures in each of the six configurations (`llc` 19 and 22, `-O0`,
  `-O0 -fast-isel=0`, `-O2`), hod calling `llc` and `llc` calling hod,
  executed. A shadow of 40 bytes fails 552 checks.
- `tests/win64cc.rs`, every linker (bfd, lld, mold, wild):
  `win64_callees_keep_what_llc_keeps` (14 callees by hod plain, hod
  stressed, `llc` 22 and 19: kept registers after the call and through
  the unwinder during it; System V callers by hod and `llc` 22);
  `win64_calls_in_both_directions` (hod and `llc` 22 call each other's
  `win64cc` functions, directly, through pointers, `llc`'s variadic
  `v_sum`; GCC `ms_abi` calls each; equal to `llc` 19's build);
  `exceptions_through_win64_frames` (through hod's frame to an `llc`
  catcher, and in a hod landing pad); `corpus_module_runs`
  (`dcl.attr.cconv/cc-run.cpp`'s module less `x86_regcallcc` and
  `x86_vectorcallcc`, by hod and `llc`, output `pm 21 pa 3.5 ms 60 msr 44
  v 7 l 9` / `t 42`); `win64_functions_refuse_by_name`.
- Measured on the way (§13.6.4): libgcc does not install `rsi` or `rdi`
  in a landing pad reached through a `win64cc` frame, for `llc`'s frames
  as for hod's.
