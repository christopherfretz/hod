# WP-122 — x86-64 integers (design 0006 §2.2–2.5, X-int)

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/int.rs`, `crates/hod_x64/tests/int.rs` with
`crates/hod_x64/tests/common/int_gen.rs`, the x86_64 column of the
integer rows of `crates/hod_ir/src/classify/matrix.rs` (and
`docs/status/support.md`), `spec/codegen.md` §5.6 "Integers (WP-122)",
and the seed `tests/ir/exit_odd_widths.ll`.

## What is lowered

Every `Gpr` integer at i1 to i64, and pointers where the row says so:

| form | lowering |
|---|---|
| add, sub, mul, and, or, xor | two-operand, into the left operand's register; a constant that fits the sign-extended imm32 is an immediate, else through r11; identities emit nothing; xor of all ones is `not` |
| udiv, sdiv, urem, srem | dividend in rax (extended by zero or sign), `mov edx, 0` + `div` or `cdq`/`cqo` + `idiv`; the result from rax or rdx |
| shl, lshr, ashr | a constant amount as an immediate (odd widths: `shl` then `shr`/`sar`); a register amount through cl, masked with `and ecx` below i5 |
| trunc, zext, sext, ptrtoint, ptrtoaddr, inttoptr, bitcast | copies, or `extend_to` (movzx/movsx, movsxd, `mov r32, r32`, `and`, a shift pair) |
| icmp | deferred for fusion when it may; else `cmp` + `setcc` + `movzx` |
| select | `cmov` at 32 bits for every width up to 32 (no 8-bit `cmov`; never a branch); the condition fused or `test r8, 1` |
| freeze | a copy |

`zext`/`sext` of a fused compare are `setcc` + `movzx` (+ `neg`).

Refused by name: `x64:int-binary` (an opcode the binary path does not
know), `x64:int-cast` (a cast outside the table), `x64:addrspacecast`.

Helpers for later groups (`pub`): `Ext`, `size_for_bits`, `extend_to`,
`cond_of`, `fusable`, `flags` (the last two are the fusion contract with
WP-124's `condbr`).

## Evidence

Four legs per suite (hod, hod under `Stress::ALL`, `llc` 22, `llc` 19 at
`-O0 -fast-isel=0`), each compared with `hod_interp`, run under qemu with
one process per object:

| suite | functions | cases compared | text hod/llc |
|---|---|---|---|
| every binary operation at every width | 5,369 | 65,044 | 2.50 |
| every compare and select | 7,169 | 238,947 | 2.63 |
| every cast (no `llc` 19: `ptrtoaddr`) | 461 | 9,498 | 2.95 |
| random integer functions | 1,500 | 5,626 | 3.61 |

Mutations, each caught: `sdiv`/`srem` dividend extended by zero (10,398
disagreements); no `shl` before an odd-width register compare (52,038);
no `and ecx` mask below i5 (132).

`exit_odd_widths.ll` passes on x86_64 and aarch64 with LLVM 22 and 19,
with bfd, lld and mold, and the witness. The rest of the seed corpus is
still declined on x86-64 (globals, `global:variable-definition`).

## Differences from `llc`

`llc` omits the frame of a leaf; hod does not. hod never uses `lea` for
`add`, and `select` is always `cmov`. The text is 2.5 to 3.6 times
`llc`'s.
