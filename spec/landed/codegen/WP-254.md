# WP-254 — `asm goto` with a template on x86-64 (WP-212 part 2)

Landed: a `callbr` whose inline-assembly template is not empty, on
x86-64 (`crates/hod_x64/src/barrier/template.rs`: `Place::Label`,
`LABEL_PREFIX`, `goto_edges`, `outputs`; `crates/hod_x64/src/barrier.rs`
hands it to the template lowering). Spec: `spec/asm.md` §21 and §14's
table, `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.23. Tests: `crates/hod_x64/tests/asm_goto.rs`
(data in `tests/data/asm_goto/`), `crates/hod_x64/tests/callbr.rs`
(`callbr_of_instructions_is_refused_by_name`),
`crates/hod_x64/tests/inline_asm.rs` (`a_callbr_with_a_template_is_built`).

## Design

- A label operand prints as `.Lhod_asm_goto_<n>` for every modifier:
  `llc` 19 and 22 print the block's label bare for `l`, `P`, `c`, none
  and any other letter.
- Each branch of the template to a label (a `jmp`/`jcc` field the
  assembler relocates as `R_X86_64_PLT32` of the label alone) is a
  fixup to a trampoline of that destination: the outputs are stored
  again from the registers the template left them in, the result is
  defined again, that edge's `phi`s are written and the trampoline
  branches to the block. A block is always entered with nothing in a
  register; at the template's branch the outputs still are.
- The fallthrough is the template call's ordinary end (outputs, result,
  `phi`s) and a branch to the default destination. A destination no
  branch of the template names gets no trampoline.
- The callee-saved registers the template writes are saved by the
  prologue (as for any template), so a trampoline reloads none (on
  AArch64 it does, `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.3).
- The sketch of `c65a7219` (untested) had this design; kept, with the
  refusal names it lacked, x87 refused, trampolines only for named
  destinations, and the result let go on the fallthrough too.

## Refused

`x64:inline-asm-goto`: a label referred to by anything but a branch to
it (`leaq L(%rip)`, `jmp L+2`), and x87 operands or clobbers in an `asm
goto` (the register stack is not modelled on its edges); `llc` 19 and 22
build all of them. A `.quad` of a label is the assembler's
`asm:operand/symbol`, as for any symbol in a template. `x64:inline-asm`
is no longer produced.

## Evidence

- `asm_goto.rs`: 9 functions (one label, three labels, a label that is
  also the fallthrough, `.cfi_*` and a label, a frame with every
  callee-saved register written, an output, an output taken by a `phi`,
  two outputs, a label back to a loop head) x 26 arguments, taking
  every path; hod plain, stressed and far, `llc` 22 and 19 at `-O0` and
  at `-O0 -fast-isel=0 -global-isel=0`, each linked with a C driver
  under bfd, lld, mold (and wild where present) and run under qemu:
  28 runs agree. `llc` 19 is wrong on one edge per selection (its
  column is left out of that build's comparison): at `-O0` it reloads
  `goto_phi`'s output on the indirect edge before spilling it; at
  `-fast-isel=0` it loses `goto_out2`'s first output there (AArch64's
  seed `goto_out2` shows the same). hod agrees with `llc` 22 on both.
- The corpus module `dcl.asm/asm-goto.cpp` (LLVM 22 and 19 forms):
  hod's three builds and `llc`'s four print `0 1 7 8 9` under every
  linker, 56 runs.
- Template references: `callbr.rs` checks that `llc` 19 and 22 build
  each refused shape.
