# WP-205 — the AArch64 intrinsic headers: census, built and refused

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/neon.rs` (SM3/SM4, the scalar `frint32/64`,
`fjcvtzs`, the half precision scalar conversions and min/max, `bfloat`
vectors in the structure loads and stores, constant splats with `poison`
lanes), `hod_ir`'s 14 new `Intrinsic` members (after
`Aarch64SisdFcvtxn`), their matrix rows (`on(NEON)`, `never(A64_ONLY)`)
and signatures in `hod_ir/tests/body_llvm.rs`, `docs/status/support.md`
(generated), the encoders `sm3reg`, `sm3ss1`, `sm3tt`, `sm4e`,
`fp_half`, `fjcvtzs` in `crates/hod_mc_a64` (L1 rows against llvm-mc 19
and 22), `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.34. Census:
`crates/hod_a64/tests/data/neon_census/` (`census.py`, `table.py`,
`census.tsv`). Tests: `crates/hod_a64/tests/neon_census.rs`.

## Census

Every `__ai` function of `<arm_neon.h>`, the functions of `<arm_fp16.h>`
and `<arm_acle.h>`, and every function-like macro called with constants,
one wrapper each, compiled by clang 19 and 22 at `-O0` for
`aarch64-linux-gnu` with the features clang names; each function's
module compiled alone by `hod --strict`. 4883 header functions, 4753
compiled by at least one clang (130 by neither: AArch32 DSP and
coprocessor functions, `__arm_rsr`/`__arm_wsr`, MTE macros, `vcmla*_f64`).

Per function, built under every clang that compiles it: 4516 before,
4624 after, 129 refused (by group: §5.6.34). By clang: 19 built 4320
of 4440 compiled before; 22 built 4510 of 4744 before. Before, every
baseline `+neon`, `+crc`, `+aes`, `+sha2`, `+sha3`, `+dotprod`, `+rdm`
and `+i8mm` function was already built except `vqshlub_n_s8` and
`vqshluh_n_s16` (a shift constant with `poison` lanes).

The real-project lanes' saved modules (`.build/findings/oss`, `oss2`)
held no AArch64 `intrinsic:llvm.aarch64.*` refusal, so the order was the
header groups.

## kiln

235 wrappers from the census (every group), compiled one per file by
kiln++ at `-O0`: 158 compile; 62 stop at kiln's "code generation for
`__builtin_neon_*` is not supported yet" (`vld2/3/4_dup`, `vld*_lane`,
`vst3/4_lane`, `vqshlu_n`, `vrsraq_n`, `vfmad_lane_f64`, `vdivh_f16`,
`vbcaxq`, `vfmlal_high`, ...); the rest use names kiln's header (clang
19's) lacks (fp8, `vamin`, `vluti`). Of the 158, 155 give clang 22's
names; 3 give `neon.uminv`/`saddv`/`smaxv` where clang 22 gives
`vector.reduce.*` (both built). hod builds 154 of the 158; one of the
4 is `__arm_ld64b` (refused by name).

## Evidence

- `neon_census.rs`: 158 cases (127 native, 10 `i16` of half against
  `llc` 22 alone, 21 SM3/SM4 under `qemu-aarch64 -cpu max`), 64 rounds
  each from per-type edge pools (±0, ±1, halves, ±2^31, ±2^31±1, ±2^63,
  ±inf, qNaN, sNaN, denormals, the largest finite values, integer
  saturation bounds; every fourth round random). Compiled by `llc -O0
  -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` 19 and 22 and by hod plain, `Stress::ALL`
  and far, linked with bfd, lld and mold: 8128 + 640 + 1344 rounds agree
  in every build. Two out-of-range immediates refused by name.
- Mutations, each caught and reverted: the `sm3tt` lane `index ^ 1`
  (1024 rounds differ), the half conversions without `fp_half` (714),
  the half fixed point with the `S` element (1407).
- Byte identity, every 10th corpus entry (669 modules), base (031a3d5)
  against head, `-j 4`: 669 objects identical.
- `cargo test -q` of `hod_a64`, `hod_mc_a64`, `hod_ir`, `hod_x64`:
  green.
