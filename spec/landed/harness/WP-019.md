Landed: `spec/harness.md` "roundtrip", layer L2 of `docs/VALIDATION.md`.
Not here: the command in `cargo xtask ci` (see "Cost of a quick tier").

## Failure classes

None. No entry was `parser-error`, a crash of the parser or printer,
`printer-not-expressible`, `printer-error`, `llvm-as-refused`,
`verifier-refused`, `llc-failed`, `link-failed`, `behaviour-differs`, a
tool crash or timeout, or `harness-error`: every module `hod_ll` accepts
prints as text LLVM 19 and 22 accept, verify, and build into programs
that behave as recorded, with each linker, through SelectionDAG at -O0.
So there is no reduced example to record. The outcomes that would have
been found are each provoked in `xtask/tests/roundtrip_fakes.rs`, and a
behaviour difference with the real tools in `xtask/tests/roundtrip_real.rs`.

## Evidence

- `cargo test -p xtask --lib roundtrip`: 7 unit tests.
- `cargo test -p xtask --test roundtrip_fakes`: 5 tests, every outcome
  of a tool step, the gate in both directions, selection, missing tools,
  `--keep`.
- `cargo test -p xtask --test roundtrip_real`: LLVM 19 and 22, every
  linker found.
- Filtered runs while building: `--limit 10` (20 entries, 2.4 s at
  `-j 3`); `--filter kiln/ --filter /ieee/ --limit 120` (240 entries,
  20.4 s at `-j 4`); all pass.

## Cost of a quick tier

The full corpus is 550 s at `-j 4` on the shared host (about 0.1 s of
wall time per entry and LLVM major at `-j 4`, three linkers): about 9
minutes, merge-gate material. A quick tier of 100 entries per major, all
three linkers, is about 19 s at `-j 4` (measured: 240 entries in 20.4 s);
with one linker about a third of the link-and-run part less. The parse
and print in the harness's process are a negligible part (the corpus is
parsed in seconds in a release build, a few times that in xtask's debug
build).

## Deviations and notes

- The reference `llc` configuration is applied by a wrapper script around
  `llc` (`stages::reference_llc_script`), because `irdiff`'s engine gives
  `llc` plain `-O0` until WP-023 lands. The wrapper adds
  `-global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` only when neither flag is in the
  arguments, so it stays correct once the engine passes them itself
  (`llc` refuses an option given twice); it can be removed then.
- The engine's outcome is read from the text of its `ERROR` details
  (`oracle: llc ...`, `oracle: link with ...`, `oracle: the program of
  llc's object ...`). An unknown shape is a `harness-error`, never a pass;
  `roundtrip_fakes.rs` breaks if WP-023 changes those words.
- Steps 1 and 2 run in worker threads of the harness without a jobserver
  token of their own (the children do hold theirs); they are cheap next
  to the children.
- Outcomes beyond the brief's list: `parser-crashed`, `printer-crashed`,
  `printer-error` (`PrintError::Malformed`), `tool-crashed`,
  `tool-timed-out`, `harness-error`, and `skipped` (not compared).
