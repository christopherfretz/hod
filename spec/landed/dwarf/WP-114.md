# WP-114: DWARF version 4

The contract is `spec/dwarf.md` §6.20. This file records what the work
package did, what it measured and what it left.

## What landed

- **`crates/hod_dwarf/src/dwarf4.rs`** (new): `UnitPlan::version`
  (4 when the module flag `"Dwarf Version"` is 4, else 5), and a
  transcoder. Each version 4 unit is written as DWARF 5 by the unchanged
  `Writer::unit` into scratch buffers. It is then rewritten slot by slot
  from the abbreviation table (§6.1), and a version 4 `.debug_abbrev` is
  written for the codes used.
  - Strings become `strp`, and addresses `DW_FORM_addr`/`DW_OP_addr`
    with their relocations.
  - Range lists go to `.debug_ranges`; `decl_file`/`call_file` become
    index + 1.
  - The three base attributes and `DW_AT_deleted` are dropped.
  - Static data members become `DW_TAG_member`.
  - The lower bound follows the language's version 4 default.
- **The version 4 line table** (`line.rs`: `LineProgram::for_version`
  and `file_base`; `dwarf4::write_line_header`):
  - inline `include_directories` and `file_names`;
  - files numbered from 1;
  - no `.debug_line_str` when every unit is version 4.
- **Hooks**, one call each:
  - `die.rs`: the plan's `version`, and `check_dwarf5` at the version 5
    writer's entry;
  - `split.rs` `write_unit_dies`, and `check` (split DWARF 4);
  - `types.rs`: the subrange's default lower bound;
  - `hod_cg/src/emit/lines.rs`: `UnitPlan::line_program`.
- **`classify`** (`hod_ir/src/classify/walk.rs`, `matrix.rs`) lets
  version 4 through, and `docs/status/support.md` is regenerated.
- **The `trees` harness** (`xtask/src/debug/trees.rs`) recognises
  `llc`'s version 4 `sret` lists, which have no `loclists_base` and are
  relocated against `.debug_loc`.

## Refused, by name

| Name | What it refuses |
|---|---|
| `debug:dwarf-version` (`classify`) | versions 2 and 3, and no flag |
| `debug:split-dwarf-4` (`hod::back` and `hod_dwarf::split`) | `-gsplit-dwarf -gdwarf-4` |
| `debug:dwarf-4-shape` (`hod_dwarf::DWARF_4_SHAPE`) | a family appended after `SPLIT_COMPILE_UNIT` (WP-113's call sites), or an expression with a branch whose length changes |

## Evidence

**Trees against `llc` 19 and 22** (`-O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`):
the nineteen `tests/debug` sources, for each front end and target, with
`-gdwarf-4` and with `-gdwarf-5`.

| Front end | Target | Same | Permitted | Differ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| clang 19 | x86-64 | 16 (1 305 DIEs) | 3 (`sret`, `byval`, `realigned frame`) | 0 |
| clang 22 | x86-64 | 16 (1 309 DIEs) | 3 | 0 |
| kiln++ | x86-64 | 16 (395 DIEs) | 3 | 0 |
| clang 19 | AArch64 | 6 | 0 | 13 |
| clang 22 | AArch64 | 6 | 0 | 13 |
| kiln++ | AArch64 | 6 | 0 | 13 |

- On AArch64, version 5 gives exactly the same counts. Every AArch64
  difference is a location: `llc`'s `DW_OP_breg31` against hod's
  `DW_OP_fbreg`, or a `sret` list.
- Each module's outcome is its version 5 outcome; only the depth of the
  first reported difference moves for the `sret` modules.
- `llvm-dwarfdump --verify` is clean on every object, with dwarfdump 19
  and 22.

**What the trees do not show**: the forms (constant widths folded,
§6.7), the `.debug_*` section set, the line header and the sections
`.debug_ranges` names. All equal `llc`'s on every module, apart from
the three modules with an `sret` list.

**The shapes test** (`tests/debug/dwarf4_shapes.cpp`), on both targets
and all three front ends:

- 5 static members as `DW_TAG_member`;
- no `DW_AT_deleted` (version 5 writes 2);
- a hand-made `lowerBound` 0 written as `llc` writes it (2 bounds in
  version 4, 1 in version 5);
- `.debug_ranges`: absolute entries for the several sections of
  COMDATs.

**Debugger sessions** (gdb, lldb-19; GNU ld, lld, mold):

- AArch64, native, clang 19, clang 22 and kiln++: the nine twins and the
  shapes test give 174 results, all PASS.
  - The `(function, line)` sets: 459 equal, plus 2 of `llc` alone in
    class P1.
- x86-64 through qemu: 174 results, 168 PASS and 6 SKIP. The skips are
  TLS under qemu's gdb stub.

**Corpus**: the two `-g` modules that stopped at `debug:dwarf-version`
(kiln's `define-debuginfo-run.cpp`, llvm19 and llvm22) now build with
`--strict` and verify. Their only tree difference is AArch64's
`breg31` location; there is no next refusal.

**DWARF 5 is byte-identical**, checked with the main binary (f011d9b)
against this branch:

- 255 of the 257 `-g` modules are identical:
  - the corpus's 14 `-g` modules;
  - the census's 123;
  - the tests' 120 version 5 sources (19 sources by six front-end and
    target pairs, and the shapes test by six).
- The other two are the version 4 modules above, which main refuses.

**Throughput** (release; `dies_throughput`'s generator, emission only,

## Mutations (caught, reverted)

1. `decl_file`/`call_file` not shifted by 1. Caught by
   `dwarf4_lower_bounds_are_llcs` (the tree) and by
   `a_version_4_module_is_written_as_version_4_on_both_targets`.
2. Every absolute range written empty (end = start). Caught by
   `dwarf4_forms_sections_and_line_headers_are_llcs`, where a function
   is in no range.
3. The version 4 lower bound disabled. Caught by
   `dwarf4_lower_bounds_are_llcs` and by
   `dwarf4::tests::lower_bounds_of_version_4`.

## What is left

- **WP-113's call sites in version 4.** `llc` writes them as
  `DW_TAG_GNU_call_site` and `DW_AT_GNU_*`. They are refused as
  `debug:dwarf-4-shape` until their families are mapped in `tag4`,
  `form4` and `keeps` and checked (`crates/hod_dwarf/src/dwarf4.rs`,
  `LAST_CHECKED`).
- **Split DWARF 4** (GNU fission: `DW_AT_GNU_dwo_name`,
  `DW_FORM_GNU_addr_index`).
- **No flag.** `llc` writes version 4 for it; hod still refuses it.
- **Optimized code.** The `GNU_entry_value` of version 4 is not needed
  at `-O0`.
- **`docs/status/debug.md`** has not been regenerated.
