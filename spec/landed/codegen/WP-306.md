# WP-306 — i386: the rest of the integers of 33 to 64 bits

Landed: in `crates/hod_x86/src/int.rs`, the integer intrinsics of
`i33`..`i64` (`bswap`, `ctpop`, `ctlz`, `cttz`, `bitreverse`, `abs`,
`smax`/`smin`/`umax`/`umin`, `sadd.sat`/`uadd.sat`/`ssub.sat`/`usub.sat`,
`sshl.sat`/`ushl.sat`, `fshl`/`fshr`), `switch` on one, a `ptradd` index
of one. Behaviour: `spec/codegen-x86.md` §8. Tests:
`crates/hod/tests/i386_i64_rest.rs`.

Edits outside `int.rs`, each a dispatch to it:
`call.rs` (`lower`: an arm for an integer intrinsic with a wide result),
`control.rs` (`switch`: a wide condition goes to `int::switch`),
`mem.rs` (`ptr_add`: `int::index_ok` in the class check,
`int::put_index` in place of `FnCx::put`). `target.rs`: none.

## Decisions

- Everything in `eax`, `ecx`, `edx`, as the rest of `int`; where a
  lowering needs more than three words at once (`sshl.sat`, `ushl.sat`,
  the funnel shifts) a frame slot of its own (`FnCx::alloc_slot`), not a
  pool register, so `Stress::ALL` changes nothing.
- Odd widths are normalized rather than special-cased: a reversal or a
  funnel/saturating shift moves the value to the top of 64 bits (`<< (64
  - N)`, which drops the unspecified bits), works at 64 bits, and moves
  the result back; counts and compares extend the high word (§8).
- The variable funnel amount `c mod N` (N not 64) is two 32-bit `div`s
  by `N` (high word, then low word with the remainder): exact, inline.
  `llc` calls `__umoddi3` for N = 33 and 57 (inline magic for 48); no
  `RuntimeFn` entry was added.
- `is_zero_poison` / `is_int_min_poison` are ignored: the defined result
  is computed.
- The `*.with.overflow` intrinsics return `{iN, i1}`: their results are
  read with `extractvalue`, refused as `x86:aggregate` for every width
  (the `{i32, i1}` forms too); I11's. The call itself stays
  `x86:intrinsic`.
- `switch` compares each case's two halves (`cmp`/`jne`/`cmp`/`je`), no
  table; a constant condition is a branch.

## Branches of `llc` (for `x87::barrier`)

Measured with `gen.py --probe <dir>` and `branches.py` (both in
`crates/hod/tests/data/i386/i64_rest/`) on `llc` 19 and 22, `-O0
-global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`, at N = 33, 48, 57, 64:

- CPU `generic` (no `cmov`): `ctlz`, `cttz` (both `is_zero_poison`),
  `smax`, `smin`, `umax`, `umin`, `sadd.sat`, `uadd.sat`, `ssub.sat`,
  `usub.sat`, `sshl.sat`, `ushl.sat`, `fshl`/`fshr` by a variable amount
  all split the block (select expansion).
- CPU `i686`, `pentium4` (`cmov`): none of them.
- Never: `bswap`, `ctpop`, `bitreverse`, `abs` (both flags), `fshl`/`fshr`
  by a constant.

## Evidence

`i386_i64_rest.rs`: five programs (`unary.ll`, `binary.ll`, `funnel.ll`
written by `gen.py`; `switch.ll`, `ptradd.ll` by hand) × LLVM 19, 22 ×
CPU generic, pentium4 × position-dependent, PIE = 40 modules; 120 runs
(llc's, hod's, hod's under `Stress::ALL`), linked by the cross gcc's
default linker, outputs (about 23,600 lines of hex per set) equal byte
for byte. Operands: 0, 1, -1, 2^31, 2^31 - 1, 2^32 - 1, 2^32, 2^63 - 1,
-2^63, four fixed patterns and the width's own bounds, pairwise; funnel
amounts 0, 1, 31..33, 63..65, N - 1..N + 1, 2N - 1, 2N + 5 and patterns
with high halves set; 14 constant amounts. Refusals: `i128` (and `i96`)
operands of every new construct give `x86:i64:wide`; `i65` intrinsics are
refused earlier by the shared classifier (`intrinsic:<name>/int-wide`).

`cargo xtask irdiff --target i686 --tier quick --linker bfd` over
`tests/ir`, base `08a9503c` and head: 25 tests, 3 PASS, 17 UNSUPPORTED,
5 ERROR (oracle side) at both; declined `x86:i64` 6 → 5,
`x86:indirect-branch` 0 → 1 (`exit_control.ll`: its `i64` `switch`
compiles, an `indirectbr` is next); the rest equal.

`cargo xtask compare-binaries` over the AArch64 and x86-64 kiln corpora:
13438 comparisons, 13434 identical, 0 different, 0 changed outcome, 4
identical failures.

## Remaining

- `i64` arguments and results (I6), `*.with.overflow` (I11's
  `extractvalue`), `i128` (`x86:i64:wide`).
- `scmp`/`ucmp` of wide operands were not asked for and stay
  `x86:intrinsic`; the `i32` forms of every integer intrinsic also stay
  `x86:intrinsic` (no package owns them yet).
