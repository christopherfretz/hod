# WP-133 — x86-64 atomics (X-atomic)

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/atomic.rs`; `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.10; the
support-matrix rows below; `tests/atomic.rs` with
`tests/common/atomic_wide.rs` and `tests/common/atomic_threads.rs`.

## What is lowered

- atomic `load` and `store` of 1, 2, 4, 8 bytes (integers, pointers,
  `half`, `bfloat`, `float`, `double`) under every ordering; a
  `seq_cst` store is `xchg`, as `llc` writes it;
- `atomicrmw` of every integer operation (`xchg`, `add`, `sub`, `and`,
  `nand`, `or`, `xor`, `max`, `min`, `umax`, `umin`, `uinc_wrap`,
  `udec_wrap`, `usub_cond`, `usub_sat`) at 1..8 bytes and pointers:
  `xchg`, `lock xadd`, `lock <op>` when the result is unused, else a
  `lock cmpxchg` loop; `xchg` of every floating type; `fadd`/`fsub` of
  `float` and `double` (a `lock cmpxchg` loop through `xmm15`);
- `cmpxchg` strong and weak (weak is strong), both result fields, every
  ordering pair;
- `fence`: `mfence` for a system-scope `seq_cst`, nothing otherwise;
- 16 bytes with `+cx16` in the function's `target-features`: every
  operation above as `lock cmpxchg16b` (`rbx` taken for it).

## Refused, by name

`x64:atomic-unaligned` (alignment below the size), `x64:atomic-type`
(`fp128`, `x86_fp80`, vectors, odd integer widths, a mismatched
operation), `x64:atomic-bfloat-op` (`bfloat` arithmetic),
`x64:atomic-float-op` (`fmax`, `fmin`, `fmaximum`, `fminimum`; `half`
`fadd`/`fsub`: WP-127's arithmetic), `x64:atomic-libatomic` (16 bytes
without `+cx16`: `hod_cg::RuntimeFn` has no `__atomic_*_16`).

## Evidence

- **Table** (`every_atomic_operation_agrees`, `common/aggr_gen.rs`): 646
  functions, 4,858 cases, 4,691 against `hod_interp`, 148 against `llc`
  only (`half`/`bfloat` the interpreter does not model), 19 undefined;
  legs hod plain and `Stress::ALL`, `llc` 22 and 19, both mixed
  objects; under `qemu-x86_64`. No disagreement. Text hod 153,330 B,
  `llc` 22 50,208 B (3.05×); 11,123 IR instructions in 40 ms.
- **16 bytes** (`atomic_wide.rs`): 182 functions, 4,230 cases (also
  `usub_cond`/`usub_sat` at 1..8 bytes, which LLVM 19 does not read)
  against the interpreter, on a runner whose CPU model adds `cx16`;
  legs hod plain and stress, `llc` 22, `llc` 19 (its object for what it
  reads, `llc` 22's for the rest). No disagreement.
- **Threads** (`atomic_threads.rs`): 93 counters (every operation and
  width, `i128` included, `cmpxchg` increment loops strong and weak) and
  8 structures (spinlocks on strong, weak, 16-byte `cmpxchg` and on
  exchange; a Treiber stack; 16-byte tearing; message passing through
  release/acquire and through fences), 4 threads × 2,000 operations,
  hod plain and stress and `llc` 22, linked by bfd, lld and mold, 3 runs
  each: 27 runs, all exact against a gcc reference under a mutex.
- **Forms**: `xchg` for a `seq_cst` store, `mov` otherwise, `lock xadd`,
  `lock or` for a dead result, the `cmpxchg` loop, `sete`, `mfence` only
  for a system `seq_cst` fence, `lock cmpxchg16b` with `+cx16`.
- **Seed**: `tests/ir/atomics.ll` without its `@fp` (doubles) passes
  `irdiff --target x86_64` on bfd, lld, mold; the seed itself keeps
  `;@ unsupported(x86_64): type:double` until WP-127.

Mutations (each reverted):

- `lock` dropped from `xadd`: the single-threaded table passes; the
  threaded counters fail (`sub i32 acquire` lost updates).
- `cmpxchg` success flag inverted (`setne`): 2,772 table disagreements
  and the threaded programs fail.
- `xchg` → `mov` for a `seq_cst` store: the table and the threaded
  programs pass (emulated TSO cannot show it); the forms test fails.

## What the emulator proves

qemu user mode runs guest threads on host threads, so atomicity under
real contention is shown. It adds host barriers for the orderings TSO
guarantees and maps `lock`ed instructions to host atomics, so the
message-passing results do not depend on hod's ordering choices: TSO
ordering is pinned by the forms test, not proven by execution.

## Follow-ups
