# WP-314: i386 exceptions and debug information (design 0008 I10)

Part 1 (exceptions) is complete (sessions 1 and 2). Part 2 (debug
information, `x86:debug-info` stays refused) is measured and designed,
not implemented: `PARKED.md` at the branch root has the measurements
and the edit plan for a third session.

## What changed

- `hod_eh`: `CfiTarget.arch` became `machine: Machine`; new fields
  `address_size` and `abs32_reloc`; the `I386` table and
  `for_machine(machine, large)` (`spec/eh.md` §2.1). The `DW.ref` slot,
  the type-info stubs and `.debug_frame`'s locations and ranges are
  `address_size` bytes. The 64-bit tables keep 8 and the relocations
  they had, so their output is unchanged (compare-binaries below).
- `hod_cg/src/emit/unwind.rs`: the early return for `EM_386` removed;
  the FDE table chosen by machine.
- `hod_cg/src/emit/mod.rs` (structors, authorized by the supervisor): an
  `.init_array`/`.fini_array` entry is `arch.address_bytes()` long. It
  was 8 on every machine, so on i386 each `R_386_32` entry was followed
  by a zero word and the program called address 0 at startup
  (`tests/ir/global_ctors.ll`; the `x86:eh` refusal had hidden it). 8 on
  AArch64 and x86-64: unchanged.
- `hod_x86/src/eh.rs` (new), `call.rs` (the `x86:eh` refusal of
  `invoke` and of calls that may unwind removed; `llvm.eh.typeid.for`
  arm), `target.rs` (landing pad, resume, the pad value's
  `extractvalue`/`insertvalue`). `spec/codegen-x86.md` §12.

## Decisions

- PIC encodings in every model, as on x86-64 (`llc`'s static ones are
  absolute and make a PIE text-relocated; measured with `llc` 19 and
  22). A documented deviation (supervisor's decision): the static
  objects' tables differ from `llc`'s by design; they are tested by
  linking and running `-no-pie` programs with GNU ld, lld and mold and by
  decoding the LSDAs (`lsdas_decode_as_llc_s`, static and PIC).
- The landing pad value is class `Mem` and its two fields are handled in
  `eh.rs`; other aggregates stay `x86:aggregate` (I11a's).
- The decoded `.eh_frame` comparison with `llc` covers the CIEs, FDE and
  LSDA counts, the prologue rules per FDE without `ebx`/`esi`/`edi`
  (hod saves all three always), and the relocation kinds; after the
  first exit `llc` writes one exit per `ret` and hod one per function,
  and a module without `"frame-pointer"="all"` has an `esp`-based CFA in
  `llc`, so those parts are not compared.
- The LSDA comparison decodes call sites, actions, catch types (through
  the relocations and the stubs), cleanups and filters; it compares
  whether a call site has a pad, not which: where several `invoke`s
  unwind to one IR pad `llc` has one landing address and hod one per
  `invoke` (measured on `basic.cpp`).
- `x86_stdcallcc`/`x86_fastcallcc` stay refused (`cc:<name>`, the
  shared classifier's) for `invoke` as for `call`; the callee pop hod
  has on i386 is the `sret` pointer, tested by `eh/callconv.ll`.
- x87 (`spec/codegen-x86.md` §11, i386-group-2 rule): an `invoke` is a
  terminator, hence a barrier; no new barrier. Measured in `llc -O0
  -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` assembly, LLVM 19 and 22, CPU `generic`
  and `pentiumpro`: a product computed before an `invoke` is stored
  rounded (`fstpl`/`fstps`) before the call and reloaded in both
  successors; one computed and used inside the pad stays in a register.
  `eh/callconv.ll` checks both shapes on `llc`'s run (1e300 * 1e300 / 1e300
  infinite after rounding, finite at 80 bits) and hod's runs equal it.
