# WP-273: `!exclude` on a global

Spec: `spec/codegen.md` §9.7.4 "Excluded variables"; the readers:
`spec/ir.md` §2.2 (the attachment and `Module::exclude`), `spec/ll.md`
§3.3 and §3.4, `spec/bc.md` §5.8 "Attachments" and §11.7.

Requested by the mortar linker project (`spec/odr.md` §11 of
github.com/christopherfretz/mortar, items H-1 and H-2): kiln's records
are a private constant in an excluded section that the linker drops.
Before this package both readers noted `!exclude` as
`global-metadata:exclude` and hod refused the module.

## What `llc` does (measured)

`llc` 19 and 22, `-O0` (AArch64 also with `-global-isel=0
-fast-isel=0`), x86-64 and AArch64, `readelf -SW`, `-sW`, `-x`; the two
majors and the two targets agree in every case except where noted.

That is LLVM's `getKindForGlobal`: after thread-local, common and BSS
(a variable with a section is never BSS), a variable with a section
whose `!exclude` node has no operands is `SectionKind::Exclude`; then
`getELFKindForNamedSection` may override the kind by name, the type is
the name's (`getELFSectionType`), and the flags of `Exclude` are
`SHF_EXCLUDE` without `SHF_ALLOC`.

## The rule

- Readers: a variable's first `!exclude` whose node is a tuple without
  operands (`!{}`, `distinct !{}`, in place or numbered) marks it with
  `Module::exclude(sym)` (`Symbol::is_excluded()`); a tuple with
  operands is dropped; `!exclude` on a function is dropped.
- Codegen (`emit/mod.rs` `place_variable`, `emit/section.rs`
  `Kind::Exclude`): an excluded variable with a section, not
  thread-local, not common, gets `Kind::Exclude`, flags exactly
  `SHF_EXCLUDE`, before the name refines the kind and type as for any
  explicit section. Everything else of explicit sections (groups,
  `SHF_GNU_RETAIN`, large names, one section per name, flags, entry size
  and group, relocations) is unchanged.
- The printer writes `, !exclude !{}` after the alignment.

## Refused

- `global-metadata:exclude`: a variable's first `!exclude` whose node is
  not a tuple (`!DIExpression()`, which `llvm-as` takes), and in text a
  tuple with a comment between its braces (hod does not tell its
  operands apart then). Neither is a shape a producer writes; each is
  refused by name rather than guessed.
- A non-zero initializer in an excluded variable whose section name is a
  BSS one keeps the existing `global:initializer-in-bss` refusal.

## Tests

- `crates/hod_cg/tests/emit_llc.rs` snippet `exclude`: the requester's
  shape and every case of the table that a module can hold (no section,
  zero initializer, relocation, thread-local, COMDAT, shared and mixed
  names, operands, `distinct`, two attachments, `.bss.x`,
  `.init_array.7`, `.note.x`, `llvm.used`, a declaration, a function)
  agree with `llc` 19 and 22 on both targets (sections with bytes,
  flags, groups, symbols, relocations).
- `crates/hod/tests/exclude.rs`: through the session, from text and from
  the bitcode of `llvm-as` 19 and 22, both targets: `.mason.meta` is
  `SHT_PROGBITS`, flags exactly `SHF_EXCLUDE`, align 8, in no group, no
  relocation section names it, and its 16 bytes are the initializer's
  (0x00, 0xff, `C3 28`, `80`, `FE FF`, `E2 82 00`: not UTF-8); linked
  with `-pie` by bfd, lld and mold (and wild) on both targets, the
  program runs and has no `.mason.meta`; a non-tuple node is refused as
  `global-metadata:exclude`.
- `crates/hod_bc/tests/exclude.rs`: `hod_ll` and `hod_bc` (LLVM 19 and
  22 bitcode) mark the same variables (with a section, without one,
  `distinct`, a declaration), drop operands, the second attachment and
  a function's (definition and declaration), and leave no note; a
  non-tuple node gives `global-metadata:exclude` in both.
- `crates/hod_ll/tests/syntax/rows.rs`: four rows (empty, operands,
  non-tuple, on a function) checked against `llvm-as` 19 and 22.
- `crates/hod_ir`: `symbol.rs` `excluded_bit`, `tests/kit.rs`.

## Example

`crates/hod_cg/tests/data/exclude/mason_meta.ll` (no target triple; the
command line gives it):

```
hod -O0 -filetype=obj -mtriple=x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu crates/hod_cg/tests/data/exclude/mason_meta.ll -o mason_meta-x86_64.o
hod -O0 -filetype=obj -mtriple=aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu crates/hod_cg/tests/data/exclude/mason_meta.ll -o mason_meta-aarch64.o
readelf -SW mason_meta-x86_64.o | grep '\.mason\.meta'
```

prints (the section's index is hod's and not promised):

```
  [ 2] .mason.meta       PROGBITS        0000000000000000 000050 000010 00   E  0   0  8
```

and `readelf -x .mason.meta` shows `4d4d7631 00ffc328 80feff00 e282007a`.

## Not done

- The section's position in the section table is hod's, not `llc`'s
  (the comparison does not compare section order; mortar finds the
  section by name and type).
- No other attachment kind changed: `!associated`, `!absolute_symbol`,
  `!kcfi_type` and unknown kinds stay `global-metadata:<kind>`.
