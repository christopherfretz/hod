# WP-120 — x86-64 packed SSE2, scalar extras, relocatable immediates, locked sweep, counters and string forms

Landed: `spec/mc-x64.md` sections "Packed SSE2", "Relocatable
immediates", "String instructions" (new); additions to "Scalar floating
point" (`mins`/`maxs`, `cvts2si`, `movup_rr`, `movdqu_rr`, `movq_rr`,
`movd_rm`/`movd_mr`), "System and padding" (`rdtsc`, `rdtscp`,
`cpuid`), "Memory operands" (`Mem::has_field`), "Instruction layout" (the
`rep` order), "Stack" (`push_i32`), "Atomics" (the locked sweep),
"Fixups and relocations" (`Fixup::Abs32U`, `Fixup::Abs64`, the `32`,
`64` and immediate `32S` rows), "Not provided" and "Test method". Design
0006 section 5, rows E2.

## What it did

- `src/packed.rs` (new): 179 constructors: 57 integer instructions in
  `_rr`/`_rm` form, 10 shifts by an immediate, `pshufd`/`pshuflw`/
  `pshufhw`, the packed floating-point arithmetic, `min`/`max`, `unpck`,
  `cmpp`, `shufp`, `movlhps`/`movhlps`, `movlp`/`movhp`, 8 conversions,
  `pmovmskb`, `movmskp`, `pextrw`, `pinsrw`.
- `src/sse.rs`: `mins`/`maxs`, `cvts2si` (checked), `movup_rr`,
  `movdqu_rr`, `movq_rr`, `movd_rm`/`movd_mr` (checked).
- `src/mov.rs`, `src/branch.rs`: `movabs_ri`, `mov_ri32s`, `mov_ri32`,
  `mov_mi32s` (checked), `push_i32`; `src/fixup.rs`, `src/inst.rs`: the
  two new absolute field kinds, the site bits widened; `src/reg.rs`:
  `Mem::has_field`.
- `src/system.rs`: `rdtsc`, `rdtscp`, `cpuid`, `cld`, `rep_movs`,
  `rep_stos`.
- The crate has 431 instruction constructors, 79 with a checked twin
  (230 and 74 before).
- `tests/l1`: five new groups (`locked-memory-destinations`,
  `relocatable-immediates`, `packed-integer`, `packed-floating-point`,
  `packed-memory-operands`), the scalar and system sweeps extended, 18
  more relocation sites, the fixup tests over five kinds; `rep` joins
  `lock` as a prefix in the disassembled mnemonic.

## Evidence

- 428,881 encodings in nineteen groups equal `llvm-mc`'s bytes and
  disassemble as the table says, at LLVM 22 and 19 (162,365 of them new).
  488 rejections (16 new; the movd/cvts2si ones with text are errors to
  `llvm-mc`).
- 104 relocation sites (18 new: `R_X86_64_64`, `R_X86_64_32`,
  `R_X86_64_32S` on the immediates, packed operands through RIP with and
  without an `imm8` after the field) agree with LLVM's relocations in
  offset, type and addend, at both majors.
- Mutations, each caught and reverted: `psrldq`'s `/3` changed to `/2`
  (288 disagreements in `packed_integer_matches_llvm`); `rep` placed after
  `66` by the common layout routine (`rep movsw` disagrees in
  `system_and_padding_matches_llvm`).
- Every instruction form of `docs/design/0006-data/abi/02-fp`, `07-vec`
  and `12-libcalls` (llc 19 and 22) is encodable, except the x87 forms
  (`fldt`, `fstpt`, `flds`, `fsqrt`, `fildll`, `faddp`), which are WP-119's.

## Decisions, and why

- **One constructor per integer mnemonic** (`paddb_rr`, `paddw_rr`, ...)
  rather than an element-size operand: the opcodes of the element sizes
  do not follow one rule across the families (`paddq` is `D4`, the
  saturating forms exist for two sizes only), so an operand would need a
  precondition per family. The floating-point forms keep the `FpSize`
  operand of "Scalar floating point", whose prefix rule is uniform.
- **Relocatable immediates are their own constructors**, not a flag on
  `mov_ri`: `mov_ri` picks the shortest encoding by value, and a field
  must have a fixed width whatever its placeholder value. Each new form
  always writes the full field and reports it as the site, with addend 0,
  like a `with_abs32` displacement.
- **`Abs32U` is a separate kind** from `Abs32`: `R_X86_64_32` checks an
  unsigned range, so `with_disp`/`patch` must accept `0..2^32` and refuse
  negatives there, unlike `32S`.
- **`mov_mi32s` refuses a memory operand that is a field**
  (`Mem::has_field`): an `Inst` has one site. The code generator loads
  such an address into a register.
- **`rep` is written first**, ahead of `66`, as LLVM does; the common
  layout routine would put it after `66`, so the string forms prepend it.
- **`movq` memory forms are LLVM's** (`F3 0F 7E`, `66 0F D6`), selected by
  the size operand of `movd_rm`/`movd_mr`.

## Gaps

- `push_i32` with a value that is a sign-extended byte has no LLVM text
  with the same bytes (LLVM picks `6A ib`); it is checked as the zero form
  patched (`constructors_agree_with_patching`) and through its relocation.
- In "Not provided": SSE3 and later, MMX, `maskmovdqu`, non-temporal
  stores; the other string instructions; a relocatable `imm32` on
  instructions other than `mov` and `push`.
- `Fixup` gained two variants: an exhaustive `match` on it outside the
  crate (none exists today) must add them.
