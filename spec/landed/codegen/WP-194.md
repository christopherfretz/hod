# WP-194: the hardening flags together

## What is implemented

- **Composition** (`spec/codegen.md` §5.6.30): the order of the pieces in
  the prologue and the epilogue on both targets, and the registers each
  piece may use. No combination is refused for being one.
- **AArch64 looped probe under a temporary frame record**
  (`crates/hod_a64/src/probe.rs`, §5.6.27 item 2): for `k > 4` pages the
  allocation is `stp x29, x30, [sp, #-16]!` · `mov x29, sp` · the loop ·
  the rest · `ldr x29, [x29]`, with the CFA on `x29 + 16` while `sp`
  moves, then `sp + FRAME` until the usual record is saved. Before, the
  CFA was on `x17`, then `sp`. `.eh_frame` was right, but lldb emulates
  frame 0 at a non-call site and does not model the loop, so it lost one
  page per pass and `step-inst-over` left the function. Nothing is
  written below `sp`; the unrolled shapes (`k <= 4`) are unchanged.
- `crates/hod/tests/hardening.rs`: every combination of `ssp`
  (none, `ssp`, `sspstrong`, `sspreq`) × probing × protection (x86-64:
  none, `cf-protection-branch`; AArch64: none, `bti`, `non-leaf`,
  `all`+`bti`, `non-leaf`+B key+`bti`, `all`+B key) over 15 shapes
  (dynamic, VLA, stack and `byval` arguments, `invoke` with and without
  them, `musttail` (plain, indirect, dynamic, with arguments), `nest`
  (AArch64), varargs, `setjmp`) and five frame sizes (24 to 1 MiB), plus
  `preserve_most`/`preserve_all` on x86-64. Built by hod, hod under
  `Stress::ALL`, and `llc` 19 and 22, linked with bfd, lld and mold; the
  output must be the same everywhere. Also: BTI enforced at every
  landing pad (AArch64 host, kernel-enforced), an overflow caught under
  every flag set, and a guard-page fault inside the probe loop unwinding
  through signed, protected frames (`backtrace()` from the handler).
- `tests/debug/hardening_a64.cpp`, `tests/debug/hardening_x64.cpp`: gdb
  and lldb break at the first instruction of a probed, protected,
  guarded function with a dynamic area, single-step every instruction of
  its prologue, body and epilogue (and through a throw), and check `bt`
  at each step; then the caller's frame and its variables.

## Evidence

- Combination, BTI, overflow and guard-fault tests: 4/4 pass, today's
  shape and the looped shape (`HOD_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1`).
- Debug: x86-64 6 PASS (gdb, lldb-19; bfd, lld, mold); AArch64 6 PASS
  with the looped shape (3 XFAIL in lldb before it).
- `cargo xtask debug verify` on both `-g` modules: clean. `.eh_frame`:
  every row ascending and inside its FDE, in every hod object below.
- Real units: five small programs (STL, exceptions, virtual calls, VLA,
  `alloca`, varargs, 10 arguments and a by-value struct, 50 KB recursive
  frames, `setjmp`, a coroutine) and fmt (`src/format.cc`, `src/os.cc`,
  a driver), with Debian's flag set (`-fstack-protector-strong
  -fstack-clash-protection -D_FORTIFY_SOURCE=2`, `-fcf-protection` or
  `-mbranch-protection=standard`, `-z relro -z now`, PIE) and Fedora's
  (also `_FORTIFY_SOURCE=3`, `_GLIBCXX_ASSERTIONS`,
  `-fasynchronous-unwind-tables`, `-fno-omit-frame-pointer
  -mno-omit-leaf-frame-pointer`, `-z pack-relative-relocs`), at `-O0` and
  `-O0 -g`, on both targets, three linkers:

  | front end | modules | hod (strict) | refused | outputs as control | notes as control |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | kiln | 64 | 56 | 0 | all | all |
  | clang 22 | 64 | 56 | 8 `debug:retained-nodes` | all built | all built |

  kiln takes the LLVM route itself for the coroutine unit (8 modules), so
  those are `llc`'s. kiln accepts `-fstack-clash-protection` and writes no
  `"probe-stack"` (its `docs/status/silent-drops.md`), so only the clang
  rows exercise probing; with clang every module carries
  `"probe-stack"="inline-asm"`. `_FORTIFY_SOURCE` makes no `__*_chk` call
  at `-O0` with either front end. The objects' property notes (AArch64
  BTI, PAC, GCS; x86-64 IBT, SHSTK) are `llc`'s, and so are the linked
  programs' with every linker: BTI, PAC with bfd and lld on AArch64
  (mold writes none); on x86-64 no linker keeps IBT or SHSTK, as the
  cross start files carry neither.
- `debug:retained-nodes` (not hardening: `hod_dwarf/src/inline.rs:234`,
  refused without the flags too): an `always_inline` function with a
  function-local using-declaration (fmt's `FMT_INLINE` `visit`).
  Reproducer: `.build/findings/oss/hardening/debug:retained-nodes/`.
- kiln rejects two Fedora flags: `-grecord-gcc-switches` and
  `-mtls-dialect=gnu2`. They were dropped from the set.
