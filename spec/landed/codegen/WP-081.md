# WP-081 — vector masks (design 0003, V2)

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/mask.rs` (`<N x i1>`, `N ≤ 64`, as a
bit-packed general-register value, and `llvm.vector.reduce.*`), the hooks
below, `crates/hod_a64/tests/mask.rs`, `tests/ir/vector_masks.ll`, the
heading "Vectors, masks (WP-081)" in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1, the
support-matrix rows, and the differential harness's `vectors` switch
(`crates/hod_a64/tests/common/groups.rs`).

## Hooks (one call each)

- `crates/hod_a64/src/target.rs`, `vector_class`: a vector of `i1` of at
  most 64 lanes is `Gpr`; the first line of `A64::lower`
  (`mask::lower`); the constant arm of `A64::materialize`
  (`mask::materialize`, which hands every non-mask to
  `common::materialize_gpr_const` as before).
- `crates/hod_a64/src/vector.rs`, `has_mem_vector`: any vector of `i1`
  in a signature is `a64:vector-call` (a `<4 x i1>` is now `Gpr` and
  would otherwise pass as an integer: an ABI WP-082 must decide); its
  unit test `shapes_follow_the_layout` expects `<8 x i1>` to be `Gpr`.
- `int::cond_of` and `float::conditions` made `pub` (the predicate
  tables, reused per lane).
- `pub mod mask;` in `lib.rs`.
- Tests of other groups that asserted refusals now lowered, removed:
  `tests/aggr.rs` (`icmp <3 x i32>` → `a64:mem-vector`), `tests/float.rs`
  (`icmp`/`fcmp`/`select` of `Fpr` vectors → `a64:vector-icmp`,
  `a64:vector-fcmp`, `a64:vector-select`), `tests/vector.rs`
  (`extractelement`/`shufflevector` of `<4 x i1>`, `<64 x i1>` →
  `a64:vector-mask`). `float::vector_icmp` and the `VECTOR_*` names stay
  (unreachable for masks now).

## What is lowered

Vector `icmp` (`i8`..`i64`, `ptr` lanes) and `fcmp` (`half`, `float`,
`double`), every predicate, 1 to 64 lanes, both classes of operand;
`add` .. `xor`, the shifts, divisions and remainders, and `icmp` on
masks (one bit instruction); `select` of masks and of lanes on a mask;
`zext`, `sext`, `uitofp`, `sitofp` of a mask; `trunc`, `fptoui`,
`fptosi` to a mask; `extractelement`, `insertelement` (constant or
variable index), `shufflevector` of masks; stores of masks (padding
cleared); mask constants (poison and undefined lanes 0); and
`llvm.vector.reduce.{add,mul,and,or,xor,smax,smin,umax,umin}` of masks
and of integer lanes, `{fadd,fmul,fmax,fmin}` of float lanes, up to 256
lanes. Matrix: `Vector` added to `inst:icmp` and `inst:fcmp`; 13 rows
`intrinsic:llvm.vector.reduce.*`.

## What is refused, by name

`a64:mask-wide` (a lane operation on more than 64 `i1`);
`a64:vector-call` (a mask in a signature: WP-082); `a64:vector-bfloat`
(`fcmp` of `bfloat` lanes); `a64:vector-reduce` (a reduction whose
operand list is not the intrinsic's); an intrinsic of `i1` lanes stays
`a64:intrinsic-vector`; `reduce.fmaximum`/`fminimum` stay the intrinsic
group's refusal.

## Evidence

- `tests/mask.rs`, seven suites, 1,708 functions, 79,853 comparisons
  with a Rust model, bit by bit (poison lanes and the bytes that hold
  them excluded; padding of stored masks compared): `icmp` 245 / 10,290,
  `fcmp` 240 / 9,600, mask operations 499 / 20,933, shuffles 42 / 1,764,
  casts 224 / 9,216, selects and libc++ chains (`sext (icmp)`, `select
  (fcmp)`, `reduce.or (icmp)`, `cttz (bitcast (icmp))`) 136 / 5,616,
  reductions 322 / 13,194. Legs: `hod_interp` on value-form twins (no
  `half`; no `reduce.fmax`/`fmin`, which it lacks; a NaN result, where
  it stops, skipped), hod plain under bfd, lld and mold, hod under
  `Stress::ALL`, `llc -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` of LLVM 22 and
  19. Zero differences. Plus masks loaded with their padding bits set,
  hod legs only (`llc` assumes them zero): 385 functions, 9,240.
- Settled by those legs: stored padding zero (`llc` and the interpreter
  agree); `llc` lets the garbage of an out-of-range `fptoui`/`fptosi` to
  `i1` lane spill into the other lanes of its byte, which LLVM's
  byte-granular poison allows, so such bytes are not compared. No other
  `llc` defect met on a mask path.
- `tests/diff.rs` with the generator's `vectors` switch on (the line
  under WP-081 in `tests/common/groups.rs`): 1,000 + 200 + 200 programs
  agree with the interpreter, none skipped; `missing` is now empty.
- `tests/ir/vector_masks.ll` (mismatch, find, reductions, `<3 x i1>`
  through memory, a global mask) through `cargo xtask irdiff`: PASS
  under bfd, lld and mold.
- Cost (`the_code_is_linear_in_the_lanes`, 16 .. 64 lanes, equal bytes
  per lane asserted): `icmp <N x i32>` 32 bytes per lane, `fcmp double`
  32, `select` of `i16` lanes 23, `sext` to `i64` 12, `reduce.umax` of
  `i16` 21; under 1 µs per lane at 64 lanes in a debug build. Nothing is
  allocated per lane.
- Mutations, each caught and reverted: (1) the padding not cleared on
  store: 840 differences (mask operations); (2) shuffle lanes of the
  first operand read reversed: 444; (3) signed compares sign-extending
  only lanes under 32 bits: 408.
