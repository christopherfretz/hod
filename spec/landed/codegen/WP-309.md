# WP-309 — i386: the integer intrinsics of 1 to 32 bits

Landed: `crates/hod_x86/src/bits.rs`: `bswap`, `ctpop`, `ctlz`, `cttz`
(both `is_zero_poison`), `bitreverse`, `abs` (both `is_int_min_poison`),
`smax`/`smin`/`umax`/`umin`, `sadd.sat`/`uadd.sat`/`ssub.sat`/`usub.sat`,
`sshl.sat`/`ushl.sat`, `fshl`/`fshr` (constant and variable amounts)
with a result of 1 to 32 bits, and `scmp`/`ucmp` of operands of 1 to 32
bits with results of 2 to 64 bits. Behaviour: `spec/codegen-x86.md` §4.1.
Tests: `crates/hod/tests/i386_bits.rs`.

Edits outside `bits.rs`: `lib.rs` (`pub mod bits;`), `call.rs`
(`lower`: one arm, after WP-306's wide arm, sending a narrow integer
intrinsic to `bits`). `alu.rs`, `int.rs`, `target.rs`: none.

## Decisions

- The shapes are `hod_x64`'s at 32 bits; every `cmov` is a branch, on
  every CPU (`generic` has no `cmov`; as `alu::select`).
- No `popcnt`/`lzcnt`/`tzcnt`: `hod_ir::X86Features` carries `popcnt`
  but not `lzcnt` or `bmi`, and no `hod_x86` lowering reads the
  function's features yet; the generic sequences are exact on every CPU.
  `llc` uses all three at `haswell`; the results are equal.
- Each operand is asked for once (`use_reg` must not see a value twice);
  repeated operands (`fshl(x, x, c)`, `smax(x, x)`, `sshl.sat(x, x)`)
  share the register. At most three pool registers are pinned, no
  temporary: `Stress::ALL`'s minimum pool suffices.
- The unspecified bits above `N` are dropped by the operation (reversals
  shift right, the saturating shift moves the value to the top first,
  counts/compares/clamps extend; `cttz` sets bit `N`).
- The variable funnel amount `c mod N` for `N` no power of two is a
  32-bit `div` by `N`; `llc` multiplies by a magic constant (same result).
- `bswap` of 8, 24 bits: the verifier rejects it ("bswap must be an even
  number of bytes"); `bits` refuses it as `x86:intrinsic` if it ever
  arrives. No width of 1..32 is refused by the shared classifier for
  these intrinsics.
- `scmp`/`ucmp` of operands of 33 to 64 bits were not done: `int.rs`'s
  word compare (`compare_slots`) is private to that file; they stay
  `x86:intrinsic`.

## Branches of `llc` (for `x87::barrier`)

Measured with `gen.py --probe <dir>` here and
`tests/data/i386/i64_rest/branches.py` on `llc` 19 and 22, `-O0
-global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`, `N` = 1, 7, 8, 16, 24, 31, 32:

- CPU `generic` (no `cmov`): `ctlz` (`is_zero_poison` false) at
  7..32; `cttz` (`is_zero_poison` false) at 32 only; `smax`, `smin`,
  `umax`, `umin` at 7..32; `sadd.sat`, `uadd.sat`, `ssub.sat`, `usub.sat`
  at 7..32 (LLVM 22) and also at 1 (LLVM 19); `sshl.sat`, `ushl.sat` at
  7..32 (the same-operand forms likewise; `ssub.sat(x, x)`,
  `usub.sat(x, x)`, `smax(x, x)` fold and never branch).
- CPU `pentium4`, `haswell`: none.
- Never: `bswap`, `ctpop`, `bitreverse`, `abs`, `ctlz`/`cttz` with
  `is_zero_poison` true, `fshl`/`fshr` (constant or variable), `scmp`,
  `ucmp`.

## Evidence

`i386_bits.rs`: four programs (`unary.ll`, `binary.ll`, `funnel.ll`,
`compare.ll`, all written by `gen.py`) × LLVM 19, 22 × CPU generic,
pentium4, haswell (qemu with `popcnt`, `abm`, `bmi1`, ...) ×
position-dependent, PIE = 48 modules; 144 runs (llc's, hod's, hod's
under `Stress::ALL`), 758,640 lines of llc output in all, equal byte for
byte. Operands: per width 0, 1, all ones, the sign bit, the sign bit
less one, 2 and four patterns, each also with the bits above the width
set, and four 32-bit patterns; every pair for two-operand intrinsics;
funnel amounts 0..33, `N - 1..N + 1`, `2N - 1`, `2N + 5` and patterns
with high bits; 14 constant amounts; `scmp`/`ucmp` results of 2, 8, 16,
32, 33, 64 bits.

`cargo xtask irdiff --target i686 --tier quick` over `tests/ir`, base
`fe28dec4` and head: 25 tests (75 results), 9 PASS, 51 UNSUPPORTED, 15
ERROR (oracle side) at both; declined `x86:intrinsic` 1 → 0, `x86:eh`
7 → 8 (`tail_intrinsics.ll`: its narrow intrinsics compile, an `invoke`
is next); the rest equal.

`cargo xtask compare-binaries` over the AArch64 and x86-64 kiln corpora:
13438 comparisons, 13434 identical, 0 different, 0 changed outcome, 4
identical failures.
