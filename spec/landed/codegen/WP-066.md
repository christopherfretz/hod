# WP-066 — the AArch64 instruction groups together

Landed: the fused `condbr` in `crates/hod_a64/src/control.rs`; the
facade's classifier (`crates/hod/src/front.rs`, `classifier_for`); the
WP-066 line of `crates/hod_a64/tests/common/groups.rs` (no group missing
now); the doc comments of `crates/hod_a64/src/mem.rs` near
`float::load`/`float::store`; `SHIPPED_REASONS` for the AArch64 body
column in `crates/hod_ir/tests/classify_matrix.rs`; the heading "The
groups together (WP-066)" in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1. No hook was
added to a group file outside `control.rs`.

## What it did

- **`musttail` through the facade.** `hod::compile`, the `hod` binary
  and the census judge AArch64 `musttail` calls with
  `TargetStackArgs::new(&A64)`; x86-64 keeps no rule.
  `crates/hod/tests/musttail.rs`: a program of seven `musttail` callers
  (ten permuted arguments, `signext`/`zeroext` stack arguments, `byval`,
  a variadic thunk forwarding `...` to `printf`, a clobber before the
  thunk call, an indirect callee, a `{i64, i64}` result) built by hod
  (strict) and `llc`, run under GNU ld, lld and mold: the same output
  (`llc` 22 cannot tail-call the `byval` forward, so its build calls).
  A `musttail` whose area grows is refused, `musttail:stack-args-grow`.
- **Fused `condbr`.** A deferred `icmp` or `fcmp` that is the branch's
  only operand sets the flags and the branch tests them (one condition,
  or two for `one`/`ueq`; `fp128` calls the comparison routine first).
  `tests/fused.rs`: every `icmp` predicate × widths 1..64 and `ptr` ×
  six operand forms × three block layouts × four shapes (fused, phi on
  the edge, two uses, separated): 5670 functions, 34020 calls against
  the interpreter and `llc`; `fcmp` of `float`/`double` 288 functions,
  1728 calls; `half` and `fp128` 1152 calls each against an IEEE oracle
  of the test's own; an object check that a fused branch has no `cset`
  and a separated one keeps its `tbnz`.
- **Phis and selects of every class** (`tests/phis.rs`): diamonds with
  critical edges, loops with permuted phis (swap cycles, a phi reading a
  phi), selects, over 15 types (`i33` .. `i256`, `half` .. `fp128`, three
  vectors, four aggregates). Quick: 300 functions, 1800 calls; the
  ignored `many_phi_graphs_agree`: 6000 functions, 36000 calls. No gap
  found.
- **`Mem`-class values across calls** (`tests/mem_calls.rs`): `i128`,
  wide and odd integers, aggregates with odd leaves, `fp128`, `sret`,
  `byval`, in registers and on the stack, between hod (plain and
  stressed), `llc` and `gcc` in every pairing, both directions. Quick:
  450 IR calls and 300 gcc calls; ignored: 4500 + 4500. No bug found.
- **The odd-leaf test of WP-069** (`tests/call.rs`,
  `data/call/odd*.{ll,c}`) observes the home directly now: the callee
  stores its argument, the driver checks each leaf's bytes (before
  WP-067 that store was `a64:mem-store`).
- **The generator harness**: `tests/diff.rs` with every `GROUPS` line on,
  1000 programs in the quick tier; `HOD_DIFF_SEEDS=40000`: 40000
  programs agree, none skipped, 3.12 s per 1000.
- **`SHIPPED_REASONS`**: the AArch64 body column has no `*` left; every
  reason is supported except `debug:locations`, `inst:atomicrmw`
  (`atomicrmw add`), `bundle:deopt`.

## Still refused

- `a64:invoke-odd-int-result`: an `invoke` whose `Mem`-class result has
  an odd-width integer leaf. The clear above the leaves must come after
  the call and before the normal edge's phi stores, and `FnCx::call`
  (`hod_cg/src/call.rs`) emits those with the branch; there is no
  target hook between. No corpus module has one.

## Mutations caught

- `branch_on` branching on `first` for `first.invert()`: four fused
  tests fail.
- the second condition's branch dropped: the `half`, `fcmp` and shape
  tests fail.
- a phi of class `Fpr` stored as `D` instead of `Q`: `phis` fails.
- `clear_above_leaves` skipped after a call: `mem_calls` fails.
- the entry clear of `hod_cg` (`driver.rs`) skipped: `mem_calls` and the
  odd-leaf test fail (136 of 240 checks, 62 of them the new store
  checks).

## Corpus

`cargo xtask corpus reasons -j 4` (strict): 3074 of the LLVM 22 modules
and 3040 of the LLVM 19 ones compile. The last report
(`docs/status/reasons.md`, 2984/2952) is older than WP-069 and WP-070,
so most of that rise is theirs. WP-066's part is the six modules per
LLVM that were `musttail:stack-args-unknown` (`musttail-deep-run`,
`variadic-thunk-run`, two `coro.generator`, `symmetric_transfer`,
`awaiter_protocol`). They compile now, and no `musttail:` reason is
left. `corpus verify --strict -j 4` over those 12 modules: 36 results,
36 PASS (bfd, lld, mold).
