# WP-128 — x86-64 intrinsics and the short tail (X-intrinsic)

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/intrinsic.rs`, `crates/hod_x64/src/barrier.rs`;
`spec/codegen.md` §5.6.8; the support-matrix rows below;
`tests/intrinsic.rs` (with `tests/common/intrinsic_gen.rs` and
`tests/common/intrinsic_mem.rs`) and `tests/barrier.rs` (with
`tests/data/barrier/`); the seeds `tests/ir/bit_intrinsics.ll`,
`mem_intrinsics.ll` and `overflow_intrinsics.ll` now run on x86-64.

## What is lowered

- `ctlz`, `cttz`, `ctpop`, `bswap`, `bitreverse`, `abs`, `smin`, `smax`,
  `umin`, `umax`, `fshl`, `fshr`, `scmp`, `ucmp`,
  `{s,u}{add,sub}.sat` at every width from 1 to 64 bits and at 128;
  `{s,u}shl.sat` up to 64 bits.
- `memcpy`, `memcpy.inline`, `memmove`, `memset`, `memset.inline`
  (inline up to the thresholds, a loop for long `.inline` forms, a
  call otherwise).
- `trap`, `ubsantrap` (`ud2`), `debugtrap` (`int3`), `readcyclecounter`,
  `frameaddress`, `returnaddress`, `prefetch`, `clear_cache`, `ptrmask`,
  `eh.typeid.for`.
- `is.constant` and `objectsize` (lowered; their rows stay off, see
  below).
- Inline assembly whose template is empty (`call:inline-asm-empty`).

## Refused, by name

`x64:intrinsic`, `x64:intrinsic-wide`, `x64:intrinsic-vector`,
`x64:mem-inline-length`, `x64:frame-depth`, `x64:eh-typeid`,
`x64:inline-asm` (a template that is not empty),
`x64:inline-asm-output`, `-outputs`, `-constraint`, `-clobber`,
`-operand`, `-bundle`.

## Differences from `llc`

| difference | settled |
|---|---|
| `ubsantrap`: `llc` writes `ud1` with the check's kind | `ud2`: `hod_mc_x64` has no `ud1` encoding; the signal (`SIGILL`) is the same |
| `is.constant`/`objectsize` operands: `llc -O0` folds the call and does not compute them | `intrinsic::begin`/`dropped` do the same, but `Target::begin_function` and `lower` (in `target.rs`, not this package's) do not call them yet; the rows stay `todo(X64)` and `tests/ir/tail_intrinsics.ll` keeps its directive |
| constraint letters `a`, `b`, `c`, `d`, `S`, `D`, `A` | `llc` refuses them in IR ("couldn't allocate input reg"); front ends write `{ax}`. Refused as `x64:inline-asm-constraint` |

## Rows

Switched on for x86-64: memcpy, memcpy.inline, memmove, memset,
memset.inline, frameaddress, returnaddress, prefetch, ptrmask (`Ptr`,
`Int`); trap, debugtrap; ubsantrap, readcyclecounter, sshl.sat,
ushl.sat (`Int`); bswap through ucmp and the four saturating rows
(`Int`, `I128`); eh.typeid.for; clear_cache; `call:inline-asm-empty`.
`Int` added to the six `with.overflow` rows and to the x86-64 cells of
`inst:extractvalue`/`inst:insertvalue` (WP-126's rows: `aggr` lowers
their register fields, and the overflow seed needs them). Left
`todo(X64)`: `is.constant`, `objectsize`.

## Evidence

Every integer table runs hod plain and stressed, `llc` 22 and 19, and
mixed objects, natively, against the interpreter (`sshl.sat`/`ushl.sat`
against `llc` only, since the interpreter does not run them):

| suite | functions | cases | hod/`llc` text bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| narrow (bit counts, abs, min/max, funnel) | 652 | 23,845 | 140,882 / 41,818 |
| sat | 304 | 12,112 | 94,428 / 23,272 |
| cmp3 | 260 | 8,340 | 75,413 / 23,130 |
| i128 | 33 | 3,813 | 11,510 / 3,083 |
| shlsat | 72 | 3,942 | 19,210 / 7,926 |

- Memory intrinsics: 1,441 systematic functions (every length 0 to 130,
  every alignment, overlaps in both directions) and 600 random ones
  agree with the interpreter plain, stressed, and with `llc` 22 and 19.
- Every flag reader in the generated code follows its setter with only
  flag-preserving instructions in between (over 5,000 readers).
- Native forms (frames, traps as `SIGILL`/`SIGTRAP`, `objectsize`,
  `is.constant`, `ptrmask`, `readcyclecounter`): 12 link-and-run
  checks under bfd, lld and mold. Barriers: 24 link-and-run checks
  (hod plain and stressed, `llc` 19 and 22; dynamic and `-static`).
- irdiff on x86-64: 78 pass, 0 fail with LLVM 19 and 22.

Mutations, each caught and then reverted:

1. `ctlz` at the register width puts `2·w` instead of `2·w − 1` in
   place of a zero operand's `bsr` result: 12 disagreements in `every_integer_intrinsic_at_every_width_agrees`.
2. `memmove` stores the first piece before loading the last:
   `every_length_and_alignment_agrees` fails at `s_memmove_Const_17`.
3. The signed saturation bound is `2^(w−1) − 2`: 282 disagreements in
   `saturating_arithmetic_agrees`.

At the merge the supervisor added the two calls in `target.rs`
(`intrinsic::begin` in `begin_function`, `intrinsic::dropped` at the top of
`lower`), switched the `is.constant` and `objectsize` rows on for x86-64
and opened `tests/ir/tail_intrinsics.ll` for x86-64.
