# WP-315 — i386: memory intrinsics, aggregates, overflow and small intrinsics

First half of design 0008 I11. Behaviour: `spec/codegen-x86.md` §13 (§12 before the i386 join D
renumbered it; and
the edited §5, §7, §8, §11). Tests: `crates/hod/tests/i386_mem.rs`,
`crates/hod/tests/i386_agg.rs` with `tests/data/i386/{mem,agg}/`.

Landed:

- `bytes.rs`: lengths of 33 to 64 bits (their low word); `calls_libc` /
  `site_calls_libc`, `llc`'s choice between inline stores, `rep movs`/
  `stos` and a libc call, used as the x87 barrier for the memory
  intrinsics (it replaced `x87.rs`'s 32/64-byte thresholds).
- `agg.rs` (new): aggregates in frame slots (`load`, `store`, `select`,
  `extractvalue`, `insertvalue`, constants including an address as an
  `i64`, results in memory through `Incoming(0)`); the six
  `*.with.overflow` intrinsics at widths 1 to 64.
- `bits.rs` (new functions `is_small`, `small`, `FRAME_DEPTH`): the small
  intrinsics.
- `abi.rs` `Leaves::walk`: an integer of 33 to 64 bits is two leaves, so
  33 to 63 bits pass and return by value, variadic arguments included.

Edits outside the owned modules: `lib.rs` (`pub mod agg`); `target.rs`
(the `agg::owns` arm, the extract/insert arms, `materialize_mem`'s
aggregate case); `call.rs` `lower` (the overflow and small arms);
`control.rs` `ret` (results in memory); `frame.rs` `begin` (the
`ret_memory` refusal removed); `x87.rs` `barrier` (memory-intrinsic arm,
aggregate `select`); `abi.rs` `Leaves::walk` (above). Tests of other
packages: `i386_bits.rs` `what_stays_refused` and `i386_i64_rest.rs`
`overflow_intrinsics_compile` (the overflow intrinsics now compile);
`tests/data/i386/join_b/calls.c` `make()` has its plain initializer
again (an `i64`-length `memset`), as authorized.

## Decisions

- `llc`'s libc-call rule was measured, not guessed (grid of 4410 shapes,
  `the_libc_call_rule_is_llc_s`), at CPU `generic` and `i686`; a CPU with
  SSE (wider stores) is not modelled: §11 already refuses SSE floating
  point, and an SSE CPU changes only which programs keep 80-bit values.
- hod's own expansion stays `rep movsb`/`stosb` whatever `llc` picks: the
  rule decides only the barrier.
- An aggregate is a `Mem` value; no `FnState` field was added. The
  128-bit product of the wide multiplication lives in a slot of its own
  (`alloc_slot`), not in registers (the pool can be one register under
  `Stress::ALL`).
- `objectsize` answers what `hod_cg::const_fold::object_size` answers
  (-1 or 0): `llc` evaluates known objects (a global's size, for one);
  the gap is the shared fold's (`hod_cg`), as on x86-64.
- `readcyclecounter` needed no `hod_mc_x64` edit: `rdtsc` is admitted in
  32-bit mode as it is.

## Evidence (head against base `31400b11`)

- `i386_mem`: 16 modules, 96 runs equal `llc`'s; the rule: 4410 shapes in
  4 `llc` runs. `i386_agg`: 32 modules, 192 runs (the generated module
  covers 6 operations x 64 widths, all pairs of 6 to 16 edge values).
- All 16 `i386*` tests of `-p hod`: 48 passed. x87 shape counts
  unchanged: 288 equal `llc`, 72 equal the model; integer intrinsics 190
  equal `llc`, 2 follow LLVM 22. `HOD_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1 cargo test -p
  hod_x86 -p hod_cg`: 112 passed, 6 ignored.
- irdiff i686 full tier, LLVM 22: 112 tests, 248 results, 9 PASS before
  and after; declined before `x86:eh` 35, `x86:intrinsic` 6, `x86:i64` 4,
  `x86:aggregate` 3, ...; after `x86:eh` 42, `x86:intrinsic` 2
  (`coro/noop.ll`, `dwarf_cfa.ll`: not this package's intrinsics),
  `x86:stack-protector` 2, `x86:constant` 1 (`const_operands.ll`: an
  `i64` difference of two addresses in different sections, `int.rs`),
  `x86:float:fp128` 1; no `x86:i64`, no `x86:aggregate`. LLVM 19 the same
  shape (246 results, 9 PASS). The seeds move to their next refusal,
  mostly `x86:eh`; no kept-off directive names this package's
  constructs, so none was removed.
- `compare-binaries` base `31400b11` against head over `tests/ir` and
  both kiln corpora (`-j 4`): 14037 modules, 28518 comparisons;
  AArch64 and x86-64 28287 identical, 0 different, 0 changed outcome, 9
  identical failures;
  i686 8 identical, 22 changed outcome (11 seeds x plain/PIC), 192
  identical failures. Every changed outcome is a refusal that moved
  (`x86:aggregate`, `x86:i64`, `x86:intrinsic` before; `x86:eh` 7,
  `x86:stack-protector` 2, `x86:constant` 1, `x86:float:fp128` 1 after,
  per mode); none went from building to failing.
