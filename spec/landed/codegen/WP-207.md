# WP-207 — the AVX2 gathers

Landed: the encoder `gather` with its `Vsib` operand and the `Gather`
family in `crates/hod_mc_x64/src/avx.rs` (the bytes in `inst.rs`'s
`vex_vsib`; `spec/mc-x64.md`), 16 `hod_ir` `Intrinsic` members
(`X86Avx2GatherDD` … `X86Avx2GatherQPd256`, at the end of WP-202's
block; the scale in `immediate_args`), their matrix rows at the end of
`INTRINSIC_X86` (`never(X64_ONLY)`, `on(X86_SSE)`), their signatures in
`hod_ir/tests/body_llvm.rs`, `docs/status/support.md` (generated), the
rows and `gather` in `crates/hod_x64/src/x86/ext.rs`, `spec/codegen.md`
§5.6.33 (5) and (6). Tests: `crates/hod_x64/tests/x86_ext.rs`
(`x86_gathers_agree_with_llc`), `crates/hod_mc_x64/tests/l1/avx.rs`
(`vex-gathers`).

The finding: the highway module (`memory_test.cc`) refused on 8
`avx2.gather.*` names in its AVX2 functions, the only refusals there
below AVX-512.

## Census

WP-202's census (`spec/landed/codegen/WP-202.md`): 187 names, 171 built
before, 187 built after. The 16 gathers are all of `avx2intrin`'s
refusals.

`llvm.masked.gather`: clang 19, clang 22 and kiln write
`llvm.x86.avx2.gather.*` for every gather of `<immintrin.h>` under
`-mavx2` (a C file calling each, `-O0 --target=x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`),
never the generic intrinsic. It stays refused by name.

## Design

§5.6.33 (6). No register is reserved: the destination is `ymm14`, a
256-bit index or mask is loaded into `ymm15`, a 128-bit one is used in
its own register (it dies at its only use, §4.2), both 256-bit puts the
index in a pool temporary. At most two vector registers of the pool are
pinned, so the stress pool of two suffices.

Highway: the census (`HOD_CENSUS=1`) of the base lists the 8 gathers the
module calls (`d.d`, `d.ps`, `q.pd`, `q.q` and their `.256`) and 68
other names; the head's lists those 68, every
one AVX-512 (`avx512*`, `avx512bf16`, `vcvtneps2bf16128`) or a generic
`llvm.masked.load`/`masked.store` (`v16i8` to `v8f64`); every function
calling one of them has `+avx512f` in its `target-features`. Those are
WP-208's.

## Cost on the bench inputs

None: the bench inputs call no `llvm.x86.*` intrinsic, and no function
without a gather changes.
