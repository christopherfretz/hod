# WP-171 — x86-64 vectors of `i128` across calls; what stays refused

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/abi.rs` (`check_i128_vector`: the placement
rule), `crates/hod_x64/tests/vcall_i128.rs` (new),
`crates/hod_x64/tests/leftovers3.rs` (`wide_vector_and_byval_refusals_by_name`:
`<2 x i128>` after three `i64`s now compiles; the refused case is after
five), `spec/abi-x64.md` §4.3, §10, §12, `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.20.

## What `llc` does

Measured with `llc -O0 -fast-isel=0` 19 and 22, by execution (each
caller against each callee under `qemu-x86_64`): LLVM 22 passes
`<N x i128>` as 2N ordinary 8-byte pieces. LLVM 19 places only the
**first** element as a lone `i128` (two registers, or both halves on the
stack aligned to 16, `r9` then left unused) and every later piece as
LLVM 22 does: `<2 x i128>` after three `i64`s is `rcx`, `r8`, `r9`,
`s+0` under both, and `<3 x i128>` after one is `rsi` .. `r9`, `s+0`
under both. WP-152's rule (every piece in registers, or every piece on
the stack from 0 mod 16) refused those. The new rule accepts the vector
when its first element is in two registers or on the stack from 0 mod
16; the rest stays `x64:vector-i128-split`.

## What stays refused

- `x64:vector-i128-split`: the first element's low half in `r9`, or the
  vector on the stack from 8 mod 16. LLVM 19 and 22 disagree there.
- `musttail:stack-args-unknown` (3 modules): all three forward a
  signature whose `<1 x i128>` is refused as above (`r9` and the stack
  in one, on the stack at 8 mod 16 in two), so the facade's rule
  (`spec/abi-x64.md` §8.5, item 6) has no answer. These are correct
  refusals, not a gap of their own.
- `call:part-register-class` (8 modules, `<1 x i64>`; `<1 x ptr>` too):
  the value is `Fpr` and the part is a general register. The call
  sequence and function entry in `hod_cg` refuse a part of another class
  (`call.rs` `check_class`, `driver.rs` the entry's class check), and a
  `Lanes` part of an `Fpr` value is `call:part-lanes` there. Making the
  value `Gpr` would reach every vector lowering. The smallest `hod_cg`
  change: WP-167's (a `Lanes` part of a register value gathered and
  produced in a scratch register of the value's class at the argument,
  result, `place_return` and entry paths). Then `abi.rs` gives
  `<1 x i64>`/`<1 x ptr>` one `Lanes { bits: 64, lane: 8, first: 0,
  len: 1 }` part, and `vcall.rs` moves it with `movq` between `xmm` and
  the general register.

Differences from `llc`: as a leaf of an aggregate a vector of `i128`
follows LLVM 22 (LLVM 19 pairs its first element, as it does an `i128`
leaf, `spec/abi-x64.md` §4.2); unchanged by this package.
