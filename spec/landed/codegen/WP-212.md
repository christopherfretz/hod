# WP-212 part 1 — `.cfi_*` in x86-64 templates, AArch64 `.symver`

Landed: call-frame directives in x86-64 inline-assembly templates
(`crates/hod_asm/src/x64/mod.rs` `assemble_with_cfi`, `Cfi`, `CfiRow`;
`crates/hod_x64/src/barrier/template.rs` `emit`), the rows
`RememberState`/`RestoreState` (`crates/hod_eh/src/lib.rs`), `cfi_at`
inserting in offset order (`crates/hod_cg/src/cx.rs`), the core's
restatement applying remember/restore (`crates/hod_cg/src/driver.rs`
`restate_body_cfi`), `.symver` in AArch64 module assembly
(`crates/hod_asm/src/module.rs`). Specs: `spec/asm.md` §15, `spec/eh.md`
§4.2 and §4.3, `spec/codegen.md` §9.3. Tests:
`crates/hod_x64/tests/inline_asm_cfi.rs`, `crates/hod_eh/tests/encoder.rs`,
`crates/hod_asm/tests/census_x64.rs`, `crates/hod_cg/tests/emit_module_asm.rs`,
`crates/hod_cg/tests/emit_asm.rs`.

## Where the templates come from

The census's 15 templates with call-frame directives are `valgrind.h`'s
`CALL_FN_*` macros on amd64: they save `rbp`, point it at the CFA with
`.cfi_remember_state` and `.cfi_def_cfa rbp, 0`, call a function without
redirection, and restore both with `.cfi_restore_state`. No module of
either corpus has such a template, on either target.

## Evidence

- `census_x64.rs`: 212 of 220 templates assembled (197 at the base); 92
  bodies equal to `llvm-mc` 22 and 19.
- `inline_asm_cfi.rs`:
  - `.eh_frame` and `.debug_frame` instructions inside the template
    (`llvm-dwarfdump`), hod plain and stressed against `llc` 19 and 22,
    for valgrind's shape, every directive and nested pairs: 24
    comparisons equal.
  - A C++ exception thrown under the template is caught by the caller,
    hod plain and stressed, every linker, dynamic and static: 12 runs;
    without the rows the program aborts.
  - `gdb` (qemu's stub) stopped in the callee walks back to `main`;
    without the rows it does not.
- `emit_module_asm.rs`: the AArch64 `symver` case equals `llc` 19 and 22
  (sections, symbols as sets, relocations).
- Every 10th corpus entry, `--strict`, PIC, base `84600c0c` and head
  each built in its own target directory: x86-64 646 entries, 605
  identical objects, 41 malformed input at both; AArch64 646 entries,
  608 identical, 38 malformed input at both. The same counts at the
  `.symver` head (`a0d87ce8`): no sampled object changes.

## Follow-ups

- `asm goto` with a template on x86-64 (a `callbr` whose template is
  not empty, 2 corpus modules: `dcl.asm/asm-goto.cpp` of LLVM 19 and
  22) stays refused as `x64:inline-asm` (`spec/asm.md` §14's table).
  It is WP-212's second part, landed as WP-254
  (`spec/landed/codegen/WP-254.md`).

- `llvm.eh.dwarf.cfa` (`__builtin_dwarf_cfa()`, the CFA input of
  valgrind's macros) is not lowered (`intrinsic:llvm.eh.dwarf.cfa`), so
  real valgrind client code still refuses before its template.
- `.cfi_adjust_cfa_offset`, `.cfi_rel_offset`, `.cfi_escape` stay
  refused by name (§15).
