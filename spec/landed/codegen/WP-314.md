# WP-314: i386 exceptions (design 0008 I10, part 1 of 2)

Part 1 (exceptions) landed; part 2 (debug information,
`x86:debug-info`) is not started: `PARKED.md` at the branch root lists
what remains.

## What changed

- `hod_eh`: `CfiTarget.arch` became `machine: Machine`; new fields
  `address_size` and `abs32_reloc`; the `I386` table and
  `for_machine(machine, large)` (`spec/eh.md` §2.1). The `DW.ref` slot,
  the type-info stubs and `.debug_frame`'s locations and ranges are
  `address_size` bytes. The 64-bit tables keep 8 and the relocations
  they had, so their output is unchanged (compare-binaries below).
- `hod_cg/src/emit/unwind.rs`: the early return for `EM_386` removed;
  the FDE table chosen by machine.
- `hod_x86/src/eh.rs` (new), `call.rs` (the `x86:eh` refusal of
  `invoke` and of calls that may unwind removed; `llvm.eh.typeid.for`
  arm), `target.rs` (landing pad, resume, the pad value's
  `extractvalue`/`insertvalue`). `spec/codegen-x86.md` §12.

## Decisions

- PIC encodings in every model, as on x86-64 (`llc`'s static ones are
  absolute and make a PIE text-relocated; measured with `llc` 19 and 22).
- The landing pad value is class `Mem` and its two fields are handled in
  `eh.rs`; other aggregates stay `x86:aggregate` (I11a's).
- The decoded `.eh_frame` comparison with `llc` covers the CIEs, FDE and
  LSDA counts, the prologue rules per FDE without `ebx`/`esi`/`edi`
  (hod saves all three always), and the relocation kinds; after the
  first exit `llc` writes one exit per `ret` and hod one per function,
  and a module without `"frame-pointer"="all"` has an `esp`-based CFA in
  `llc`, so those parts are not compared. The LSDAs are compared by
  running the programs, not byte by byte.
