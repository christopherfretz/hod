# WP-192 — text and bitcode objects with debug information

Landed: the file order of a unit's line table is the order of the
files' contents, not of their `DiId`s (`spec/dwarf.md` §4.4, §5.7;
`crates/hod_dwarf/src/die.rs`, `UnitPlan::build`). The rule that no
consumer lets a metadata id's value or order into its output is in
`spec/bc.md` §9.2 and `spec/ir.md` §12.
`crates/hod/tests/text_bitcode_debug.rs` is the test. Neither reader
was wrong. The record lives under `dwarf/` because the fault was in
`hod_dwarf`.

## What was wrong

WP-189 found that two kiln-built abseil modules at `-O0 -g`
(`statusor_test.cc`, `numbers_test.cc`) gave one object from their
text and another from its `llvm-as-22` bitcode. kiln writes LLVM 22
text, so this is not WP-172's upgrade by a newer reader: the module
is the same. The objects differed only in the order of
`.debug_line`'s directories and files, and so in the file indices of
its rows and of every `DW_AT_decl_file`. On numbers_test `.debug_info`
was 9 bytes shorter from the bitcode, because more indices were below
128 in ULEB128. The DIE trees with file names resolved were equal.

`UnitPlan::build` added every `DiFile` to the `FileTable` in `DiId`
order, and a `DiId` is the producer's numbering. `hod_ll` numbers nodes
as the text writes them: kiln's `!N`. `hod_bc` numbers them as
`llvm-as` enumerated them: strings, then distinct nodes, then uniqued
nodes, in the order the writer reached them. The bitcode does not keep
the text's numbers, so a reader cannot get them back. Neither of hod's
two orders was `llc`'s. On numbers_test, `llc` 22 writes yet a third
order (first use), and the same one for both inputs.

## The seed

The module in `text_bitcode_debug.rs` is kiln-shaped. Its text numbers
`/usr/include/b.h` before `a.h`, and the global's header
(`src/counter.h`, relative, in the compilation directory) last.
`llvm-as` reaches them in the opposite order. It covers the three shapes
of §4.4: absolute with no directory, relative in the compilation
directory, and in another directory. It also has a struct type and a
local variable for `DW_AT_decl_file`. Before the fix, the text and the
bitcode gave different objects: `21bb9aa5…` and `cea33369…` on x86-64
for a 3-file cut of it.

The test builds the seed from text and from `llvm-as-22` and
`llvm-as-19` bitcode, for x86-64 and AArch64 and for DWARF 5 and 4, and
compares the bytes (8 pairs). It also checks, with `llvm-dis`, that the
bitcode numbers the files in another order than the text, so the seed
exercises the case.

Mutation: sorting the files back into id order makes the test fail at
the first pair (x86-64, DWARF 5, `llvm-as` 22). The mutation was then
reverted.

## Identity run

Every `.ll` of both corpora, plus the 14 `-g` modules the lanes saved
under `findings/oss/`, was built with release `hod --strict`, using the
manifest's `llc` arguments (the saved command's arguments for the
findings). Each module was built from its text and from its own
version's `llvm-as` bitcode (LLVM 22 for the findings), and the md5 of
the object, or the error, was compared.

| set | modules | differ before | differ after | same (built or refused alike) | no bitcode |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| AArch64 corpus | 6,454 | 4 | 0 | 6,106 | 348 |
| x86-64 corpus | 6,450 | 4 | 0 | 6,070 | 380 |
| real-project `-g` modules | 14 | 3 | 0 | 14 | 0 |

The corpus modules that differed were kiln's
`expr.reflect/debuginfo-run.cpp` and
`meta.reflection.define.aggregate/define-debuginfo-run.cpp`, at LLVM 19
and 22 on each target. The findings that differed were abseil
numbers_test, statusor_test, and one `attr:disable-tail-calls` module.

Only those 11 modules' objects changed between the two builds. Every
other object, debug or not, is byte-identical before and after. The
changed objects verify clean (`llvm-dwarfdump-22 --verify`). Their
multisets of resolved `DW_AT_decl_file` names are equal to the old
objects'.
