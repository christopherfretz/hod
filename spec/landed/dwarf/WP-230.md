# WP-230 — value records of optimized modules, second part

Spec: `spec/dwarf.md` §6.32 (and the edits to §6.22, §6.30, the
refusal table, and `spec/codegen.md` §9). No new abbreviation family.

## What was built

1. **`DIArgList` value records** (`hod_cg` `dbg_value.rs`, `hod_dwarf`
   `loclists.rs`). A record whose values are a `DIArgList`, or whose
   expression names its one value by `DW_OP_LLVM_arg`, is a row
   `ValueAt::Args(n)` followed by `n` rows `VarPlace::ValueArg`; the
   entry is the expression with each `DW_OP_LLVM_arg k` replaced by
   value `k`'s push, as `llc`'s SelectionDAG writes it, or no entry
   where a value has no place or a narrow value would meet an
   operation whose low bits depend on high bits. With it, the
   constants of every value record follow `llc`: sign-extended to 64
   bits and written by the variable's type (`DW_OP_consts` for a
   signed type, `emitConstu` for any other, so the expression's own
   `DW_OP_constu 1` is now `DW_OP_lit1`, and all ones is
   `DW_OP_lit0, DW_OP_not`).
2. **Declarations of a constant address** (`hod_cg` `driver.rs`
   `declares_constant`): `null`, `@G` and a constant expression on
   `@G` have no row, as `undef` and `poison` (no location in `llc` 19
   and 22, all selectors, both targets).
3. **Declarations of a slot in fragments** (`hod_dwarf` `func.rs`
   `slot_place`, `LocalWhat::Pieces`, `UnitPlan::frame_pieces`): one
   `exprloc` of `DW_OP_fbreg o, DW_OP_piece n` by fragment offset,
   gaps as empty pieces, nothing after the last; value records beside
   a slot declaration are not used (`llc`'s frame entities are
   `Processed` first). This replaces `debug:variable-twice` for that
   shape.

## Measurement against `llc`

Hand-written modules, `llc` 19 and 22, both targets:

- Argument lists: SelectionDAG pushes each value (`DW_OP_breg SP+o,
  DW_OP_deref`, also for narrow values); dead or unspilled values,
  `poison`, `undef` and globals drop the entry. FastISel and GlobalISel
  drop every `DIArgList`.
- Constant addresses: `null`, `poison`, `undef`, `@G` and `@G` with an
  expression get no location in any selector.
- Fragments on slots: the pieces of §6.32's rule in all three
  selectors; value records beside a slot are ignored, with or without
  a fragment; a `poison` fragment is dropped and its value records form
  the list.

## Byte identity

Base ae651a56 against head 6c350611, every 10th corpus entry
(`scripts/corpus-bytes.py`, `-j 4`, over sampled manifests of
`.build/corpus` and `.build/corpus-x64`): AArch64 669 modules, 669
objects identical; x86-64 684 modules, 684 objects identical; 0 differ,
0 declines on either side. The corpus is `-O0` input: its variables are
declared on slots, so none of the sampled modules has a value record
with a constant, an argument list, a declaration of a constant address
or a slot fragment, and the change of constant encoding (item 1) does
not reach it.

## Still refused

- `debug:dbg-value-arglist`: an expression that is not a computed value
  (no final `DW_OP_stack_value`), or one naming a value the list lacks.
- `debug:dbg-value-expr`: an operation §6.31 refuses (in the census,
  conversions to 128 bits).
- `debug:declare-address`: an address the policy keeps in a register.
- `debug:fragment`: a fragment of bits, a fragment after other
  operations, a fragment beside a whole declaration of the variable,
  overlapping fragments, a fragment on a declared value (§6.30) or on a
  coroutine variable.
