# WP-228 — uniqued metadata in the readers

Landed: `DebugInfo::unique_nodes` (`crates/hod_ir/src/debug/unique.rs`),
the rule in `spec/ir.md` §6.3, its calls in `spec/ll.md` §7.4 (end of
`build_debug`) and `spec/bc.md` §6.2 (after the module's nodes, and after
each function's local block from a mark); `InternTable::reserve`.
Tests: the pass's unit tests in `unique.rs`,
`crates/hod_ll/tests/uniquing.rs`, `crates/hod_bc/tests/uniquing.rs`
(with `tests/data/uniquing-llvm{19,22}.bc`) and
`crates/hod_a64/tests/dies_uniquing.rs`.

## What was wrong

A reader reserves every numbered node before it reads any, so equal
nodes got two ids: `add`'s hash-consing never saw a reserved node. kiln++
writes a coroutine frame's `void *` as a second
`!DIDerivedType(tag: DW_TAG_pointer_type, baseType: null, size: 64)`
beside the first; LLVM's parser makes the two one node, so `llc` writes
one `void *` DIE and hod wrote two.

## What LLVM does (shown with `llvm-as | llvm-dis`, 19 and 22 alike)

- Plain duplicates are one node; duplicates that name duplicates are one
  node too, forward references included (both ints, then both
  pointers).
- A node that names itself is printed `distinct` (`!6 = !{!6}`); a node
  with the same content that names it is not merged with it.
- Two cycles that are alike and share no node stay apart (least
  congruence: LLVM compares operands by pointer).
- Inside one cycle merging repeats: `!0 = !{!1, !3}`, `!1 = !{!0, !2}`,
  `!2 = !{!0}`, `!3 = !{!0, !4}`, `!4 = !{!0}` prints `!0 = !{!1, !1}`.
- `distinct` nodes are never merged; two `distinct` composites with one
  `identifier` are two structure DIEs in `llc-19` and `llc-22`'s objects
  (no ODR uniquing).
- The bitcode reader does the same: two BASIC_TYPE records with the
  distinct bit cleared, and the two pointer records naming them, read
  as one `DIBasicType` and one pointer.

## Evidence

- Without debug information, every 10th text module of each corpus,
  base (main 686ff25b) against head, release `hod` with each entry's
  llc arguments: AArch64 671 modules (670 without debug information, 1
  with), all objects identical; x86-64 680 (679 and 1), all identical.
- DIE trees (`cargo xtask debug trees -j 4 -v`) over both corpora's 28
  `-g` modules and the 8 saved kiln++ modules of
  `.build/findings/libcxx-debug`: base 22 same, 6 permitted, 1 differs,
  5 declined, 2 failed; head 22 same, 7 permitted, 0 differ, 5 declined,
  2 failed. The one change is `trees-coroutine/fullexpr-dtor.ll`
  (WP-221's module), DIFFERS to PERMITTED (`realigned frame`): base 21
  pointer-type DIEs, head 20, `llc-22` 20. `debug verify` over the same
  modules: equal reports (17 built and clean, 3 declined, 2 failures, 14
  skipped, both).
- Cost, callgrind instructions of release `hod --strict -O0 -g`-style
  builds of four saved real modules (base, head, change): glog 4 MB
  421.4M, 428.1M, +1.59%; abseil 4 MB 398.3M, 405.6M, +1.82%; protobuf
  5 MB 445.1M, 455.4M, +2.30%; protobuf 8 MB 754.5M, 766.6M, +1.60%. None
  of these modules merges anything; the cost is the walk, the hashes,
  the strongly-connected-components walk and entering every node in
  `add`'s table. Wall times, best of 5, on a shared host whose
  run-to-run noise is larger than that (up to ±10% between equal
  binaries) show no change outside the noise.
