# WP-202 — x86 vector intrinsics past SSSE3

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/x86/ext.rs` (new; reached from
`x86::intrinsic` when neither §5.6.22's table nor `sys` has a row; its
rows generated into one `match`), `x86.rs`'s `Level::Avx` and its
`cmp.ss`/`cmp.sd` with AVX, `hod_ir`'s 173 `Intrinsic` members (a block
of their own after WP-190's; their immediates in `immediate_args`),
their matrix rows at the end of `INTRINSIC_X86` (`never(X64_ONLY)`,
`on(X86_SSE)`; `crates/hod_ir/src/classify/matrix.rs`), their signatures
in `hod_ir/tests/body_llvm.rs`, `docs/status/support.md` (generated),
the encoders in `crates/hod_mc_x64/src/sse4.rs` and `avx.rs`
(`spec/mc-x64.md`), `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.33. Tests:
`crates/hod_x64/tests/x86_ext.rs`, `crates/hod_mc_x64/tests/l1/sse4.rs`
and `l1/avx.rs`.

The findings: six simdjson Haswell modules refused on SSE4.2, PCLMUL and
AVX2 names; the highway AVX2 families (`avx.addsub.*.256`,
`*.pblendvb`, `avx2.pavg.*`, `avx2.psrai`/`psrli`/`pslli`,
`vgf2p8affineqb.128`/`256`).

With the header bodies inlined into their callers (`opt
-passes=always-inline,globaldce`) and `aes`, `pclmul`, `sha` taken out
of the feature strings (see below), `hod --strict` builds every function
of `smmintrin`, `avxintrin`, `avx2intrin` (but the gathers), `fmaintrin`
and `f16cintrin`, at 19 and 22: no generic 256-bit IR shape is refused.

## What is built and what is refused

- Built where the function's `target-features` enable the feature (the
  last item naming it decides; `avx2` implies `avx` implies `sse4.2`
  implies `sse4.1`; `fma` and `f16c` imply `avx`; GFNI's 256-bit forms
  need `avx`), refused by the intrinsic's name otherwise.
- 128-bit values in `xmm` registers; 256-bit values stay in frame slots
  and pass through `ymm14`/`ymm15` (no design change). Under stress no
  lowering pins three vector registers.
- Refused by name: the 16 AVX2 gathers (`avx2.gather.{d,q}.{d,q,ps,pd}`
  and their `.256`), every AVX-512 name, VAES and VPCLMULQDQ.
- Not this package's: `features.rs` does not know `aes`, `pclmul`,
  `sha`, `gfni`, so a module naming them is `attr:target-feature-unknown`
  at the `hod` command; three of the simdjson modules pass and return
  `__m256i` by value (`x64:wide-vector-abi`, §5.6.2).

## Found on the way

WP-173's `cmp.ss`/`cmp.sd` (`x86.rs` `Op::Cmp`) wrote `cmpss` with the
predicate's low 3 bits whatever the features; with AVX, `llc` writes
`vcmpss` with all 5. Fixed here: predicates 8–31 with AVX are `vcmpss`/
`vcmpsd`.

## Cost on the bench inputs

None measurable: the bench inputs call no `llvm.x86.*` intrinsic; x86
calls pass one more `Option` before `ext`'s `match`.
