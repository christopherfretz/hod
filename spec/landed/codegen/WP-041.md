# WP-041 — the AArch64 intrinsic group

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/intrinsic.rs`, `crates/hod_a64/tests/intrinsic.rs`,
`crates/hod_a64/tests/common/intrinsic_gen.rs`,
`crates/hod_a64/tests/common/intrinsic_mem.rs`, the heading
"Intrinsics (WP-041)" in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1, the `WP-041` line of
`tests/common/groups.rs`, and the support-matrix rows.

## What is lowered

- `ctlz`, `cttz`, `ctpop`, `bswap` (widths that are multiples of 16),
  `bitreverse`, `abs`, `smax`, `smin`, `umax`, `umin`, `fshl`, `fshr`:
  every width from `i1` to `i64`, constant and variable operands.
- `memcpy`, `memmove`, `memset` with any length type: inline up to 64
  bytes (`memmove`: 32), otherwise a call of `memcpy`/`memmove`/`memset`.
  `memcpy.inline` and `memset.inline` with a constant length of any size:
  inline, a 16-byte loop above 64. Volatile flags of both values.
- `trap`, `debugtrap`, `ubsantrap`.
- `frameaddress(n)` and `returnaddress(n)` for `n` up to 256.
- `objectsize`, `is.constant`, `prefetch`, `ptrmask` (scalar).

The thresholds and sequences are in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1. No runtime
function was added: `memcpy`, `memmove` and `memset` were already in
`hod_cg::RuntimeFn`. No helper was added to `common.rs`.

## What is refused, by name

- `a64:intrinsic-wide`: an integer intrinsic of `i128` or wider.
- `a64:intrinsic-vector`: an integer intrinsic or `ptrmask` of vectors.
- `a64:intrinsic`: `bswap` of a width that is not a multiple of 16, and
  every intrinsic this group does not lower: floating-point math,
  `*.sat`, `scmp`/`ucmp`, `clmul`, `clear_cache`, `readcyclecounter`,
  `load.relative`, `instrprof.*`, reductions, coroutines.
- `a64:mem-inline-length`: `memcpy.inline`/`memset.inline` with a
  variable length.
- `a64:frame-depth`: `frameaddress`/`returnaddress` deeper than 256.

## Evidence (`tests/intrinsic.rs`)

- `every_integer_intrinsic_at_every_width_agrees`: 868 functions, 19,258
  calls. Every integer intrinsic at 18 widths (1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9, 15, 16,
  17, 24, 31, 32, 33, 40, 48, 56, 63, 64), constant and variable
  operands, both poison flags, rotates, garbage above the width. Compiled
  plain and under `Stress::ALL`, and by `llc -O0`; linked with GNU ld,
  lld and mold; the results equal `hod_interp`'s and llc's.
- `random_intrinsic_functions_agree`: 1,200 random chains, 7,200 calls
  (`HOD_INTRINSIC_FUNCTIONS` sets the count).
- `memory_intrinsics_agree_with_the_interpreter`: 900 functions of one
  to four memory intrinsics on a 256-byte buffer that ends at a guard
  page, with a 64-byte sentinel never written, lengths around every
  threshold, `i64` and `i32` variable lengths with garbage above 32
  bits, overlapping `memmove` both ways, and eight integer and three
  `double` values live across the calls. Plain, stress, PIC and llc
  builds; the buffer and the result equal the interpreter's.
- `the_native_forms_hold`: what the interpreter does not run.
  `frameaddress`/`returnaddress` at depths 0 and 1 against the C
  caller's `__builtin_frame_address`/`__builtin_return_address`; each
  trap kind raises `SIGTRAP`; `objectsize`, `is.constant`, `ptrmask`.
  Same three builds, every linker.
- `short_forms_are_llcs`: the trap encodings and the frame walk are
  llc's instruction words.
- `what_is_not_lowered_is_refused_by_name`: each name above.
- The differential harness with the `WP-041` line: 418 of 1,000
  programs agree (`many_programs_agree`), none disagree; the skips are
  `a64:overflow-intrinsic` 180 (WP-040), `a64:store-fpr` 276 and
  `a64:load-fpr` 39 (the float hooks in `mem`), and `a64:intrinsic` 87
  (the `*.sat` intrinsics the generator emits; unplanned in the matrix).

`is.constant(i32 5)` is true, as llc gives; it was first lowered as
always-false, which LangRef allows, and changed to match llc.

## Mutations (each caught, then reverted)

1. No `orr` before `clz` in `ctlz`: 46 calls differ.
2. Funnel amount `and #(2N-1)` instead of `#(N-1)`: 198 calls differ.
3. A narrow variable length moved with `mov x` (not zero-extended): the
   memory test faults on the guard page.
4. An inline `memmove` store placed before its second load: the
   overlapping cases differ.

## Matrix

Flipped on AArch64: `memcpy`, `memcpy.inline`, `memmove`, `memset`,
`memset.inline`, `frameaddress`, `returnaddress`, `prefetch`,
`objectsize`, `ptrmask` for `ptr` and `int`; `ubsantrap` and the twelve
integer intrinsics for `int`; `trap`, `debugtrap`, `is.constant` for
every class. Eight of them (`memcpy.inline`, `memset.inline`,
`debugtrap`, `ubsantrap`, `prefetch`, `is.constant`, `objectsize`,
`ptrmask`) were LATER rather than WP-041 in the matrix; they are in this
group's brief and implemented, so they are flipped.

## Follow-ups

- `*.sat`, `scmp`/`ucmp` and the floating-point intrinsics have no owner
  in the matrix (LATER); the generator's `intrinsics` switch emits
  `*.sat`, so those programs are skipped until a package takes them.
- `i128` integer intrinsics (`a64:intrinsic-wide`) need the `Mem`-class
  lowering of WP-040.
