# WP-188: system registers in AArch64 inline assembly; the always-inliner's per-call-site decision

## Part A: `mrs`/`msr` of system registers (`spec/asm.md` §5.4)

- `crates/hod_asm/src/a64/sysreg.rs`: the table `TABLE` (55 names),
  generated once from `llvm-mc-22`: the names it prints, with no
  feature, for the ID-register space (op0 3, op1 0, CRn 0, CRm 0..7) and
  `cntfrq_el0`, `cntvct_el0`, `cntpct_el0`, `tpidr_el0`, `tpidrro_el0`,
  `fpcr`, `fpsr`, `nzcv`, `daif`, `dczid_el0`, `ctr_el0`, `currentel`,
  `rndr`, `rndrrs`, each with the directions `llvm-mc` accepts; the
  generic form `S<op0>_<op1>_C<n>_C<m>_<op2>` with `llvm-mc`'s ranges
  (op0 0..3, no leading zeros). Names are case-insensitive. The words are
  built in `hod_asm` (`0xd520_0000`/`0xd500_0000` with the field at bit
  5): `hod_mc_a64::SysReg` names five registers and is no longer used by
  the template assembler.
- `Features` gains `rand` (`+rand`): `rndr`/`rndrrs` without it are
  `asm:feature`; `+v8.5a` does not imply it in `llvm-mc-22`, so not here
  either.
- Refused by name, `asm:operand/sysreg`: any other name, `msr` to a
  read-only register (`cntvct_el0`, `midr_el1`, `rndr`, ...), the names
  `llvm-mc-22` gates behind other features (`id_aa64zfr0_el1`,
  `id_aa64smfr0_el1`, `id_isar6_el1`, `id_pfr2_el1`, `tpidr2_el0`,
  `tco`, `dit`, `ssbs`, ...: their generic form is accepted), and `msr`
  of an immediate to a PSTATE field (`daifset`, `spsel`). The refusal
  carries no detail: `hod_asm::Refusal` is one static name and the
  pipeline into `hod::Unsupported::detail` has no slot for it
  (follow-up).

Evidence:
- `hod_asm/tests/sysreg.rs`: the table regenerated from `llvm-mc-22`
  equals `TABLE` (55 rows); 156 bodies (every row in each allowed
  direction, upper and lower case, `xzr`; the generic form at four
  corner points for each op0) equal `llvm-mc-22`'s words; 16 refusals.
- `hod_a64/tests/sysreg_asm.rs`: 12 functions (`cntfrq_el0` by name and
  generic, `cntvct_el0` monotonic, `tpidr_el0` equals
  `__builtin_thread_pointer()`, `midr_el1`, `id_aa64isar0_el1`,
  `id_aa64pfr0_el1`, `ctr_el0`, `dczid_el0`, an `fpcr` write/read round
  trip and restore, `nzcv` and `fpsr` writes) built by hod and by
  `llc -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` 19 and 22, linked with a C
  driver that compares each with its own `mrs`, run under every linker:
  `checked, 0 failures` for all three builds; the 17 system-register
  words of hod's code equal `llc`'s with the general register masked
  (the allocators choose different registers).
- abseil (`unscaledcycleclock.cc`, `crc/cpu_detect.cc`, kiln-built
  modules): `hod --strict` with their saved `llc` arguments compiles
  both; text and bitcode objects are byte-identical; the asm words are
  `d53be009 mrs x9, cntfrq_el0` and `d5380009 mrs x9, midr_el1` where
  `llc-22` writes the same instruction with `x8`.
- Mutation: op2 shifted one bit in `sysreg::field` fails both table
  tests; reverted.

## Part B: `alwaysinline:return-address` (`spec/cli.md` "Always-inline")

What `opt -passes=always-inline` does, 19 and 22 alike (checked on small
modules):
- it inlines a direct call when the callee is `alwaysinline`, OR the
  call site carries `alwaysinline`, even into a `noinline` callee; a
  call-site `noinline` (in place or in a group, alone or with others)
  keeps the call;
- `llvm.returnaddress`, `llvm.frameaddress` (any depth),
  `llvm.addressofreturnaddress` and `llvm.sponentry` are copied
  unchanged into the caller, so they mean the caller's frame;
- it does not inline variadic callees (with or without `va_start`),
  recursive ones, nor bodies with a `returns_twice` call, `indirectbr`
  or `llvm.localescape`; a `musttail` call in the callee becomes a
  plain call.

Both abseil refusals (`TestWithReturnAddress` in `symbolize_test.cc`,
`CaptureBacktraceNoInline` in `stacktrace_test.cc`) were `noinline
optnone` bodies with no `alwaysinline` call site: `opt` inlines neither.
hod declined because the readers dropped call-site attributes, so any
`alwaysinline` set in the module made every direct call suspect.

Implemented:
- `hod_ir`: call-site bits `CALL_ALWAYS_INLINE`, `CALL_NOINLINE`
  (`CallData::flags` bits 6, 7), `CallSite::is_always_inline`,
  `is_noinline`, `CallDesc::{always_inline, noinline}`;
  `kit::CALL_SITE_FLAGS` holds `FnAttrs::ALWAYS_INLINE` and
  `kit::CALL_SITE_NOINLINE` (bit 31, checked at compile time to be no
  `FnAttrs` flag); the verifier refuses both on one call (LLVM does);
  the printer writes them; the random generator makes them (never
  both). No random seed test moved.
- `hod_ll`: `noinline` in place on a call, or in a group
  (`AttrGroup::noinline`, set in `define_groups`). `hod_bc`: a call-site
  group's kind 14.
- `inlining.rs`: `inlined(site, callee)` is `opt`'s rule; a body's
  reason counts only when `inline_viable` (the bodies above); a direct
  call declines only when inlined; an `available_externally` copy is
  judged at its first inlined call. `may_have_inlined` is gone.
  `alwaysinline:returns-twice` is no longer reported (LLVM keeps the
  call); the name stays in `inline_reason::ALL`.

Kept: a call that LLVM does inline, to a body with one of the reasons,
still declines (hod does not inline; the depth+1 copy that would build
`opt`'s value needs the copy routing of `hod_cg/src/emit/copy.rs`, a
follow-up).

Evidence:
- `crates/hod/tests/inlining.rs` `the_decision_is_opt_s_per_call_site`:
  13 modules, each from text and from the bitcode of `llvm-as-19` and
  `llvm-as-22` (same outcome, same object bytes): the call-site rules
  above, a callee called once kept and once inlined (declined), the
  bodies LLVM cannot inline (accepted). The `returns_twice` rows of
  `bodies_that_need_inlining_are_declined_by_name` now compile.
- `tests/ir/alwaysinline_kept_calls.ll` (both targets, LLVM 19 and 22
  through `irdiff_real`): a `noinline` callee at a plain site, an
  `alwaysinline` callee at a `noinline` site, variadic and recursive
  callees, each printing whether its return address lies in its caller
  (`in-caller`), a frame address that is the callee's own, beside an
  inlined `alwaysinline` helper. `opt -passes=always-inline` then
  `llc -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` (19 and 22, AArch64) prints the
  same lines.
- abseil `symbolize_test.cc` and `stacktrace_test.cc` (kiln-built):
  `hod --strict` compiles both (0.15 s and 0.06 s, 25 MB and 10 MB peak);
  text and `llvm-as-22` bitcode objects are byte-identical. The lane's
  build trees were cleaned, so abseil's two tests were not relinked; the
  seed is the standalone reproducer.
- Mutation: `inlined` ignoring the call-site `noinline` fails
  `the_decision_is_opt_s_per_call_site`; reverted.
