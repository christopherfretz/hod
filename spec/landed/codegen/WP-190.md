# WP-190 — x86 system and state intrinsics

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/x86/sys.rs` (new; reached from
`x86::intrinsic` when §5.6.22's table has no row), `hod_ir`'s 67
`Intrinsic` members (`crates/hod_ir/src/intrinsic.rs`, a block of its own
after WP-173's; `xabort`'s operand an immediate argument), their matrix
rows at the end of `INTRINSIC_X86` (`never(X64_ONLY)`, `on(X86_SYS)`;
`crates/hod_ir/src/classify/matrix.rs`), their signatures in
`hod_ir/tests/body_llvm.rs`, `docs/status/support.md`, the encodings
in `crates/hod_mc_x64/src/state.rs` (`spec/mc-x64.md` "Processor state
and extensions"), `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.29 (a new section after
§5.6.22). Tests: `crates/hod_x64/tests/x86_intrinsics.rs`,
`crates/hod_mc_x64/tests/l1/state.rs`.

The finding: the LLVM x86-64 build's `llvm-exegesis/lib/X86/Target.cpp`
was refused on `intrinsic:llvm.x86.fxsave64` (and `fxrstor64`,
`flags.read.u64`, `flags.write.u64`).

`aand`, `aor`, `axor`), `cldemote`, `cet` (`clrssbsy`, `rstorssp`,
`saveprevssp`, `setssbsy`, `wrss*`, `wruss*`), `uintr` (`clui`, `stui`,

`movdir64b`), `enqcmd` (`enqcmd`, `enqcmds`), `invpcid`, `lwp`
(`llwpcb`, `slwpcb`, `lwpins*`, `lwpval*`), `mwaitx` (`monitorx`,
`mwaitx`), `ptwrite`, `rdpru`, `tbm` (`bextri`, clang 19), `waitpkg`

`xsusldtrk`). None is in the LLVM x86-64 build.

## Evidence

- `system_intrinsics_agree_with_llc`: 36 deterministic functions
  (`addcarry`/`subborrow`, `bextr`, `bzhi`, `pdep`, `pext`, `crc32`,
  `rdssp`, each also with one value for every operand of a type), 32
  inputs each from edge values (0, 1, all-ones, sign bits, alternating
  bits, `bextr` controls), plus the state round trips and the shape
  checks: 1178 lines, equal on hod plain (bfd, lld), hod stressed, `llc
  -O0 -fast-isel=0` 22 and 19, every function with every feature of
  §5.6.29, run under `qemu-x86_64` on `qemu64` plus `sse3`, `ssse3`,
  `sse4.1`, `sse4.2`, `rdtscp`, `xsave`, `xsaveopt`, `rdrand`, `rdseed`,
  `clflushopt`, `clwb`, `bmi1`, `bmi2`, `fsgsbase`, `rdpid`.
  - The flags round trip: `(x & 0x8d5) | 0x202` written and read back
    (`& 0xfff`) for nine values.
  - The state: for each of `fxsave`/`fxrstor`, the `64` forms,
    `xsave`/`xrstor`, `xsaveopt`/`xrstor` and the `64` forms, an image
    saved (its MXCSR `stmxcsr`'s and its control word `fnstcw`'s),
    altered (control word `0x0f7f`, MXCSR `0x7f80`), restored (both in
    force), saved again (both in the image), the original restored
    (`037f`, `1f80` again). `xgetbv(0)` is 3.
  - The shapes: `rdtsc` and `rdtscp` do not go back, and `rdtscp`'s
    `TSC_AUX` is the same twice; `rdfsbase` (64 and 32) is `%fs:0`;
    `wrgsbase`/`rdgsbase` round trip (64, and 32 zero-extended) and the
    old base is restored; each `rdrand`/`rdseed` width delivers with the
    flag field 1 and not one value four times; `rdpid`, `clflushopt`,
    `clwb` run.
  - What QEMU 10 implements in user mode: the above. It traps `rdpmc`
    (SIGSEGV), `xsavec`, `xsaves`, `rdpkru`, `serialize`, `xtest` and the
    RTM ones, `incssp` (SIGILL); `rdssp` is a `nop`. Those are compiled
    by every leg and hod's code holds each one's instruction
    (`llvm-objdump`).
- `system_intrinsics_only_with_their_feature`: each of the 58 gated ones is refused
  by its own name without its feature (no features; the baseline's),
  and with `+f,-f`; built with it, and then hod's code holds its
  instruction and no other of §5.6.29's (the "only enabled features"
  check); `xsave` built under `+xsaveopt`, `+xsavec`, `+xsaves`; those
  three refused under `+xsave` alone and after `-xsave`; `crc32` built
  under `+sse4.2` and `+avx2`, refused under `+sse4.1` and
  `+sse4.2,-crc32`; `bmi2`'s refused under `+bmi`. The nine baseline ones
  build without features.
- `system_rows_and_refusals_by_name`: x86-64 rows, AArch64 `never`; six
  names hod does not build (`wbinvd`, `clzero`, `rdpru`, `senduipi`,
  `tbm.bextri.u32`, `wrssd`) have no row, and `classify` refuses
  `wbinvd` by its name; `rdtsc` declared `i32` is
  `x64:x86-intrinsic-signature`.
- Encodings: `state_and_extensions_match_llvm` (`hod_mc_x64` L1)
  against `llvm-mc` 22: every `XState` form on the memory-operand set,
  `clflushopt`, `clwb`, `rdrand`/`rdseed` (16/32/64), the `fs`/`gs`
  base forms, `rdssp`/`incssp`, `rdpid`, `crc32` (each source size, 16
  registers), the BMI ones (32/64, operand permutations), the fixed
  ones, `xabort` immediates, `xbegin` displacements; the sizes without
  an encoding rejected.
- The finding: `Target.cpp.ll` under release-equivalent `hod --strict
  -O0 -filetype=obj -relocation-model=pic -function-sections
  -data-sections` no longer stops at `intrinsic:llvm.x86.fxsave64`; it
  stops at `call:inline-asm` (`asm sideeffect "fwait"` in
  `X86SavedState`'s destructor), outside this package. With that one
  call deleted it compiles (`fxsave64 (%r12)`, `pushfq`, `fxrstor64`,
  `popfq` in the code), and text and `llvm-as` 22 bitcode give the same
  object byte for byte. Its functions' features include `+fxsr`.

## Mutations

Each caught by `system_intrinsics_agree_with_llc`, then reverted:

- (a) the state instructions at `[p + 16]`: hod's program fails.
- (b) `addcarry`'s carry out by `setae`: 768 disagreements.
- (c) the BMI operands swapped: 387 disagreements.
- The encoder (`hod_mc_x64`): `crc32`'s word-and-wider opcode `F1` as
  `F2`: 484 disagreements against `llvm-mc`.
