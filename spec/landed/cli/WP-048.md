## Evidence

`cargo xtask corpus reasons --llc --before <hod at main>` over the
captured corpus (5,760 modules, 3 at a time; `docs/status/reasons.md`
is the command's output, regenerated with `cargo xtask corpus reasons
--llc --before <hod> --report docs/status/reasons.md`):

- **First refusal**: `module:pic` for every module of both majors (each
  is compiled `-relocation-model=pic` and carries the `PIC Level` flag).
  The census has 174 reasons; on every module: `module:pic`,
  `module:pie`, `module:uwtable`, `global:function-definition`, `cc:ccc`,
  `attr:uwtable`, `attr:target-cpu`, `attr:target-features`, `type:int`.
  The 12 modules with debug information give `debug:module-flags` and
  `debug-info` in the census, by all three routes alike.
- **Routes**: text, native bitcode and `llvm-dis` give the same outcome
  and first reason on 5,760 of 5,760 modules, and the same census as a
  set on 5,760. The census's *order* differs on 30 (14 of LLVM 22, 16 of
  LLVM 19, all with `blockaddress` or a global referenced before it is
  defined): `hod_ll` numbers a symbol at its first mention
  (`crates/hod_ll/src/globals.rs:67`), `hod_bc` in record order, and the
  census lists reasons in the order met. Native is the default.
- **Decline cost** (coverage-log field 7, so process start included;
  median, p99, worst, in µs; share of `llc -O0` on the same module):

  | | before (main) | after, text | after, native bitcode | `llvm-dis` route |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | LLVM 22 | 616 / 3,050 / 27,051 (2.1%) | 2,039 / 8,478 / 53,274 (6.8%) | 1,769 / 7,062 / 20,616 (5.9%) | 22,159 / 44,634 / 392,008 (74.6%) |
  | LLVM 19 | 704 / 3,178 / 29,425 (2.0%) | 2,269 / 8,934 / 74,334 (6.5%) | 2,067 / 7,066 / 16,163 (5.8%) | 26,541 / 64,505 / 519,687 (77.7%) |

  `llc -O0` median 29 ms (22), 32 ms (19); the `convert` of the
  `llvm-dis` route alone, median 20 ms and 24 ms. Host load about 19 on
  24 cores. Where the extra 1.4 ms comes from: in process, reading the module
  level of a typical 2 KiB kiln module takes 11 µs warm, and the first
  read of a process 0.3 to 2 ms; the new binary takes 10 major page
  faults per run against main's 2 (its reader code is paged in from the
  workspace's bind mount). Native bitcode makes a converted module's
  decline about 12 times cheaper than the `llvm-dis` route.
- **Benchmark** (`cargo xtask bench --runs 5`, `docs/status/perf.md`;
  text inputs, so no `convert`): every input now declines at
  `module:pic` after reading its module level. Decline, before then
  after (ms, a measured process's 3.8 ms start included): `small-c` 6.50
  then 7.63, `tiny-functions` 6.55 then 14.83, `stl-heavy-g` (16 MB)
  14.85 then 42.33, the `parse` phase 20.2 of it. `hod+fallback` over the
  sum of the inputs: 1.02x `llc` then 1.07x (`llc` itself 4.5 then 5.0 s
  in the two runs: a busier host).

## Decisions where the spec was silent

- **Verify** always runs, on a body `classify` accepted, right before the
  seam; never on a module refused earlier. Cost today: 0 (every module
  with a definition is refused in the module pass).
- **The cheap reason wins**; `HOD_CENSUS=1` gives every reason. In census
  mode, a body is classified and verified only while no reason has been
  found; after that only `classify`'s census names its constructs.
- **Reader limits** are `limit:reader`, not `malformed-input`: LLVM may
  take the module. `BcErrorKind::Unsupported(n)` is `bitcode:<n>`.
- **No triple and no `-mtriple`** is `unsupported-target` (the reader has
  no target to assume); before, the target check was skipped and the
  module went on to `codegen-not-implemented`.
- **`-mtriple` naming another target** than the module's is
  `unsupported-target` (no retargeting).
- **The bitcode target** in the log is the normalized triple of the
  target `hod_bc` made, not the one the module wrote.
- `Construct` lost `Copy` (it carries `Cow` names); the driver's compile
  function takes `Settings` and `Report`.
- The census is a new generated document, `docs/status/reasons.md`:
  `docs/status/support.md` is generated by `hod_ir` and checked byte for
  byte by `crates/hod_ir/tests/classify_support_doc.rs`.
