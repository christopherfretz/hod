## Causes

- **rlib format** (believed, from rustc's crate loading; no log of it
  survived): the dependency is found by listing `-L dependency=`; the
  bind mount listed the `.rmeta` (written seconds earlier) and not yet
  the `.rlib`. E0463 is the same with neither listed. The gate's steps
  are sequential and clippy's artifacts are named apart from test's, so
  a retry is the response.
- **minted tokens** (shown): the test's held children ran under the 60 s
  default limit while the test waited for the machine's pool (the log:
  "running for over 60 seconds", 72.7 s); they were killed, the client
  ended in the middle of a refill pass, which had counted idle 2 before
  and found no client after (the shell scan took 1.5 s per pass here,
  seconds at load), and refilled 2. The scan missed no live client. The
  real pool could mint the same way when its last client ends during a
  pass. `race.sh` (a private pool, a client ending at a random point of
  a pass): old script 9 of 30 passes minted, new 0 of 30.
- **stale fifo** (shown by the old fixed `assert_eq`, then a 10 s deadline):
  the bytes live while a child forked by another test thread holds the
  holder's descriptor until its exec.
- **bench, irdiff** (shown by the logs): wall-time margins of 100 to
  300 ms, and bounds of 30 to 40 s that included waits for the pool.

## Shared pool

No restart needed: the refill loop runs the main checkout's script each
pass, the guard lets the loop's own passes through (`$PPID` is the pid in
`<pool>.refill.pid`), and the deficit file keeps its format.
