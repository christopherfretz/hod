## Step A: what `llc` does (measured before code)

`llc` 19 and 22, `-O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0
-mtriple=i686-unknown-linux-gnu`, CPUs `pentium4` (SSE2) and `pentium3`
(SSE without SSE2). 19 and 22 write the same instructions for every
probe of this section (`diff` of the assembly without directives: 0
lines), except the `fp128` argument offsets (16-aligned in 22, 4 in 19:
design 0008 §2).

**Which values are SSE values.** With SSE, `float`; with SSE2, `double`
too. Arithmetic (`addss`/`addsd`, `subs`, `muls`, `divs`), `sqrt`
(`sqrts`), `fneg` (`pxor`/`xorps` of a sign mask from the constant
pool), `fabs` (`pand`), `copysign` (`pand`, `pand`, `por`), `fmuladd`
(`muls` then `adds`: two roundings, no FMA at these CPUs), `minnum` /
`maxnum` (inline: `cmpunords a, a`; `mins b, a`; the result `b` where
`a` is a NaN, else `b < a ? b : a`, so `minnum(+0, -0)` is `+0`, the
first operand), `fpext` / `fptrunc` between `float` and `double`
(`cvtss2sd`, `cvtsd2ss`), `sitofp` of at most 32 bits (`cvtsi2s`),
`fptosi` to at most 32 bits (`cvtts2si`, narrower results its low
bits), `fptoui` to `i32` (`x - 2^31` and `x` both truncated, the first
masked by the sign of the second and or-ed in), `uitofp` from `i32` (the
`2^52` or-trick in `double`, then `cvtsd2ss` for a `float`: one
rounding), `uitofp` from `i64` to `double` (`punpckldq` with `2^52` /
`2^84`, `subpd`, `unpckhpd`, `addsd`: one rounding), compares
(`ucomis`, `cmpeqs` / `cmpneqs` for `oeq` / `une` into an integer),
`select` (a `cmov` of the two addresses, a load), loads, stores.

**What stays on the x87 in an SSE function.** `x86_fp80` (every
operation, as without SSE); a conversion with `x86_fp80` on one side
(`fldt`; `fstpl` to memory; `movsd`: the 80-bit value rounded once);
`fptosi` / `fptoui` to `i64` (`movsd` to memory, `fldl`, the control
word around `fistpll`; unsigned: the `2^63` compare and subtraction in
SSE first); `sitofp` from `i64` (`fildll`, `fstps` / `fstpl`: one
rounding); `uitofp` from `i64` to `float` (`fildll`, `fadd` of 0 or
`2^64` from a two-entry table indexed by the sign, at 80 bits, `fstps`:
the 80-bit sum rounded to `float`, the x87 path's sequence);
returns (`float` / `double` in `st0`: `movss` / `movsd` to a stack
slot, `flds` / `fldl`); call results (`st0` stored at the type's width,
`fstpl`, then `movsd`: an SSE use reads the rounded value; but the
copy out of `st0` is an exact `fp_round`, which the DAG folds into an
`fpext` or `fptrunc` of the result: `fpext float (call float @f) to
double` is `fstpl` of the 80-bit register, found by the mixed test
when hod first printed `0x1p+0` where `llc` printed
`0x1.0000004p+0`); the libm
calls (`frem` = `fmod` / `fmodf`, `fma`, `floor`, `rint`, ... with
arguments on the stack, results in `st0`). Arguments are on the stack
as without SSE. A function with SSE2 calling one without (or back)
needs nothing: the convention of scalars does not depend on SSE.

**Precision.** An SSE operation rounds to its type at once, so the
80-bit copy rule of §11 does not apply to an SSE value: in an SSE2
function the only x87 values are `x86_fp80` values (which are their own
width) and a call result in `st0` before its store, which `llc` stores
at the type's width before any use. At `pentium3` `double` is an x87
type with the §11 rule, `float` an SSE one; `fpext float to double` is
`flds` of the `float` (exact); `fptrunc double to float` of a computed
`double` stores its 80-bit register with `fstps`: one rounding from 80
bits, where the `double` home would round twice.

**`fcmp`.** `ucomis lhs, rhs`, operands swapped for `olt`, `ole`,
`ugt`, `uge`; flags read as the x87 path reads `fucomi`'s (unordered
sets `ZF`, `PF`, `CF`): `ogt` `a`, `oge` `ae`, `one` `ne`, `ord` `np`,
`ueq` `e`, `ult` `b`, `ule` `be`, `uno` `p`; `oeq`, `une` by `cmpeqs` /
`cmpneqs` (the same truth values); `true` / `false` constants. A
`select` on an `fcmp` uses the flags (`cmova` of the addresses).

**Constants.** `llc` loads them from `.rodata.cst4/8/16` (`.LCPI`,
`@GOTOFF` under PIC). hod writes a constant's encoding into its frame
slot with `mov` immediates, as §11 does: the same bits, no relocation,
so the PIC case does not arise.

**Feature set.** Per function, as `llc`: its `"target-cpu"`, else the
command line's `-mcpu` (`X86::cpu`), else `generic`; then its
`"target-features"` (`X86Features::of_function`). `generic` and `i686`
have no SSE (clang's default `--target=i686-linux-gnu` writes
`"target-cpu"="i686"`: x87); `pentium3` has SSE, `pentium4` SSE2;
`+sse2` in `target-features` turns SSE2 on, `-sse` off (and SSE2 with
it).

**`half`, `bfloat`, `fp128`.** `half` with SSE2: arguments on the stack
(`pinsrw` from the slot), each operation through `__extendhfsf2`
(result in `st0`, stored with `fstps`), the `float` operation,
`__truncsfhf2` (result in `xmm0`); a `half` result is returned in
`xmm0`. `bfloat`: `shl $16` into a `float`, the operation, `__truncsfbf2`
(result in `xmm0`). `fp128`: every operation a libgcc call (`__addtf3`)
with a hidden result pointer and the 19 / 22 argument-alignment
difference: not trivial; stays refused (`x86:float:fp128`).

## Third session

of an operand before §11's copy (`crates/hod_x86/src/x87.rs`, body of

`half` with SSE2 (spec §12 "`half` with SSE2"): `X86` carries the
function's feature set (`target.rs`, `X86::features`,

`Target::load`/`store` of `xmm0` (`sse::load_xmm0`/`store_xmm0`);

before code: probes `h.ll` per CPU and `v.ll` (views, integer

`xmm0`, by an SSE2 `movd`, while `llc` without SSE2 reads `ax`: so
`half` without SSE2 stays `x86:float:half` (no oracle that runs). A
view of an SSE call result passed to `__trunc{s,d,x}fhf2` is `x`
rounded to the operand's type (`fstps`/`fstpl`/`fstpt` of `x`):
`llc`'s DAG skips the `f80 -> f16` fold. Test `half_runs_as_llc_s`: a
module of every operation (5 binary operations on 99 pairs, 6
predicates on 36 pairs, the conversions to and from `float`, `double`,
`x86_fp80`, `i8`, `i16`, `i32`, `i64` with ties, overflow, underflow,

`store`/`freeze`, a constant, mixed stack arguments), static and PIC,
`llc` 19 and 22, GNU ld, `qemu-i386` with SSE2: 3788 printed lines
equal `llc`'s, hod = hod under `Stress::ALL`. The libgcc calls are no
x87 barrier: with SSE2 §11 keeps no copy (measured by construction:
`x87::result` keeps copies only of `float`/`double`, which are SSE

`bfloat` stays `x86:float:bfloat` (`llc` 19 aborts on parts of the
