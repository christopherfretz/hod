# WP-238 (part 1): certain AVX-512 execution on the hosted x86-64 lane

Landed (part 1): Intel SDE as `HOD_X64_AVX512_RUNNER` with the emulated
processor's flags, the `avx512.yml` workflow, the zmm grid's generator for
every signature, two LLVM defects pinned, and the grid's `llc` bounded in
memory. Not yet: SDE in the `test x64` job of the CI lane (behind the
repository variable `HOD_CI_SDE`, unset, pending the operator's agreement).

## What it did

- `.github/actions/sde`: Intel SDE 10.13.1, Linux kit from Intel's
  download mirror
  (`https://downloadmirror.intel.com/924984/sde-external-10.13.1-2026-07-28-lin.tar.xz`),
  sha256 `94e97d623fec54385686e1e7ba65ebc9941748c05ee451423948334892bf2b50`
  (the one Intel's download page publishes), in the Actions cache under
  it; `sde64 -spr --` as the runner, and `cpu-flags.c` run under it as
  `HOD_X64_AVX512_RUNNER_CPUINFO`. Licence: the Intel Simplified Software
  License, "free of charge for any type of use"; used unmodified, not
  redistributed. `qemu-x86_64` 10.0 has no working EVEX (SIGILL).
- `hod_testutil::avx512`: a runner with known flags runs what the host
  cannot (host, then runner; with every wanted feature, then with the
  required ones), and the skip names what each lacks.
- `hod_testutil::run_with`: the child leads a process group, killed whole
  at the time limit; a kill by the limit says "timed out after N s", one
  by a signal "killed by signal N".
- `.github/workflows/avx512.yml` (a push to `avx512/**`, or by hand): the
  execution tests alone under SDE, a memory watch, the summary, and
  `scripts/avx512-summary.sh --check` (everything executed but `knl`).
- `tests/abi`: the generator moves 64-byte parts in `zmm` (poisoned
  whole; the area aligned to 64) and on the stack, and masks in the lanes
  of `xmm`, `ymm`, `zmm`, two `ymm`, and a byte per element in general
  registers and stack slots (§4.3); `zmm_grid` runs every accepted
  signature, against `llc` 22 `-O0` and one other configuration per
  machine; an `llc` error naming no function is pinned on one by halving
  (`Batch::bisect`); every grid-sized `llc` runs under a 2 GiB address
  space (`run::capped_llc`).
- `spec/abi-x64.md` §12: LLVM aborting on a call passing `<1 x i1>` under
  AVX-512, and LLVM 19's `FastISel` dropping a `<32 x bfloat>` under
  `bf16` (5.9 GB and a minute on `sapphirerapids`' second batch: the
  memory that killed hosted runners). Both are left out of the grid, as
  narrowly as found, and pinned by a test that fails once LLVM changes.

## Test evidence

  all six configurations, before the bf16 exclusion): `x86_avx512` vsib
  4896 and `avx512_names` 11688 compared, 0 wrong; zmm grid machines 0
  to 13 agree (69 to 130 s each executing, about 30 s comparing
  conventions); `znver4` failed only on the LLVM 19 bf16 defect.
- Hosted run 43666003644 (AMD EPYC 9V74, AVX-512 with BF16, no FP16;
  head cadf3da6): green. Executed 19 programs, 17 natively and 2 under
  SDE (`sapphirerapids`' machine, and the driver checking FP16), skipped
  1 (`knl`), failed 0; the `abi` zmm tests 646 s; least memory available
  11528 MB of 16 GB, at most 2 `llc` at once.
- Locally (no AVX-512): `cargo test -p hod_x64 --test abi`, `--test
  aggr`, `cargo test -p hod_testutil`, workspace clippy `-D warnings`.
