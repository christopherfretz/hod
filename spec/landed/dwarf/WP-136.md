# WP-136 — debug information on x86-64

The contract of what landed is `spec/dwarf.md` §6.17 and
`spec/harness.md` "debug" ("Other targets", "Trees", the `target:` and
`skip` directives, `--target`). This file records what the work package
did, what it measured, and what it left. The numbers are in
`docs/status/debug-x64.md`.

## What landed

**a. The writer for x86-64** (`crates/hod_dwarf/src/x86_64.rs`): the
psABI's register numbers; the relocation kinds (`R_X86_64_32`,
`R_X86_64_64`, `R_X86_64_DTPOFF64`); thread-local locations
(`DW_OP_const8u` relocated against the variable's symbol,
`DW_OP_GNU_push_tls_address`) for globals and functions' thread-local
statics; in a split unit such a variable is refused as
`debug:tls-location`. The other refusals of x86-64 before this package
(`debug:tls-location` in full units) are gone.

**b. The bridge** (`crates/hod_cg/src/emit/`): a thread-local's place is
its own symbol on x86-64 (`lines::tls_by_symbol`); the unit's code ranges
follow `llc`'s `addRange` (consecutive functions of a unit in a section
extend one range over padding: `lines::unit_span`). AArch64 objects are
byte-identical before and after (below).

**c. `classify`**: `debug:module-flags`, `debug:subprogram`,
`debug:slot-variables`, `debug:locations` are `ON` for x86-64;
`docs/status/support.md` regenerated.

**d. The harness** (`xtask/src/debug/`): `cargo xtask debug --target
x86_64` (`cross.rs`: one `/bin/sh` child per session through
`irdiff::process::run`, qemu's gdb stub on a port the operating system
chose, retried when taken, the emulator always killed and reaped;
gdb-multiarch and lldb-19); the directives `target:` and `skip(<scope>)`
and the scope words `aarch64`, `x86_64`, `remote`; `debug trees` (DIE
trees and `.debug_info` relocations against `llc`'s, both objects
verified); `debug verify --target`; the `return-column` rule for either
side's column being the call's.

**e. Tests**: `tests/debug/x64_places.c` (sret, byval, memory
arguments, a variable-length array, an over-aligned local; both
targets, quick tier); `xtask/tests/debug_x64.rs` (sessions and trees on
x86-64); `crates/hod_dwarf/src/x86_64.rs` unit tests; existing tests
marked for x86-64: `sret_entry.c` `target: aarch64` (on x86-64 `llc`'s
list has no gap and both show the uninitialized result), `tls.c` and
`tls_static.c` `skip(remote, gdb)` (no TLS through the stub), and
`xfail(x86_64)` on `places.c`, `local_type_inlined.cpp`,
`coro_labels.cpp` (kiln) and `coroutine.cpp` for refusals of hod_x64
that are not debug information's (`attr:signext`, `attr:zeroext`,
`attr:presplitcoroutine`).

**Found**: lldb-19 drives qemu's gdb stub (`gdb-remote`); both debuggers
are used. lldb 22 is not installed on the host.

## Mutations (each caught, then reverted)

1. A wrong register number: the frame base `DWARF_RSP` for `DWARF_RBP`
   (`crates/hod_x64/src/frame.rs` `FrameDesc`): every session of
   `debug_x64.rs` FAILs (`fact (n=0)` for `fact (n=4)`), and `debug
   trees` reports `DW_AT_frame_base DW_OP_reg7` against `DW_OP_reg6`.
2. A wrong frame offset: `base_offset` 8 bytes off: the sessions FAIL
   on every parameter and local.
3. A wrong relocation kind: `R_X86_64_64` for `R_X86_64_DTPOFF64`
   (`x86_64::tls_offset_reloc`): `debug trees` reports the
   `.debug_info` relocation of `tls.c` and `tls_static.c`, and the unit
   test `x86_64::tests::tls_relocation` fails.

## What is left

- `.debug_frame` (both targets): `llc` writes it for `nounwind`
  functions without `uwtable`; hod writes `.eh_frame` only.
- `attr:signext` / `attr:zeroext` and coroutines on x86-64 are hod_x64's
  rows; the tests marked `xfail(x86_64)` become stale when they land.
- The gdb stub's limits (thread-local variables, hardware watchpoints,
  lldb's shared libraries): sessions on an x86-64 host would cover them.
