# WP-162: variables described by `#dbg_value`

The contract is `spec/dwarf.md` §6.23. This file records what the work
package found, what it changed and what it measured. The oracle is `llc
-O0` 19 and 22 (x86-64 `-fast-isel=0 -relocation-model=pic`, AArch64
`-global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`).

## Before

`debug:dbg-value` was the last `debug:` refusal of the corpus: kiln's
`lifetime-markers-O2-g-fpic-run.cpp` (an `-O2` unit, LLVM 19 and 22),
on both targets, 4 modules. It has 92 value records: 8 with a
`DIArgList`, 34 with a `DW_OP_LLVM_fragment`. `-O0` input from clang 19,
clang 22 and kiln++ has none, so the bench `-g` inputs and the `-g` set
had none either.

## What changed

- `crates/hod_cg/src/dbg_value.rs`: the rows (one `VarPlace::Value` per
  record, its range and what it holds), closed by the next record of the
  variable, the block's end after a branch, or the terminator; homes
  made frame offsets in `finish_vars`; ranges moved by `move_rows`.
  `driver.rs` calls it at the block's start and end and per instruction,
  each behind one flag test.
- `crates/hod_dwarf/src/loclists.rs`: the expression check, the
  grouping of a variable's rows into one DIE, `.debug_loclists` (one
  contribution per unit with lists), `loclistx` and the offsets table of
  `.debug_loclists.dwo`. Families 107 to 112 in `abbrev.rs` (after WP-163's 106).
- `xtask/src/debug/permitted.rs` `only_value_ranges`, the `value-range`
  class, and its arm in `sessions.rs`.

## Evidence

- `crates/hod/tests/dbg_value.rs` (both targets, every object
  `--verify`-clean):
  - `ranges_follow_the_final_code`: eight blocks, a constant variable
    with one record per block and a home variable with one record; each
    entry starts and ends at the final line rows of its calls (x86-64
    shrinks its branches, so the offsets went through `move_rows`);
  - `split_lists_are_indexed`: the `.dwo` has two `loclistx` locations,
    `offset_entry_count = 2`, eight entries for the constant;
  - `expressions_are_written`: `plus_uconst` on a 4-byte home
    (`deref_size 4`), `deref` (a memory location), a `shr` on a narrow
    value (no entry), `consts` for a signed type, `constu` for an
    unsigned one, a typedef'd variable;
  - `refusals_are_named`: fragment, `DW_OP_LLVM_convert`, an expression
    ending in `plus_uconst`, a `DIArgList`, and DWARF 4
    (`debug:dwarf-4-shape`).
- `tests/debug/dbg_value.c` (`-O1`): gdb and lldb stop in a loop and
  print `i` and `sq` (in their ranges, equal to `llc`'s values) and `k`
  (outside its range: `<optimized out>` / `<variable not available>`
  under both programs). gdb's `info locals` is the one `value-range`
  line (hod has no range for `n` and `acc` there). 12 of 12 PASS per
  target.
- Full `cargo xtask debug` with the release hod: AArch64 594 results
  (582 PASS, 12 XFAIL), 1116 sessions; x86-64 574 results (546 PASS,
  6 XFAIL, 22 SKIP), 1032 sessions; no FAIL, ERROR or XPASS. `value-range` is used by
  `dbg_value.c`'s gdb sessions only.
- Objects before (74f1202) and after over the corpus and corpus-x64
  (12,474 modules) and the bench `-g` inputs (10 per target): 11,869
  built by both, all byte-identical; every other module refused by both
  with the same status; the only refusal that changed is the four
  `lifetime-markers` modules, `debug:dbg-value` to
  `debug:dbg-value-arglist`.
- Without the `DIArgList` records, that module stops at
  `debug:retained-nodes` (an inlined subprogram with `retainedNodes`,
  §6.9), not at a value record.
- Bench sources at `-O1 -g` (not in the corpus): `tiny_functions`
  builds and verifies on both targets; the others stop at
  `debug:retained-nodes`, `debug:local-type-scope`, `debug:fragment` (a
  declaration) or `attr:alloc-family`, none at a value record.
- Instructions (callgrind, `x86_64-containers-g`, no value records):
  272.85 M before, 273.08 M after (+0.09 %), measured with a binary
  built before the hooks were gated per function. On the final tree
  against 828b647: 272.94 M and 272.98 M (+0.02 %, the per-block and
  per-instruction flag tests); the objects are identical.

## After merging WP-163

WP-163 (range lists as `DW_FORM_rnglistx` with `DW_AT_rnglists_base`)
came first: its family 106 precedes these, now 107 to 112. hod's lists
stay `DW_FORM_sec_offset` outside a split unit (no `DW_AT_loclists_base`
is needed or written) and `DW_FORM_loclistx` in the `.dwo`, indexed from
the `.debug_loclists.dwo` header as split range lists are from
`.debug_rnglists.dwo`'s. `lists_beside_block_ranges` builds a unit
with both kinds of list, split and not, and verifies them. On the final
tree, against 828b647 (WP-163's head): every tenth corpus module, every
corpus `-g` module and the bench `-g` inputs (1,482 modules), 1,363
built by both and all byte-identical, the rest refused by both with the
same reason except `lifetime-markers` (`debug:dbg-value-arglist`).
`cargo xtask debug`: AArch64 594 results (582 PASS, 12 XFAIL), x86-64
574 (546 PASS, 6 XFAIL, 22 SKIP); no FAIL.

## Mutations (caught, reverted)

1. `map_value_ranges` removed from `shrink.rs` `move_rows`:
   `ranges_follow_the_final_code` fails on x86-64.
2. A signed constant not sign-extended in `entry_ops`:
   `expressions_are_written` fails (`DW_OP_consts +4294967293` for -3).
3. The `loclistx` conversion skipped in `split.rs` `dwo_abbrev`:
   `split_lists_are_indexed` fails (`llvm-dwarfdump` rejects the `.dwo`).

## Not done

- Ranges across blocks: a variable defined before a loop would have a
  location over the loop body, and a loop header's records would reach
  its successors. It needs the value's home to be proven written on
  every path in, and the ranges merged at joins.
- A home for register values a record names: most records of `-O1` code
  name a value the policy keeps in a register, so their variables have
  no entry (`tiny_functions`: 5 entries for 5006 records).
- Slot addresses and floating constants as values; `.debug_loc` for
  DWARF 4 (today `debug:dwarf-4-shape`).
