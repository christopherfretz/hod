# WP-079 — vectors as values (design 0003, V1)

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/vector.rs` (the shared helpers, first
commit `218d4b2`, then `extractelement`, `insertelement`,
`shufflevector` and the signature check), the hooks below,
`crates/hod_a64/tests/vector.rs`, `tests/ir/vectors.ll`, the heading
"Vectors, the base (WP-079)" in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1, and the
support-matrix rows.

Hooks (one call each, in `crates/hod_a64/src/target.rs`): the three
dispatch arms `ExtractElement`, `InsertElement`, `ShuffleVector` (they
were `unsupported(VECTOR)`), the first line of `A64::assign`
(`vector::check_signature`), the `vector` field of `FnState`; and
`pub mod vector;` in `lib.rs`.

## The helper API (for WP-080..083)

- `Shape::of(types, ty)` → element, lanes, class, store/alloc size,
  alignment; `Shape::lanes()` / `vector::lanes(cx, ty)` → `Lanes`, or
  the refusal by name (masks, odd and wide elements, more than 256).
- `Lanes::offset(i)` (lane `i` at `i × size`), `elem()`, `arr()`,
  `gpr_load()`/`gpr_store()`/`fpr_view()` (the lane's access).
- `image(cx, value, k)`: a `Mem` vector's slot, or an `Fpr` vector
  spilled to scratch image `k` (of 3, 48 bytes, per function);
  `spill`, `reload`, `write_mem`.
- `index(cx, value, &lanes)` → `Index::{Lane, Poison, Reg}`;
  `lane_cursor(cx, image, &lanes, index)` → a `Cursor` at the lane
  (masked or clamped, never outside); `lane_number`.
- `load_lane` / `store_lane(cx, &lanes, reg, &mut cursor)`.

## What is lowered

- `extractelement`, `insertelement` (constant and variable index of 1
  to 64 bits, the out-of-range rule of §5.5.1) and `shufflevector`
  (every mask, undefined entries, one and two operands, `%a, %a`,
  widening, narrowing, changes of class) on vectors of `i8`, `i16`,
  `i32`, `i64`, `ptr`, `half`, `bfloat`, `float`, `double` of up to 256
  lanes, both classes.
- Matrix: `type:vector` on for AArch64; `inst:extractelement` and
  `inst:insertelement` on for `Int`, `Ptr`, `Half`, `BFloat`, `Float`,
  `Double`, `Vector`; `inst:shufflevector` on for `Vector`; `Vector`
  added to `bitcast`, `load`, `store`, `select`, `freeze`,
  `extractvalue`, `insertvalue` (their lowerings are WP-039/040/067's);
  `Vector` added to the element-wise rows WP-039 lowers for `Fpr`
  vectors (`add`, `sub`, `mul`, `shl`, `lshr`, `ashr`, `and`, `or`,
  `xor`, `fadd`, `fsub`, `fmul`, `fdiv`, `fneg`, `trunc`, `zext`,
  `sext`, `fptrunc`, `fpext`, `fptoui`, `fptosi`, `uitofp`, `sitofp`):
  a `Mem` vector or an operation without one instruction is refused at
  code generation (`a64:mem-vector`, `a64:vector-op`, WP-080). Division,
  remainder, `frem`, `icmp` and `fcmp` stay off for `Vector`.
  `crates/hod_ir/tests/classify_matrix.rs`: `support_flips_rows` now
  flips `cc:ghccc` (its row was `inst:extractelement`).
- Calls: `Fpr` vectors as `abi::assign` places them; nothing new in
  the ABI.

## What is refused, by name (`hod_a64::vector`)

`a64:vector-mask`, `a64:mask-wide`, `a64:vector-odd-element`,
`a64:vector-wide-element`, `a64:vector-length`, `a64:vector-index`,
`a64:vector-call` (a `Mem` vector in a signature, WP-082);
re-exported: `a64:ptradd-vector`, `a64:vector-op`, `a64:mem-vector`.
Defined for later packages: `a64:vector-bfloat`, `a64:intrinsic-target`.
Whole-value forms of masks and odd elements (load, store, select, phi,
freeze, bitcast) stay lowered as WP-040/067 did; only lane operations
refuse them.

## Evidence

- `tests/vector.rs` `every_shape_operation_and_index_agrees`: 1,950
  functions over 43 shapes (18 `Fpr`, 25 `Mem`) and 11 bitcast pairs,
  5,792 cases (variable indices over lane 0, the last lane, a random
  lane, `N`, `2^w − 1` and all ones, with garbage above the index
  width), 33,194 comparisons with a Rust model on the defined bytes and
  sentinels around the result: hod plain under bfd, lld and mold, hod
  under `Stress::ALL`, `llc -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`, and clang
  `ext_vector_type` C (`__builtin_shufflevector`). Zero differences.
- `register_vectors_cross_calls_both_ways`: hod ↔ gcc `vector_size`
  (`v4si`, `v2df`, `v8qi`, `v4hf`, ten `v2sf` with stack ones), hod
  plain, stressed and llc, three linkers.
- `the_longest_vectors_compile` (256 lanes), `what_is_refused_is_refused_by_name`
  (16 cases), `tests/ir/vectors.ll` through `cargo xtask irdiff
  --witness`: PASS under bfd, lld, mold.
- Mutations, each caught and reverted: (1) the reload after a
  variable-index `insertelement` of an `Fpr` vector dropped: 1,260
  differences; (2) the lane-size scale dropped from the variable lane
  address (`lsl #0`): 2,400; (3) the index not reduced to its width
  before the clamp: 740.
