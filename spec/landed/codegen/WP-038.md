# WP-038 — AArch64 calls

Landed: `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1 "WP-038: calls"; `hod_a64/src/call.rs`
(calls, invokes, variadics, landing pads); the support-matrix rows of
calls, conventions, argument attributes and the `va_*` intrinsics.

## What it did

- `crate::call` drives `hod_cg`'s call sequence (§7.1) and adds: the
  refusals, the clearing of the bits above a narrow integer leaf of a
  `Mem`-class result after a call (`{ i7, i64 }`, `i65`), the variadic
  save areas, `va_start` (the 32-byte AAPCS64 `va_list`), `va_copy`,
  `va_end`, the reload of the unnamed argument registers before a
  `musttail` call in a variadic function, `landingpad` (`{ ptr, i32 }`,
  `catch` and `filter` clauses) and `resume` (`_Unwind_Resume`).
- `fastcc`, `swiftcc`, `cxx_fast_tlscc` assign as `ccc` on Linux (as
  `llc` does); `nest` is `x15`; `byref` is a plain pointer.
- Refused by name: `a64:call`, `a64:call-inline-asm`, `a64:call-bundle`,
  `a64:invoke-odd-int-result`, `a64:call-wide-int-extension`,
  `a64:call-narrow-ext-stack-arg`, `a64:call-mismatched-declaration`,
  `a64:va-intrinsic`, `a64:va_arg`, `a64:landingpad`,
  `a64:landingpad-clause`, `a64:resume`.
- Rows flipped on AArch64: `function:variadic`, `cc:fastcc`,
  `cc:swiftcc`, `cc:cxx_fast_tlscc`, `attr:zeroext`, `attr:signext`,
  `attr:nest`, `attr:sret`, `attr:byval`, `attr:byref`,
  `attr:returns_twice`, `inst:call`, `call:indirect`, `call:variadic`,
  `intrinsic:llvm.va_start`, `intrinsic:llvm.va_end`,
  `intrinsic:llvm.va_copy`, and with WP-049's tables
  `function:personality`, `inst:invoke`, `inst:landingpad`,
  `inst:resume`, `landingpad:cleanup`, `landingpad:catch`,
  `landingpad:filter`. `intrinsic:llvm.eh.typeid.for` waits for the
  intrinsic group, `tail:musttail` for the stack-argument hook.
- The differential harness generates calls, indirect calls and many
  arguments (`tests/common/groups.rs`).

## Tests (`crates/hod_a64/tests/call.rs`)

- Random signatures (`tests/common/callgen.rs`; 300 per run,
  `HOD_CALL_SEED`, `HOD_CALL_COUNT`): integers of every width with and
  without extensions, pointers, floats, HFAs, aggregates in registers,
  in memory and `byval`, `sret`, variadic calls, the four conventions;
  gcc-compiled C against modules compiled by hod and `llc` in every
  pairing, stressed, PIC with a shared object, under GNU ld, lld and
  mold; the call and GOT relocations equal `llc`'s.
- `hod_interp` as the oracle: 150 chains of IR functions, plain and
  stressed.
- Handwritten forms (`tests/data/call/hand.ll`, `hand.c`): odd leaves,
  `byval` both ways, `_setjmp`, `musttail` (stack and register
  arguments swapped, a variadic thunk, indirect), declared extensions,
  `noreturn`; `nest` and `byref` against `llc`.
- Real C++ exceptions (`tests/data/call/eh.ll`, `eh_comdat.ll`,
  `eh.cpp`) thrown through and into hod's frames with `emit_module`'s
  tables: cleanups, catch by type, catch-all and rethrow, nested pads,
  nested try in one frame, filters, pads after stack arguments, a
  dynamic alloca, a throw from hod, a COMDAT function in two objects;
  static, stressed and PIC in a shared object under every linker, with
  `llc`'s objects as the control. `tests/unwind.rs`
  `a_throw_passes_through_a_hod_frame` is enabled.
- Every refusal by name; two ignored tests record bugs outside the
  group: a wide `signext` result is not extended
  (`hod_a64/src/target.rs:349`, `hod_cg/src/call.rs:339`), and a direct
  call through a mismatched function type takes the declaration's
  attributes (`hod_cg/src/call.rs:143`; refused here).

## Results

- The differential harness with every group on (int, mem, control,
  calls, float): 20000 programs, 13169 agree, 6831 skipped, none
  disagree; the skips are all `a64:store-fpr` (6066) and
  `a64:load-fpr` (765), refused by `hod_a64/src/mem.rs:381,430`.
