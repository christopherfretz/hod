# WP-137 — coroutine bodies on x86-64 (design 0005 package C7)

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/coro_body.rs` (new) and `coro.rs` (the
handle operations, which refused as `x64:todo:coro` before); one hook
each in `target.rs` (`X64::coro`, `for_function`, `reg_info`,
`CORO_BODIES`, `NOOP_CORO_FN`, the body intrinsics in `lower_call`,
`coro_stubs`, `coro_dispatch`, `coro_transfer`), `frame.rs`
(`FrameState::coro`, `coro_body::enter` after `sub rsp`) and `common.rs`
(`FrameRef::Coro` in `frame_mem`, `coro_access` in `frame_inst_shifted`);
`crates/hod_x64/tests/coro_body.rs`; the x86_64 column of the coroutine
rows of `crates/hod_ir/src/classify/matrix.rs`, `docs/status/support.md`;
`spec/codegen.md` §7.6 "x86-64"; the seeds of `tests/ir/coro`.

## What is implemented

Every target obligation of §7.6 with `rbx` as the frame base: the stubs
`F.resume` / `F.destroy` (entry CFI, then F's own prologue rows), the
mode and frame words, the body intrinsics, the suspend switch, symmetric
transfer (`jmp r11` from a resumed activation, `call r11` from the ramp),
the suspended-region check on every heap address, and WP-108's debug
obligations (the frame word zeroed in the prologue, written at
`coro.begin` and in each stub; `coro_stub_ready` in each stub). `rbx` is
in x86-64's save set already, so a coroutine only removes it from the
pool (`REG_INFO_CORO`).

## Refusals

| name | what |
|---|---|
| `x64:coro-wide-atomic` | an atomic access wider than 64 bits in a coroutine (`cmpxchg16b` writes `rbx`) |
| `x64:coro-promise` | `coro.promise` with an alignment that is not a constant power of two ≤ 2^30, or a non-constant direction |
| `x64:code-model` | `coro.noop` outside the small code model |

The generic `coro:*` refusals of the plan are unchanged.

## Evidence

- `cargo xtask irdiff tests/ir/coro --target x86_64` on LLVM 19 and 22,
  bfd, lld and mold: 57 PASS, 0 FAIL, 17 SKIP (the other dialect); hod
  built 19 of 19 objects. AArch64 unchanged (the same counts).
- `tests/coro_body.rs`, clang 19 and 22 pre-optimisation IR, oracle `opt
  default<O0>` + `llc -O0 -fast-isel=0 -relocation-model=pic`, bfd, lld,
  mold, under qemu, plain and with poisoned frees:
  - mixed objects: hod's coroutine under `llc`'s driver, the reverse,
    and both hod, the stubs called from `llc` code that keeps a value in
    `rbx`, an exception thrown out of a resumed body across `resume`;
  - `Stress::coro_frame` with `rbx` clobbered, returning and unwinding;
  - symmetric transfer: 1,000,000 awaits between two objects (hod, `llc`,
    mixed) under a 1 MiB guest stack (`QEMU_STACK_SIZE`), every
    activation at one depth;
  - the shapes (no `[rbx…]` in the suspended region, the transfer, the
    tail exits, the stubs, the prologue's words) and the refusals.
- The 50 corpus-x64 modules that name `coro` (25 per LLVM major) are
  post-optimisation output without `presplitcoroutine`; `irdiff
  --hybrid` passes all 150 results, but hod builds none of them yet:
  each falls back on `attr:zeroext`, an x86-64 ABI row this package does
  not own.

## Seeds

`tests/ir/coro` were produced for AArch64: their `target-cpu` and
`target-features` attributes are removed (with the triple and datalayout
already gone) and `unsupported(x86_64)` lines dropped, so every seed runs
on both targets.

## Mutations (each caught, then reverted)

| mutation | caught by |
|---|---|
| the transfer always calls (no `jne` to the tail exit) | `symmetric_transfer_between_objects_runs_in_constant_stack`: the guest stack overflows (SIGSEGV) |
| no null resume pointer at the final `coro.save` | `coroutines_mixed_with_llc_objects_keep_rbx`: `final: rbx 1 done 0` where `llc` prints `done 1` |
| `rbx` left in a coroutine's pool (`reg_info` ignores `X64::coro`) | `a_coroutine_takes_rbx_out_of_its_pool`; the mixed programs crash (SIGSEGV) |
