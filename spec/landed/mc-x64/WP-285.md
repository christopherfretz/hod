# WP-285 — the 32-bit mode of the x86 encoder (i386, design 0008 I2)

Landed: `spec/mc-x64.md` section "32-bit mode" (new); additions to the
head, "Where hod and LLVM's assembler differ" and "Test method". Design
0008 section 1.3, row I2 of 6.1.

## What it did

- `src/mode.rs` (new): `Mode` (`X64`, `X86`), `Mode::admit(inst) ->
  Result<Inst, ModeError>`, `ModeError` with four stable names
  (`x86-mode:extended-register`, `x86-mode:operand-size-64`,
  `x86-mode:rip-relative`, `x86-mode:x86-64-only`). `X64` is the
  identity; `X86` refuses, then rewrites `67`, the absolute `SIB` form,
  the `moffs` moves, `inc`/`dec` `40+r`/`48+r` and `xchg eax, eax`.
- `src/inst.rs`, `src/evex.rs`: the three free bits of `Inst`'s flags
  record a RIP-relative operand, an absolute operand in its `SIB` form,
  and a 64-bit-only instruction; `Inst::remove`/`set` keep the site and
  the lock position on their bytes. `src/state.rs` (`segbase_r`, `bmi_rrr`
  at `Q`) and `src/evex.rs` (`kmov_kr`/`kmov_rk` at `Q`) mark themselves
  64-bit-only. No 64-bit constructor's bytes, site or `has_rex` changed;
  the API gained `Mode` and `ModeError` only.
- `tests/l1`: `Batch::for_mode`, `Tools::all_in(mode)` with the triple
  (`-mattr=+nopl` at `i686`), `text::to_i386`; the `differential!` macro
  also emits `i386::<test>` for every sweep; `i386_forms.rs` (the
  rewritten forms, 18 `i386` relocation sites); `src/mode.rs` unit tests.

## Evidence

- The 31 existing sweeps and their unit tests pass unchanged in 64-bit
  mode (`cargo test -p hod_mc_x64`, `HOD_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1`: 10 unit, 86
  L1, 2 doc tests).
- 32-bit mode, LLVM 22 and 19 alike: 32 groups (the 31 translated and
  `i386-forms`), 99,465 encodings equal `llvm-mc -triple=i686`'s bytes
  and disassemble as intended; 497,078 rejections, 495,160 confirmed by
  `llvm-mc` at `i686`. Of them, mode refusals: extended register 371,381,
  64-bit operand size 111,511, RIP-relative 10,916, 64-bit-only 576 (the
  remainder are the checked forms' own rejections and the lenient
  `cvtsi2s` rows below).
- 18 `i386` relocation sites (`R_386_PC32`, `PLT32`, `32`, `GOTOFF`,
  `GOT32X`, `TLS_LE` through `moffs`) agree with `llvm-mc -filetype=obj`
  in offset, type and implicit addend at both majors.

## Corrections to the design

- `syscall` is not refused: `llvm-mc` 19 and 22 encode it at `i686`
  (`0F 05`). It is still never the Linux `i386` system call.
- `endbr64` assembles at `i686`; `endbr32` is the `i386` marker and is not
  provided.
- `xchg eax, eax` is a fifth short form (`90`), and `ud1` loses its `67`.
- LLVM's default `i686` CPU has no `NOPL`: `.nops` above 1 byte is an
  error there; the L1 runs at `-mattr=+nopl`.
- `llvm-mc` at `i686` accepts `cvtsi2sd xmm, qword ptr [..]` and drops the
  size; `llvm-objdump` at `i686` prints EVEX `vmovdqu64` as `vmovdqu32`.
- The absolute address needs no new operand: `Mem::abs` is rewritten by
  the mode, so the design's "`Mem::absolute` form" is the existing one.

## Not done

- `endbr32`, the 32-bit-only instructions (`pusha`, segment pushes, ...),
  16-bit addressing, a 32-bit store of a relocatable immediate
  (`movl $sym, mem`: `mov_mi32s` is `Q` only).
- `hod_asm` 32-bit operands (I13) and the back end (I5) are not touched;
  `llc`'s padding at the default `i686` CPU is not measured.
