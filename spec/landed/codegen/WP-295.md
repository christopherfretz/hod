# WP-295 — x86-64: `sitofp` / `uitofp` of `i128` to `bfloat`

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/aggr.rs` (`fp_int_cast` takes the
`bfloat` result; `wide_to_bfloat`), `crates/hod_x64/src/float.rs`
(`magnitude_to_double_odd`, the 64-bit round-to-odd sequence of
`int_to_bfloat` made a function, its bytes unchanged), `spec/codegen.md`
§5.6.5 ("Casts") and §5.6.14 (2). Tests:
`crates/hod_x64/tests/wide_bfloat.rs`; `tests/leftovers.rs`'s refusal
list trades `sitofp i96 to bfloat` (now compiled) for `i129`.

## What `llc` does, checked

- `llc-22 -O0 -mtriple=x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu` calls `__floattisf`
  (`sitofp`) or `__floatuntisf` (`uitofp`), the `i128` in `rdi:rsi`, and
  then `__truncsfbf2` on the `float` in `xmm0`; the `bfloat` comes back
  in the low 16 bits of `xmm0`. `llc-19` makes the same two calls.
- That rounds twice. Under `qemu-x86_64`, `uitofp i128 (0x183 << 20) -
  1` is `0x4dc2` from `llc` 22 and `0x4dc1` rounded once (libgcc's
  `__floatuntibf`, which GCC calls, gives `0x4dc1`); `(0x181 << 40) + 1`
  is `0x57c0` from `llc` and `0x57c1` rounded once.
- libgcc 14 (x86-64, what the tests link; no compiler-rt) has
  `__floattisf`, `__floatuntisf`, `__truncsfbf2` and also
  `__floattibf`, `__floatuntibf`.
- `llc-22` for AArch64 aborts on the same IR (`report_fatal_error`);
  hod's AArch64 back end refuses it (`a64:wide-fp-cast`, unchanged).

## Decision: round once, inline

hod already rounds every narrower integer to `bfloat` once and states
where `llc` does not (§5.6.7: `uitofp i33 0x1feffffff`). The wide case
follows the same rule rather than `llc`'s double rounding, and needs no
routine: the magnitude is cut to 64 bits with a sticky last bit, goes
through §5.6.7's round-to-odd sequence to `double`, is scaled by an
exponent add, then `double` to `float` rounded to odd and to `bfloat`.
Round to odd composes, and `float` has more than `8 + 2` bits, so the
last rounding is the only one that counts. Results therefore equal
libgcc's `__floattibf`/`__floatuntibf` and `llc` 22's wherever `llc`'s
`float` rounding does not land on a `bfloat` tie.

## Still refused (`x64:wide-fp-cast`)

`fptosi`/`fptoui` from `bfloat` to 65 to 128 bits; every conversion with
an integer wider than 128 bits (`aggr::fp_int_cast`, the `bits` match;
`x87::` the same for `x86_fp80`).
