# WP-226: NEON in AArch64 inline-assembly templates

Spec: `spec/asm.md` §20 (and §5.1, §5.2, §6, §7, §8, §16, §17).

## What was built

- `crates/hod_asm/src/a64/operand.rs`: vector registers with an
  arrangement, an element, and register lists (ranges, an element list
  with an index) as operands (`Op::VReg`, `Op::VLane`, `Op::VList`).
- `crates/hod_asm/src/a64/neon.rs`: the Advanced SIMD forms on vector
  operands and the scalar two-register forms. It reuses `hod_mc_a64`'s
  `Fp3`, `Fp2`, `Long3` and structure encoders, and builds the
  by-element, `fmla`/`fmls`, compare and `2`-form words itself.
- `crates/hod_asm/src/a64/mod.rs`: `Features` gains `neon`, `aes` and
  `fp16`, read in order with LLVM's implications. `Arg::Fp` carries the
  value's size, so the `w`/`x` modifiers print the view of that size.
- `crates/hod_asm/src/a64/encode.rs`: tries the NEON forms first. Scalar
  `fcvtzs`/`fcvtzu` move to `neon.rs`; scalar `fcmeq` needs `neon`.
- `crates/hod_a64/src/inline_asm.rs`: the `w`/`x`/`y` operand types,
  as `llc` allocates them.

## LLVM's behaviour, measured with llvm-mc and llc 19 and 22

- **Features**:
  - `+neon` implies `fp`.
  - `+aes`/`+crypto` imply `aes`, `neon` and `fp`.
  - `+fullfp16`/`+fp16fml`/`+sve` imply `fp16` and `fp`.
  - `-fp-armv8` clears all four; `-neon` clears `neon` and `aes`.
  - `+v8.2a`, `+v9a` and `+sha2` imply neither `aes` nor `fp16`.
  - Scalar SIMD needs `neon`; scalar `fadd` does not.
- **Operand grid**: `llc` 19 and 22 print every cell alike.
  - `w` takes 16, 32, 64 and 128-bit values and fails on 8-bit values
    and `<3 x float>`.
  - `x` takes only 128-bit values; `y` takes no non-SVE type.
  - `a` prints `[s0]`; `z` prints `z0`; `v`, `c`, `n` and `t` are
    errors.

## Evidence

- `cargo test -q -p hod_asm --test llvm_mc`:
  - 182 NEON bodies (182 words) equal `llvm-mc` 22's and 19's;
  - the 415 earlier bodies (428 words) stay equal;
  - 42 new refusals by name.
- Feature matrix: 13 bodies × 18 `target-features` strings, 468 cells
  over both majors. hod accepts exactly what `llvm-mc` accepts (216
  accepted) and otherwise refuses `asm:feature`.
- `cargo test -q -p hod_a64 --test inline_asm_neon`:
  - The operand grid has 375 cells (`w` × 25 types × 13 modifiers, and
    `x`, `y` × 25 types):
    - 157 cells: hod's word is the one of `llc`'s printed text.
    - 193 cells: hod refuses by name where `llc` fails or prints no
      register (`a`, `z`).
    - 25 cells: hod alone refuses (`i128`, `<2 x i8>`, `<2 x half>`;
      `a64:inline-asm-operand-type`).
  - The census templates of Eigen, highway, xxhash and abseil appear in
    hod's code as `llvm-mc` writes them.
  - 12 refusals by name.
- `cargo test -q -p hod_a64 --test inline_asm`, new seeds:
  - The seeds:
    - Eigen `fmla` by element;
    - highway `fcvtzs`/`fcvtzu` (`.4s`, `.2d`, scalar `d`) and `fcmeq`
      (scalar `d`, `.4s`, `.8h`);
    - `fcvtzs .8h` with `fullfp16` (the driver calls the two `.8h`
      seeds only where the processor has FP16, `HWCAP_ASIMDHP`: the
      emulated baseline, a cortex-a53, does not, so they run on an
      AArch64 host and are skipped on an x86-64 one);
    - OpenCV `fcvtns`;
    - xxhash `umlal`;
    - abseil `pmull` and `pmull2` with `aes`;
    - the `w`/`x` modifiers;
    - the `x` constraint;
    - `ld1`/`st1` with post-index;
    - a template that clobbers `v8`..`v15` in every view.
  - Each seed was built by hod (plain, stress, far) and by `llc` 19 and
    22. It was linked with the C driver under bfd, lld and mold, dynamic
    and static, and run natively on AArch64.
  - The driver's checks:
    - saturation, NaN, ties-to-even;
    - `umlal` arithmetic;
    - carry-less products against a C model;
    - the loaded and stored sums;
    - `d8`..`d15` intact after the clobbering template.

    Every check passes. `llc` 19's one failure is the known
    `goto_out2`, unrelated.
  Under `HOD_REQUIRE_TOOLS=1`: 30 linked runs (5 builds × bfd, lld and
  mold × dynamic and static); 24 exit 0 and the 6 of `llc` 19 print
  only that failure.
- Objects untouched elsewhere: every 10th corpus entry,
  `scripts/corpus-bytes.py` (`-j 4`) against main's release `hod`.
  - aarch64: 669 of 669 modules identical, 0 differ.
  - x86-64: 684 of 684 modules identical, 0 differ.
