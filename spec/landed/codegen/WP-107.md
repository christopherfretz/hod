# WP-107 — symmetric transfer on AArch64 (design 0005 §3.3, C4)

Landed: `llvm.coro.await.suspend.handle` in coroutine bodies, in the
ramp and in resumed activations, with a guaranteed tail transfer;
`spec/codegen.md` §7.6 "Symmetric transfer (WP-107)" and "Exceptions in
the ramp and in an await"; the seeds `tests/ir/coro/<stem>-{19,22}.ll`
(`deep-transfer`, `handle-throws`, `resume-inside-handle`,
`gro-throws-uaf`); `crates/hod_a64/tests/coro_transfer.rs`; the support
matrix row (AArch64 supported, x86-64 not implemented) and
`docs/status/support.md`.

## What is lowered

After the wrapper's call returns `h` in x0 (from `FnCx::coro_called`,
which `call_through` already called there): `ldr x16, [x0]`, the mode
word, `cbnz` to a tail exit in a resumed activation (the epilogue's
teardown, x19 restored, then `br x16` with x0 = h), `blr x16` in the
ramp (own call-site row, no landing pad), then the `invoke`'s normal
edge and the suspend as before. No access to the coroutine's frame
after the wrapper returns. `.void` and `.bool` are unchanged (WP-106)
and are compiled in both modes.

## Hooks and edits outside the WP-106 files

- `crates/hod_cg/src/target.rs`: `Target::coro_transfer(cx, exit)`
  (default `coro:target`).
- `crates/hod_cg/src/call.rs:270`: `coro_called` returns `CgResult`
  (`?` added), because the transfer can fail (fixups).
- `crates/hod_cg/src/coro_body.rs`: `coro_called` starts the transfer;
  `FnCx::coro_tail_exit` (a `TailExit` to the callee register).
- `crates/hod_a64/src/coro_body.rs` (`transfer`), `target.rs` (the hook).
- `crates/hod_ir/src/classify/matrix.rs`: the row.
- `spec/harness.md` "Oracle skips": the two `gro-throws-uaf` rows.
- Tests that asserted the refusal: `hod_a64/tests/coro.rs`,
  `hod_a64/tests/coro_body.rs`.

## What stays refused

Nothing new. `intrinsic:llvm.coro.await.suspend.handle` is gone on
AArch64; x86-64 still refuses coroutine bodies (`coro:target`; the
matrix's x86-64 column says not implemented).

## Evidence

- Seeds, `cargo xtask irdiff tests/ir/coro`, LLVM 19 and 22 (strict):
  36 tests, 57 PASS, 17 SKIP (other dialect) each; hod built 19 of 19
  objects. The eight new seeds pass on bfd, lld and mold.
- `deep-transfer`: 1,000,000 awaits (2,000,000 transfers) from resumed
  activations and from a ramp; `flat 1` (every activation of the awaited
  coroutine at the same stack address) for hod and for LLVM.
- `crates/hod_a64/tests/coro_transfer.rs`: the ping-pong across two
  objects (awaiting side, awaited side) for LLVM 19 and 22: llc/llc,
  hod/llc, llc/hod, hod/hod, each under bfd, lld and mold, plain and
  with poisoned frees, under `ulimit -s 1024`, entered from `llc` code
  that keeps a value in x19: 48 runs, all `flat 1` and x19 intact. Plus
  the shape check (no `[x19` from the wrapper's return to `blr x16`;
  every `br x16` tail exit reloads x19 from `[x29…]`; 5 transfers, 5
  exits per dialect).
- Corpus: the 4 pre-opt modules per major with `.handle`
  (`expr.await/symmetric_transfer`, `expr.await/awaiter_protocol`,
  `coro.generator/libstdcxx-generator-{int,elements-of}-run`), from
  WP-100's pre-opt regeneration with the corpus directives and
  `input: pre-opt`: `irdiff --hybrid`, LLVM 19 and 22, 12 of 12 PASS
  each; hod built 4 of 4 objects each (no fallback). With WP-106's 18
  of 21, hod now builds 22 of 25 coroutine modules per major; the 3
  left are `coro:frame-access-after-suspend` (the contract
  postconditions).
- libc++: no `support.coroutines` test uses symmetric transfer (design
  0005 §4: 0 `.handle` in libc++), so nothing changes there.
- Poisoned (`GLIBC_TUNABLES=glibc.malloc.tcache_count=0
  MALLOC_PERTURB_=165`): hod's builds of all 34 seeds of `tests/ir/coro`
  and the 8 corpus modules print their expected output plain and
  poisoned (84 of 84). LLVM's build of `gro-throws-uaf` crashes (SIGSEGV)
  without any environment, both majors: hence its `oracle-skip`.
- Mutations (design 0005 §5.7 and the WP's list), each caught and
  reverted:
  1. transfer by call (no tail exit; a resumed activation calls and
     returns): `deep-transfer-{19,22}` SIGSEGV (stack overflow).
  2. the tail exit leaves 16 bytes of its frame (`sub sp, sp, #16`
     before `br x16`): `deep-transfer`, `resume-inside-handle` SIGSEGV.
  3. the suspend index stored after the wrapper returns instead of at
     `coro.save`: the structural check (Internal error, "a coroutine
     frame access in the suspended region") on every handle seed; with
     the check bypassed, `resume-inside-handle` (the nested resume
     enters at the wrong point and recurses), `deep-transfer` and
     `gro-throws-uaf` fail.

## Notes

- `cargo xtask corpus reasons`: the corpus is post-`opt`, so no corpus
  module ever reached hod with `.handle`; the refusal had no row there.
  The pre-opt view is WP-109's census.
- The throwing-`get_return_object` case (`gro-throws-uaf`) needs nothing
  new from hod: the plan's rule 3 already keeps the exception slots on
  the machine stack; the seed pins it.
