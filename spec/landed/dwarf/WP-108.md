# WP-108 — coroutine debug information (AArch64)

The contract of what landed is `spec/dwarf.md` §6.16, `spec/codegen.md`
§7.6 "Debug information" and `spec/harness.md` "debug" (`pre-opt:
checks`). This file records what the work package did, what it
measured, and what it left.

## What landed

**a. Locations on the frame word** (`crates/hod_dwarf/src/coro.rs`,
`crates/hod_cg/src/coro_debug.rs`). `hod_dwarf::CoroPlace` (heap,
copied at `coro.begin`, ramp-only parameter) and `LocalPlace::Coro`;
`FnCx::coro_vars`, from `finish_vars`, rewrites a coroutine body's
variable rows: heap slots and values, the `coro.begin` copies, clang's
parameter declared on its slot and on its frame copy (one DIE), kiln's
parameter and its artificial frame copy of the same name (both DIEs the
copied form), `!coro.outside.frame` parameters. One expression per
variable, valid at every pc of the body: no location list.

**b. The subprogram and the stubs.** The subprogram ends where
`F.resume` starts (R1): a stub builds its frame before it writes the
frame word, so no location holds there; the stubs are described by
their symbols, line rows and CFI only, like clang's `.await_suspend`
wrappers. Each stub has a scope-line row at its entry and a
`prologue_end` row at its ready point (R2), so gdb's `break F` (which
resolves the stub symbols) stops past the stub's prologue.

**c. The facade.** `coro:debug-info` is lifted
(`crates/hod/src/session/coro.rs`).

**d. L7.** `pre-opt: checks` (`xtask/src/debug/{directives,build,sessions}.rs`):
the front end's IR before LLVM's passes, `opt -passes='default<O0>'`
before `llc`, the sessions judged by the checks, no lines comparison.
`tests/debug/coroutine.cpp`: before the first suspend (the parameter),
after a resume (`bt` four frames deep through `coroutine_handle::resume`,
`finish` from a callee, the parameter, a loop variable, a local, the
promise), in the destroy path (`bt` from a destructor, `up`, the
parameter and the promise), by gdb and lldb.

Hooks, one call each (outside `hod_dwarf` and `coro_debug.rs`):
- `crates/hod_cg/src/driver.rs:699-700` `self.coro_vars(start)` in
  `finish_vars`; `driver.rs:786` `record.coro_ready = self.coro_ready()`;
- `crates/hod_cg/src/coro_body.rs:119-120,138` the `ready` field;
  `coro_body.rs:660-695` `coro_debug_view`, `coro_stub_ready`,
  `coro_ready` (one block at the end of the file);
- `crates/hod_a64/src/coro_body.rs:93-99` the prologue's zero store to
  the frame word; `:177-178` `cx.coro_stub_ready()` in each stub;
- `crates/hod_cg/src/record.rs` (`VarPlace::Coro`, `coro_ready`),
  `crates/hod_cg/src/emit/mod.rs` (`stubs` into `Placed`),
  `crates/hod_cg/src/emit/lines.rs` (the DIE's size, `stub_rows`).

No abbreviation family was added.

## Evidence

- **L7** (`cargo xtask debug tests/debug/coroutine.cpp`): 18 PASS
  (clang 19, clang 22, kiln++ × GNU ld, lld, mold × gdb, lldb-19), all
  "permitted: pre-opt checks". gdb's transcripts equal `llc`'s but for
  the frames' names (`count(int) [clone .resume]` against `count`) and
  the location count of `break count` (7 against 6); lldb's diverge
  where `llc`'s `breakpoint set -n count` also stops in the clones. With
  kiln++, hod's frames show `n=4` where `llc`'s clones show no argument.
- **`--verify`** (`cargo xtask debug verify`): the corpus has no `-g`
  coroutine module; kiln++'s 34 coroutine entries regenerated with `-g`
  before LLVM's passes (`scripts/preopt-corpus.sh`'s command,
  `-j 2`): 24 built, 24 clean; 10 declined by code-generation names
  (`coro:frame-access-after-suspend` 6,
  `intrinsic:llvm.coro.await.suspend.handle` 4), none by a debug name.
- **Tests**: `crates/hod_dwarf/src/coro.rs` (the three expressions'
  bytes); `crates/hod_a64/tests/coro_debug.rs` (verify, R1, the zero
  store, and the gdb session's values for the three front ends).
- **Mutations**, each caught and reverted: the prologue's zero store to
  the frame word removed (`coro_debug.rs`: "the frame word is not 0
  before the first call"); the copied form's `DW_OP_bra` one byte short
  (`a_copied_variable_branches_on_the_word`, and the gdb session loses
  `count (n=4)`); the merge of a variable declared twice removed
  (the module is then refused as `debug:variable-twice`).

## Left

- `__coro_frame`, the frame's structure type and the `__coro_resume_N`
  labels (§6.16 "Left out"): no installed tool reads them.
- Describing a stub after its ready point (`DW_AT_ranges` on the
  subprogram families), which would show the variables at a stop in
  the stub.
- x86-64: no coroutine bodies yet (`attr:presplitcoroutine` is
  `not-implemented` there); the target obligations of §7.6 apply when
  they land.
