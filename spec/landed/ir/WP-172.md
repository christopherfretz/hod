# WP-172 — text and bitcode objects of one module

Landed: the rule "same module, same version" in `spec/bc.md` §9.2,
`spec/ir.md` §12 and the dialect table of `spec/ll.md` §3.2;
`crates/hod_bc/tests/upgrade.rs`. No reader or kit change: neither
reader was wrong.

## What was wrong

The identity tables of WP-092, WP-159 and WP-169 made every module's
bitcode with `llvm-as-22`, the LLVM 19 corpus included. One module,
`kiln/llvm19/conformance/target.neon/bfdot-target-attribute-run.cpp.ll`,
built to two objects. Its LLVM 19 text calls
`llvm.aarch64.neon.bfcvtn` and `bfcvtn2`. LLVM 22's reader upgrades
those intrinsics as it reads them, into `fptrunc <4 x float> to
<4 x bfloat>` and `shufflevector` (`llvm-dis-22` of the bitcode has no
`bfcvtn` left). So `hod_bc` was handed a different module from the one
`hod_ll` was handed. The readers read what they were given.

## Evidence (AArch64, release `hod --strict --filetype=obj -O0`)

| input | object (md5) |
|---|---|
| LLVM 19 text | `6a6b7683…` (`bfcvtn`, `bfcvtn2`; `.text` 0x2a4) |
| `llvm-as-19` bitcode of it | `6a6b7683…`, equal to the text |
| `llvm-as-22` bitcode of it | `968d9e40…` (the `fptrunc` expansion; `.text` 0x674) |
| `llvm-dis-22` of that bitcode, as text | `968d9e40…`, equal to its bitcode |
| the LLVM 22 module, text and `llvm-as-22` bitcode | `968d9e40…` both |

The oracle agrees with this. `llc-22 -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`
gives the same object from the LLVM 19 text and from its `llvm-as-22`
bitcode, because llc's own text reader upgrades too. `llc-19` keeps
`bfcvtn`. Both of hod's objects are right for their module. The two
forms could not run differently. All four programs (hod text, hod
bitcode, llc-19, llc-22) print the same output on this host, which has
`bf16`. hod writes `fptrunc` to `bfloat` as integer round-to-nearest-even
with NaNs made quiet. That conversion equals the hardware `bfcvt` for
all 2^32 `float` inputs under the default FPCR (0 mismatches). The two
can differ only under a non-default FPCR rounding or flush mode, and
there LLVM 22 itself has replaced the instruction.

The seed cannot go in `tests/ir`. It needs AArch64 intrinsics, and
every seed there must build for both targets. It lives in
`crates/hod_bc/tests/upgrade.rs`. That test checks two things:
- the LLVM 19 module reads to the same bodies and module through
  `hod_ll` and through `llvm-as-19` + `hod_bc`;
- `llvm-as-22` changes the module.

## Search

Every module of the AArch64 corpus (6,250) and the x86-64 corpus
(6,244) was built with release `hod --strict --filetype=obj -O0` from
three inputs: its text, the bitcode of its own version's `llvm-as`, and,
for LLVM 19 modules, the `llvm-as-22` bitcode. For each input the row
holds the object's md5, or the exit status and the start of stderr.

| corpus | built, text = own-version bitcode | differ | refused by both, same text | one form builds | no bitcode |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AArch64 | 5,955 | 0 | 6 | 0 | 289 |
| x86-64 | 5,894 | 0 | 29 | 0 | 321 |

In the "no bitcode" column, `llvm-as` refuses the file (most are
corpus entries that hold several modules). `hod_ll` refuses every one
of them too.

Against `llvm-as-22` bitcode, the LLVM 19 modules give 2,960 equal on
AArch64 and 1 different (this module), and 2,930 equal on x86-64 with
none different. Building the AArch64 corpus with
`--mtriple=x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu` builds nothing: every module is
refused, mostly as `unsupported-target`. x86-64 was therefore checked
over its own corpus. Differences between same-version forms: none.
