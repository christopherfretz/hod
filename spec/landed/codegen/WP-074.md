# WP-074 — atomics on AArch64

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/atomic.rs` (new); its dispatch in
`crates/hod_a64/src/target.rs` (`Target::lower`: the atomic `Load` and
`Store` arms before the class-based ones, and `Fence`, `CmpXchg`,
`AtomicRmw`); 100 outline-atomics helpers in `hod_cg::RuntimeFn`
(`crates/hod_cg/src/sym.rs`, after `ClearCache`); the support-matrix rows
`inst:fence`, `inst:cmpxchg` (Int, I128, Ptr, Aggregate),
`inst:atomicrmw` (Int, I128, Ptr, Half, BFloat, Float, Double),
`mem:atomic-load`, `mem:atomic-store` and the 21 `atomicrmw:<op>` rows;
the heading "Atomics (WP-074)" in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1;
`crates/hod_a64/tests/atomic.rs` with `tests/common/atomic_threads.rs`;
`crates/hod/tests/atomic_cxx.rs`; `tests/ir/atomics.ll` now builds
(`;@ unsupported(x86_64): *`). No new encodings: `hod_mc_a64` had them.

## What is lowered

Every atomic operation of `spec/ir.md` §5.4 at the widths LLVM allows:

| | 1, 2, 4, 8 bytes | `i128` | `half`, `bfloat`, `float`, `double` | `ptr` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| atomic `load` | `ldar` / `ldr` | `ldxp`/`stxp` loop, or `cas16` (outline) | via `x16`, `fmov` | `ldar` / `ldr` |
| atomic `store` | `stlr` / `str` | `ldxp`/`st(l)xp` loop | via `x16` | `stlr` / `str` |
| `atomicrmw` (all 21 operations) | helper or LL/SC | LL/SC (`ldaxp`, ...) | LL/SC (`xchg` also `bfloat`; arithmetic not `bfloat`) | `xchg`: helper or LL/SC |
| `cmpxchg` strong, weak | `casN` (outline) or LL/SC | `cas16` or LL/SC pair | — | as 8 bytes |
| `fence` | `dmb ish`, `dmb ishld` (acquire); singlethread: nothing | | | |

The sequence follows the function's `target-features`: outline helpers
where `+outline-atomics` is on and `+lse` is not (what kiln and clang
write), exactly where LLVM 22 `-O0` calls a helper; LL/SC loops
otherwise. The rules are in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1 "Atomics".

## What is refused, by name

- `a64:atomic-unaligned`: an atomic access whose alignment is below its
  size (LLVM calls libatomic's `__atomic_*`).
- `a64:atomic-type`: `fp128`, vectors, other integer widths, an
  operation that does not fit its type.
- `a64:atomic-bfloat-op`: `atomicrmw` arithmetic on `bfloat`.
- `a64:atomic` (`target::ATOMIC`) stays as the never-reached fallback
  of `mem.rs` and `aggr.rs`.

Note: `classify` still refuses `type:bfloat` (not this package's row), so
through the facade a `bfloat` atomic stops there; `compile_module`
lowers it (tested).

## Evidence

- `atomic::every_atomic_operation_agrees_with_exclusive_loops` and
  `..._with_outline_helpers`: 812 functions each (every `atomicrmw`
  operation x i8..i128 x five orderings, a volatile one per operation;
  the floating-point operations at `half`, `float`, `double`; `bfloat`
  and `ptr` exchange; loads and stores at every type and ordering;
  `cmpxchg` strong and weak at i8..i128 and `ptr` under all 15
  success/failure pairs, succeeding and failing; fences; constant
  operands; the edges of the wrapping and saturating operations): 6,368
  cases each, 5,790 against `hod_interp`, 560 against `llc -O0
  -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` only (`fmax`, `fmin`, `fmaximum`,
  `fminimum`, which the interpreter leaves to `llc`), 18 undefined (a
  NaN result); hod plain and `Stress::ALL`, and `llc`, linked and run
  natively; zero disagreements.
- `atomic::no_memory_access_between_exclusive_load_and_store`: every
  word of both tables, plain and `Stress::ALL` (every value forced
  through memory), scanned: 758 exclusive windows (LL/SC table), 417
  (outline), none holding a load, store or call. The guarantee is the
  lowering's shape: operands, addresses and temporaries are in
  registers before the loop label, and the loop is only words
  (`spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1).
- `atomic::outline_helpers_are_called_where_llvm_calls_them`: the
  libgcc names, and no call without the feature.
- `atomic::contended_programs_on_threads_agree`: 108 counters (every
  `atomicrmw` operation at every width it takes, a strong and a weak
  `cmpxchg` increment loop at every width) on 4 threads x 2,000
  operations, each exact against the same operations under a mutex in
  gcc-built C; spinlocks on strong `cmpxchg` i32, weak i8, weak i128,
  and exchange (the `atomic_flag` pattern); a Treiber stack (8,000
  concurrent pushes, then concurrent pops, each node once); 128-bit
  stores, exchanges and loads (no torn value); message passing through
  release/acquire and through fences, 2,000 round trips. Six builds
  (hod LL/SC and outline, plain and stressed; `llc` both), bfd, lld,
  mold, three runs each: 54 runs, all exact.
- `crates/hod/tests/atomic_cxx.rs`: a C++20 program with
  `std::atomic<int, unsigned long long, short, unsigned char, double,
  float, int*>`, `atomic_flag`, `compare_exchange_weak`/`_strong`,
  `shared_ptr`/`weak_ptr` copied on four threads, a function-local
  static, fences; kiln++ and clang++ at `-O0` and `-O1`, through
  `hod::compile`, linked under bfd, lld and mold, run twice each (24
  runs): every run prints what g++'s build prints.
- `cargo xtask irdiff tests/ir/atomics.ll`: PASS under bfd, lld, mold.

## Corpus

Before (`docs/status/reasons.md` at the branch point): an atomic row was
the first refusal of 16 LLVM 22 modules (`mem:atomic-load` 8,
`inst:atomicrmw` 6, `mem:atomic-store` 2) and 15 LLVM 19 ones; in the
census, `mem:atomic-load` stopped 14/13 modules, `inst:atomicrmw` 11/10,
`mem:atomic-store` 8/7, `inst:cmpxchg` 4/3. After (`cargo xtask corpus
reasons -j 4`, release hod): no atomic reason anywhere in the first
refusals or the census. Of the 19 LLVM 22 modules with an atomic (18 at
LLVM 19), 16 (15) compile; the other three stop at `tls:general-dynamic`
(2) and `attr:convergent` (1). Compiled in all: 3,084 (LLVM 22) and 3,049
(LLVM 19), from 2,984 and 2,952 in that report, which other packages'
merges also moved.

`cargo xtask corpus verify --strict -j 4`: LLVM 22 2,883 of 3,143 objects
built by hod, LLVM 19 2,851 of 3,107; every built one runs like the
`llc -O0` build under bfd, lld and mold except `gcc-torture/pr79286.c`
(both LLVMs), which does not link: a constant `getelementptr` far past a
global, not an atomic (reproducer:
`atomic::a_far_constant_offset_from_a_global_links`, ignored). Every
other FAIL is a module hod declines (strict mode).

## Speed and memory

The LL/SC table as a module (929 functions, 13,970 IR lines without the
`bfloat` functions `classify` refuses), the release `hod` binary in
strict mode: 21 ms for the whole process (parse, classify, lower,
write), peak 5.5 MB; `llc -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`: 140 ms,
70 MB. The outline table: 21 ms, 6.1 MB. The lowering allocates
nothing per instruction (fixed tables and stack arrays).

## Mutations (caught, reverted)

1. The exclusive loop without its retry (`cbnz w17` dropped): caught
   only by `contended_programs_on_threads_agree` (lost and torn updates
   under contention); the single-threaded table cannot see it.
2. `udec_wrap` selecting on `hs` instead of `hi`: 158 disagreements in
   each table.
3. The helper suffix with the acquire and release bits swapped (`acq` for
   `rel`): `outline_helpers_are_called_where_llvm_calls_them`.
