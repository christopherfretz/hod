## What is lowered

A `presplitcoroutine` function the `hod_xform` coroutine plan accepts
(`CoroPlan`; `hod_cg` now depends on `hod_xform`), compiled once as the
ramp plus two entry stubs; §7.6 is the contract. In short:

- The heap frame: header (resume at 0, destroy at 8), the promise, the
  4-byte suspend index, the heap slots, the heap values; `coro.size` is
  its end, `coro.align` `max(frame align, 16)`. Homes: `FrameRef::Coro`
  for what the plan puts on the heap, the machine stack otherwise.
- `x19` is the frame base in every coroutine: saved after the other
  callee saves, restored at every exit, with its CFI row. Two machine
  words: the mode (0 in the ramp, 1 in a stub activation) and the frame.
- `coro.begin` copies the shadows of pre-begin values to the heap and
  switches `x19`; `coro.suspend` is a call that stores nothing
  (`w0 := -1`, the re-entry label, the result); the suspend `switch`
  dispatches first and stores the phis of each edge after.
- `F.resume` / `F.destroy`: local `STT_FUNC` symbols after the function;
  they rebuild the prologue, set the frame and the mode, and branch on
  the index to the re-entry label with 0 or 1 (`brk #1` otherwise).
- `coro.id`, `alloc`, `size`, `align`, `begin`, `save`, `suspend`, `end`
  (plain and unwind; LLVM 19's `i1` result), `free`, `frame`,
  `is_in_ramp`, `await.suspend.void` and `.bool`.
- The suspended region (between an await wrapper's call and its
  suspend) addresses no heap home; a violation is an Internal error.

## Notes

- kiln's `!coro.outside.frame` marker (kiln PR #666): the corpus predates
  it. The 6 corpus modules `run-coroutine-post-{enforce,observe,quick-enforce}`
  (LLVM 19 and 22), refused `coro:frame-access-after-suspend` for the
  return slot named by `post(r: ...)`, are the cases where the marker
  would have made the refusal unnecessary (kiln marks that slot; not yet
  verified on rebuilt modules). The clang 22 seeds carry marked parameter allocas, the
  clang 19 seeds do not; both pass.
- `gro-throws-uaf` (handle-returning final `await_suspend`) waits for
  WP-107; `gro-throws` is its variant without symmetric transfer.
- `xtask/tests/irdiff_real.rs:184` (`the_seed_corpus_passes_in_hybrid_mode`)
  asserts every record is a PASS; the dialect pairs give one SKIP per seed
  of the other major, so it fails with these seeds. The fix belongs to its
  owner: count `Outcome::Skip` with the passes.
- `hod_cg` now depends on `hod_xform` (the coroutine plan); the crate
  graph in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` is not updated here.
- The CLI's pre-opt trigger set (`spec/cli.md`) is WP-109's.
