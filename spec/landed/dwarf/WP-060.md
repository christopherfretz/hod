# WP-060 — the L7 debugger harness

The contract of what landed is `spec/harness.md` "debug" (the command)
and `spec/dwarf.md` §8.4 (what L7 checks, the seeds, the findings). This
file records what the package did, what it measured and what it left.

## What landed

- `xtask/src/debug.rs` and `xtask/src/debug/` (new): `sessions` (the run,
  outcomes, results file), `directives` (the test format), `transcript`
  (gdb and lldb sessions through `irdiff::process::run`, masking, crash
  detection), `build` (front ends, `llc`, hod strict, the link matrix),
  `lines` (the `(function, line)` comparison and its P1/P3 classes),
  `kiln` (kiln's debugger tests through hod's shim), `verify`
  (`llvm-dwarfdump --verify` over the corpus's `-g` modules).
  Registered in `xtask/src/lib.rs` and `xtask/src/main.rs`
  (`cargo xtask debug`).
- `tests/debug/` (new): 12 tests, 13 sources (`step.c`, `places.c`,
  `globals.c`, `classes.cpp`, `inlined.cpp`, `exception.cpp`, `tls.c`,
  `comdat.cpp` + `comdat_other.cpp` + `comdat.h`, and the four findings
  `tls_static.c`, `sret_entry.c`, `gep_column.c`, `finish_column.c`).
- `xtask/tests/debug_real.rs` (new): the quick tier as a test with the
  lines threshold, and the harness's own outcomes (a mutated hod FAILs,
  a wrong check is an ERROR, a stale `xfail` an XPASS).
- The gate: the quick gate has no step; `cargo test` runs the quick tier
  (`step.c`, `comdat.cpp`, `tls_static.c`: 78 results, 120 sessions,
  about 20 s at load, under the 30 s budget). The full gate runs
  `debug::gate` (every test, then `verify`; about 45 s plus 12 s).

## Findings (each a seed with an `xfail`)

1. `tests/debug/tls_static.c`: a function's `_Thread_local` static has
   `DW_OP_addrx` of its TLS symbol; gdb prints a wrong value.
   `crates/hod_dwarf/src/global.rs:51-58` returns for function-scoped
   variables before the TLS check at `:59`. A hod bug (WP-085's path).
2. `tests/debug/sret_entry.c`: `llc`'s location list for an `sret`
   variable has a gap at the first line's address; hod's shows the value.
   In hod's favour; needs a §6.7 entry to be permitted.
3. `tests/debug/gep_column.c`: hod's first instruction of a line is a
   `getelementptr` `llc` folds into a store; lldb prints another column.
   `crates/hod_cg` instruction selection.
4. `tests/debug/finish_column.c` (from kiln's `step_finish.cpp`): the
   return address of a call has the assignment's column in hod's program
   and the call's in `llc`'s. `crates/hod_cg`.

## Notes for later

- lldb 19 is the only lldb installed; `lldb-22` is used when present.
  lldb 19 cannot read compressed sections in relocatable objects, which
  the harness never asks of it (it debugs linked programs).
- kiln describes `geo::Box` of `classes.cpp` as an incomplete type (`llc`
  and hod alike), so that test runs on clang's IR only.
- Columns are not part of the `(function, line)` comparison; findings 3
  and 4 are seen only in lldb's transcripts.
