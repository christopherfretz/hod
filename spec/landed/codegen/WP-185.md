# WP-185: AArch64 `-mbranch-protection`

## What is implemented

`spec/codegen.md` §5.6.26, `spec/eh.md` §2.2 and §4.5, `spec/object.md`
"GNU property note", `spec/ir.md` §2.1.

- **BTI.** `bti c` at every function entry that does not sign; `bti j`
  on address-taken blocks, landing pads and after a call that returns
  twice (`hod_a64/src/protect.rs`, through the new `Target::begin_block`
  hook of `hod_cg`, called once per block after its landing-pad setup).
- **PAC-ret.** `paciasp`/`pacibsp` first, `autiasp`/`autibsp` before
  every `ret` and every `musttail` exit (`b`, `br x16`), in the resume
  and destroy stubs of coroutine bodies too; every function is signed
  under `non-leaf` because hod's frame always saves `x30`.
  `llvm.returnaddress` is stripped with `xpaclri`, always (as `llc`).
- **CFI.** `hod_eh::Op::NegateRaState` (0x2d) after each sign and
  authenticate; `Op::BKeyFrame` as an FDE's first row gives it a CIE
  with `B` (`zRB`, `zPLRB`), ordered after the plain twin as `llc`'s
  CIE key; `.debug_frame` sorts B-key FDEs last. `hod_cg`'s body restate
  after a non-final exit restores the signed state by parity.
- **The note.** `hod_obj::elf::gnu_properties_note` /
  `add_gnu_properties`, a list of `(type, data)` properties; `hod_cg`
  writes `GNU_PROPERTY_AARCH64_FEATURE_1_AND` (BTI 1, PAC 2, GCS 4) from
  the module flags. The x86-64 path (`add_gnu_property_note`) is
  unchanged.
- **classify.** The five module flags and four attributes `ON` on both
  targets (x86-64 ignores them, as `llc`); `docs/status/support.md`
  regenerated.

## What stays refused

- `branch-protection-pauth-lr` (flag and attribute, FEAT_PAuth_LR,
  `paciasppc`): `module:`/`attr:branch-protection-pauth-lr` on AArch64.
- An unknown `sign-return-address` scope or key:
  `a64:branch-protection-value`.

  goto, switch, `musttail`, 70000-byte frame with dynamic alloca,
  `setjmp`/`longjmp`, `llvm.returnaddress`) and
  `tests/ir/branch_protection_unwind.ll` (an exception through four
  signed frames of both keys, a cleanup that resumes): `xtask irdiff
  --witness`, LLVM 19 and 22, AArch64 and x86-64, bfd/lld/mold: 24 PASS.
  The exception is in a seed without BTI because `llc` 19 puts no
  `bti j` on a landing pad: its own program dies with SIGILL there.
- `hod_a64/tests/protect.rs`:
  - 14 functions x 6 forms against `llc` 19 and 22, 168 comparisons:
    the entry instruction, every exit authenticated with the right key,
    `bti j` equal to `llc` 22's count and at least `llc` 19's, the B-key
    CIE where `llc` has it, and hod's negate rows at 4 and after every
    `auti*`. The note is byte-equal to `llc`'s in all 12 cases.
  - The programs (C++ driver built with `-mbranch-protection=standard`)
    under bfd, lld (`-z force-bti`, silent) and mold, hod and `llc` 22
    objects: same output. `readelf -n`: bfd and lld show `BTI`, `PAC`,
    `BTI, PAC` as the form has them. GCS is never seen, because glibc's
    start files lack it and the property is an AND. mold 2.37 writes no
    AArch64 property.
  - This host enforces both (`bti`, `paca` in `/proc/cpuinfo`). A
    `bti j` made a `nop`: SIGILL. An `autiasp` made a `nop`: SIGSEGV.
    The negate rows made `DW_CFA_nop`: SIGSEGV in the unwinder. Each is
    caught.
  - `gdb` backtraces `may_throw`, `mid`, `pads`, `main` through signed
    A- and B-key frames.
  - C++ that clang 22 compiles with `-mbranch-protection=standard`

- `hod/tests/branch_protection.rs`: both seeds give the same object

- `hod_eh/tests/signed.rs`: the encodings, `zR`/`zRB`/`zPLR`/`zPLRB` in

- Real units, `kiln++ -O0 -mbranch-protection=standard`, hod `--strict`,

  `ext.gnu.cleanup/cleanup-run` print what their directives say; notes

## Differences from `llc`

- hod signs leaf functions under `non-leaf` (it always saves `x30`).
- hod puts `bti c` on every function. `llc` 22 omits it for a local
  function whose address is not taken.
- `bti j` on landing pads: as `llc` 22. `llc` 19 has none.
- Negate rows sit right after the signing instruction. `llc` places
  them with the frame's other CFI.

## Follow-ups

- `hod_a64/src/target.rs:167`: `NOOP_CORO_FN` is a bare `ret` reached
  by an indirect call. Under BTI it needs `bti c` first. It is only
  produced for `llvm.coro.noop`, and `hod_a64/tests/coro.rs` pins its
  size at 4 bytes.
- An unreachable address-taken block is bound to the trap and has no
  `bti j`. Reaching it is undefined anyway.
