## Decisions, and why

- **The personality is `__gxx_personality_v0`, by name.** LLVM leaves the
  landing pad's inputs to it; the model states libstdc++/libc++abi's
  behaviour and traps where the two differ (`eh-phase-mismatch`).
- **Uncaught means no cleanups.** The search phase runs first, so an
  uncaught exception terminates at the throw, as `__cxa_throw` does.
- **Only selector equality with `typeid.for` and its sign are defined.**
  LLVM numbers type ids otherwise, and its inliner merges clauses; the
  model's numbering is first mention, as hod_cg's.
- **Comparing two block addresses traps** (deviation from the brief,
  which asked for equality): LangRef defines a `blockaddress` only as an
  `indirectbr` operand and compared with `null`, and `llc -O2` folded an
  equality differently from what it emitted. §12 has it as open.
- **The oracle printer marks non-entry functions with pads `noinline`**
  (§10.2): inlining merges the caller's clauses into the pad, which is
  correct LLVM but not what the generator's sign dispatch expects.
- **Pads observe what they received.** Which pad was entered, the
  selector (as the test that matched it) and the integer values live into
  the pad are sunk, so the trace compares them; the raw selector is not,
  since it differs between correct compilations.
- **`callbr` stays unsupported**: it needs inline assembly.
- **Switches draw nothing when off**, proved by a hash of printed
  programs fixed before the change.

## Validation

- LLVM 22.1 and 19.1 (AArch64): 12 hand-written cases, 102 quick and
  2,120 slow (`HOD_ORACLE_SEEDS=1000`) generated programs agree at `-O0`
  (SelectionDAG) and `-O2`, per major; an uncaught exception aborts with
  `SIGABRT` and prints nothing of its cleanups.
- `hod_cg` differential, five stress modes: quick, 12 seeds × 16 sets,
  960 compilations compared, none refused, 1,130 throws, 1,290 pads
  entered; 400 seeds (`HOD_CG_EH_SEEDS=400`, seeds 1 + set × 1,000,003 +
  0..400): 32,000 compared, 42,470 throws, 46,745 pads entered. No core
  bug was found.
- Mutations planted in `hod_cg/src` (each reverted), all caught by
  `unwinding_programs_run_as_the_interpreter_runs_them`:
  1. a call-site row names the function's first pad (`driver.rs`,
     `row.pad = 0`): a pad's sunk number differs;
  2. the `invoke`'s unwind-edge `phi` stores left out (`call.rs` §7.1
     step 1): a pad `phi`'s sunk value differs;
  3. a new type id counted from the chunk's table, not the function's
     (`cx.rs` `type_id`): a clause's type id is outside the table;
  4. the pad stub's landing offset not recorded (`call.rs`): the pad is
     entered with the stack lowered; caught by the `leave` check, which
     was added for it (without it the test machine cannot see it);
  5. a `filter` recorded as a catch-all (`cx.rs` `pad_filter`): the
     handler's sunk selector test differs.

## Follow-ups

- `hod_ir` proposals (from WP-030): `Phi::value_for(block)`, and an
  address-taken marker on blocks (`spec/codegen.md` §9.6, §13).
- The `existing_seeds_keep_their_programs` constants depend on `hod_ir`'s
  printer; a printer change must re-derive them after checking that the
  generator did not change.
- `spec/codegen.md` §12 could name the unwinding differential test and
  the mutations above.
- `Body::verify` accepted a generator body whose use was not dominated
  by its definition (values of a `try_throw` chain used in the pad); the
  interpreter caught it as `undefined-value`. For WP-026's verifier.
- Class-type exceptions (`__do_catch`), foreign exceptions, funclets.
