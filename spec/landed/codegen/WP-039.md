# WP-039 — the AArch64 floating-point group

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/float.rs`, `crates/hod_a64/tests/float.rs`,
the heading "Floating point (WP-039)" in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1, the
`WP-039` line of `tests/common/groups.rs`, and the support-matrix rows.

## What is lowered

- `fadd`, `fsub`, `fmul`, `fdiv` of `float` and `double` use one
  instruction each. `half` is computed through single precision, which
  is exact (§5.5.1). `fp128` calls `__addtf3`, `__subtf3`, `__multf3`
  and `__divtf3`.
- `frem` of `float` and `double` calls `fmodf` and `fmod`.
- `fneg` of every scalar format, `bfloat` and `fp128` included, and of
  every vector.
- `fcmp`: all 16 predicates on `half`, `float` and `double`, using one
  or two conditions. It is fused into `select`, and `float::flags` is
  exported for `condbr`.
- Conversions:
  - `fptrunc` and `fpext` between `half`, `float` and `double`.
  - `fptoui`, `fptosi`, `uitofp` and `sitofp` between those formats and
    every integer width from `i1` to `i64`, odd widths included. They
    extend with `sbfx`/`ubfx` first.
  - `bitcast` between `Fpr` and `Gpr` values and between `Fpr` values.
- `select` and `freeze` of every `Fpr` value. `select` uses `fcsel` up
  to 64 bits and `bsl` at 128 bits.
- Vectors, as listed in §5.5.1.

## What is refused, by name

- `a64:fp128-libcall`: `fp128` `fcmp`, its conversions, and `frem`. No
  `RuntimeFn` exists for them.
- `a64:half-frem`.
- `a64:bfloat`: arithmetic, compares and conversions of `bfloat`.
- `a64:vector-op`: integer vector `udiv`, `sdiv`, `urem` and `srem`;
  `mul` of `i64` elements; vector `frem`; arithmetic on `half` vectors.
- `a64:fp-cast`: vector casts with no single instruction.
- `a64:vector-fcmp` and `a64:vector-icmp`: the result `<N x i1>` is
  `Mem` class.
- `a64:vector-select`: a vector condition.

The skeleton's names `a64:fp-binary`, `a64:fneg`, `a64:fp-freeze`,
`a64:fcmp` and `a64:fp-select` remain the fallback for a form that
cannot occur.

## Evidence (`tests/float.rs`)

- `every_form_agrees_with_llc_and_the_interpreter`: 612 functions and
  35,673 argument sets.
  - Every operation is tried with every type.
  - Every predicate is tried on every pair of special values: ±0,
    ±denormal min and max, ±1, ±max, ±inf, quiet and signalling NaNs
    with payloads.
  - Every conversion is tried at widths 1, 7, 8, 16, 17, 31, 32, 33, 63
    and 64, at both ends of the range.
  - The forms are also tried with constant operands and as fused
    select chains.
  - The functions are compiled plain and under `Stress::ALL`, and also
    compiled by `llc -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`. They are linked
    into one C driver with GNU ld, lld and mold, and run.
  - hod's results equal `hod_interp`'s (`NanPolicy::Host`) bit for bit,
    NaN payloads included.
  - Out-of-range conversions are poison, so they are not compared; the
    test uses `ops::float_to_int` to find them.
  - 8 results differ from llc's in the NaN only. The cause is llc's DAG
    folds (§5.5.1).
- `random_chains_agree_with_llc_and_the_interpreter`: by default 300
  random straight-line chains with 3,600 calls (seeded; set the count
  with `HOD_FLOAT_SEEDS`). 13 of them differ from llc in the NaN only.
- `fast_math_flags_change_nothing`: the same code words with and
  without `fast`.
- `a_compare_into_a_select_is_fused`: one `fcmp`, one `fcsel`, and no
  `cset`.
- `what_the_group_does_not_lower_is_refused_by_name`: covers every name
  above.
- `fp128` and `half` forms are compared with llc only, because the
  interpreter has no `half` or `fp128`.

## Mutations (each caught, then reverted)

1. `one` with only the MI condition: 427 calls differ.
2. `olt` mapped to LT instead of MI: 477 calls differ.
3. No zero extension before `ucvtf`: 585 calls differ.

## Corpus counts (5,760 `.ll` files) for what is refused

- `fp128` compares and conversions: 80 files. That is 310 `fcmp` and
  90 conversions.
- `bfloat` compute operations: 120.
- Integer vector division: 138.
- `i64`-element vector `mul`: 8.
- Vector `icmp`: 33.
- Vector `fcmp`: 4.
- Vector `select`: 1.
- `frem` of any type: 0.
- Non-zero vector constants: about 167. These are refused by the
  skeleton's `common::materialize_fpr_const`, not by this group.

## Follow-ups

- `crates/hod_cg/src/sym.rs` (`runtime_fns!`) needs `__eqtf2`,
  `__lttf2`, `__unordtf2` and the others, the `fp128` conversion
  functions, and `fmodl`. With those, `a64:fp128-libcall` goes away.
- `crates/hod_a64/src/common.rs` `materialize_fpr_const`: non-zero
  vector constants. §5.5.1 says "until WP-039", but the helper is the
  skeleton's.
- Vector compares need WP-040's `<N x i1>`.
- `condbr` (WP-037) uses `float::flags`.
- Matrix: `type:half` stays LATER, because its loads and stores belong
  to WP-036, but its rows are listed with the `half` class.
  `inst:bitcast`, `inst:select` and `inst:freeze` are WP-035's rows; the
  `Fpr` forms of them are in place.
