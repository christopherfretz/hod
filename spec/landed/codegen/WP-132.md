# WP-132 — vectors on x86-64 (X-vector)

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/{vector,lanes,mask,vcall}.rs`; one-line hooks
in `float.rs` (`binary`, `fneg`, `cast`), `intrinsic.rs` and
`fpintr.rs` (the top of `intrinsic`); tests
`crates/hod_x64/tests/{vector,lanes,mask,vcall}.rs` with the generator
and runners of `tests/common/vector_gen.rs`; the vector cells of the
x86-64 column of `hod_ir`'s matrix (`type:vector` and 120 instruction
and intrinsic rows) and `docs/status/support.md`; the seeds
`tests/ir/{vectors,vector_lanes,vector_arith,vector_masks}.ll` run on
x86-64; `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.13.

## What is lowered

SSE2 only. Every fixed vector the shapes of §5.5.1 allow:

| what | how |
|---|---|
| `extractelement`, `insertelement`, `shufflevector` | through frame images; constant extracts from an `xmm` by `pshufd` + `movd`/`movq` or `pextrw` |
| element-wise integer and floating-point operations, casts | one packed instruction where SSE2 has it (`padd*`, `psub*`, `pand`, `por`, `pxor`, `pmullw`, `addps`..`divpd`, `fneg` by `xorps`, shifts by a constant splat by `psll*`/`psrl*`/`psra*`); else the lane loop over the scalar kernels |
| the vector intrinsics AArch64 lowers | the lane loop; library calls per lane for rounding, `fma`, `pow`, the transcendental functions; reductions folded in order |
| masks `<N x i1>`, `N ≤ 64` | bit-packed in a general register: compares (every predicate; `half`/`bfloat` by integer keys), bit rules, `select`, casts, element access, shuffles, stores, reductions |
| loads, stores, `phi`, `freeze`, constants | the memory, control and constant groups' (class `Fpr`, `Gpr` mask or `Mem`) |
| calls and returns | `abi::assign`'s placement; masks of 2/4/8/16 elements as the lanes of one `xmm` (`vcall.rs`); complex `float` as `<2 x float>` |

## Refused, by name

- `x64:mask-wide` (lanes of a mask of more than 64 elements),
  `x64:vector-odd-element` (`i4`, `i24`), `x64:vector-wide-element`
  (`i128`, `fp128`, `x86_fp80` lanes), `x64:vector-length` (more than
  256 lanes), `x64:vector-index` (an index wider than 64 bits),
  `x64:ptradd-vector`, `x64:vector-bfloat` (64-bit integers to `bfloat`
  lanes), `x64:vector-reduce` (a reduction of `bfloat` lanes, or with
  operands that are not the intrinsic's), `x64:vector-mask` (an
  intrinsic of `i1` lanes other than a reduction).
- Across calls: `x64:mask-vector` (a mask of other than 1, 2, 4, 8, 16
  elements), `x64:wide-vector-abi` (AVX/AVX-512 signatures, WP-140).
- `x64:todo:constant`: a constant mask other than zero as a `phi`
  incoming, call argument or returned value.
- `x64:todo:vcall` and `x64:todo:mask` are no longer returned.

## Evidence

- `lanes::generated_vectors_agree`: 600 random functions of the
  interpreter's vector oracle; 530 compared on hod (plain and every
  stress mode), `llc` 22 and 19 under `qemu-x86_64`, 0 disagreements
  (`llc`: 0; `llc` 19 aborts on 2 batches); 14 undefined; refused:
  `x64:vector-wide-element` 51, `x64:mask-vector` 3,
  `abi:vector-element` 2. Text hod/`llc` 22: 565798/216435 bytes
  (2.6×); 7453 instructions in 44 ms.
- `vector::element_access_and_shuffles_agree`: 385 functions on 11
  shapes, 289 compared, all agree. `shifts_by_constants_agree`: 147
  functions, all compared. `half_reductions_and_min_max_agree`: 128
  functions against `llc` 22. `three_operand_lanes_fit_the_minimal_pool`,
  `the_longest_vectors_compile`, `what_is_refused_is_refused_by_name`.
- `mask`: 2441 functions (every `fcmp` predicate on `half`, `bfloat`,
  `float`, `double` over NaN, zeros, infinities, subnormals: 1616; every
  `icmp`: 500; bit rules and selects: 94; casts, access, shuffles,
  stores, reductions: 231), all agree.
- `vcall::vectors_cross_calls_both_ways`: 90 calls over 22 vector types
  (register, two-register, memory, masks), with nine vectors among
  scalars (the stack), complex `float`; callers and callees each from
  hod plain, hod stress, `llc` 22, `llc` 19, 13 pairings (52 linked
  programs), all agree with the interpreter (`half` with `llc` 22).
- Mutations caught: `ashr` of 16-bit lanes as `psrlw` (16
  disagreements); `vcall` gather without `and 1` (36, all `llc`→hod);
  `half` NaN test as `≥` infinity (440); `half` `fadd` reduction as
  `fsub` (8).
- `cargo xtask irdiff --target x86_64` on the four seeds with bfd, lld
  and mold: 12 PASS of 12 against `llc` 22 and against `llc` 19 (hod
  built 4 of 4 objects); their `unsupported(x86_64)` lines are gone.
- The probe of every flipped matrix row (each instruction and intrinsic
  on `<4 x i32>`/`<4 x float>`, `<3 x i16>`/`<3 x double>`,
  `<8 x i16>`/`<8 x half>` under every stress mode) compiles but for
  `fpext` to `fp128` lanes (`x64:vector-wide-element`); it found the
  `half` min/max register shortage and the `half` reductions, fixed.
