# WP-085 — `hod_dwarf`: inlined subroutines, abstract instances, functions' statics

The design is `spec/dwarf.md`. The contract of what landed is §6.9
(families 72 to 80 appended to §6.1's table), the additions to §6.4's
refusals, §6.7 and §7.2. This file records what the work package did,
what it measured, and what it left.

## What landed

- `crates/hod_dwarf/src/inline.rs` (new): `llc`'s `LexicalScopes` keys
  `(scope, inlinedAt)` and abstract scopes; abstract instances
  (`DW_AT_inline`) with their parameters, locals, labels and blocks,
  written by the first function that inlines a subprogram; the
  `DW_AT_abstract_origin` forms of the concrete DIEs;
  `DW_TAG_inlined_subroutine` (`call_file`/`call_line`/`call_column`,
  `low_pc`/`high_pc` or a range list); functions' statics under the
  function's early DIE or its abstract instance; the refusals of §6.9.
- `src/func.rs`: the scope walk over keys, entities in inlined scopes,
  the early DIE taken by `add_function`, the writer's hooks (one call
  each in the subprogram, variable, block and label arms).
- `src/die.rs`: the DIE kinds, `for_functions` knowing which subprograms
  have code, a function's static through `static_parent` (where
  `debug:local-static` was).
- `src/abbrev.rs`: `DW_TAG_inlined_subroutine`, `DW_AT_inline`,
  `abstract_origin`, `call_column`/`file`/`line`; families 72 to 80
  (`CODE_END` 9279 → 9851). No `Error` variant.
- `hod_cg`: unchanged (the bridge already passes every row with its
  `LocId`; the names reach it as `debug:*`).
- Tests: `crates/hod_a64/tests/dies_inline.rs`, `dies_inline_debugger.rs`,
  `dies_inline_throughput.rs`, `data/dies_inline/`; `dies.rs` and
  `lines.rs` lose the two refusals that went.

## Evidence

- Trees equal to `llc`'s (19 and 22): 18 objects, 558 DIEs, 80 inlined
  subroutines, 43 abstract instances (`inline.cpp`, `statics.cpp` by
  clang 19, clang 22 and kiln++; `hand.ll`), both objects `--verify`
  clean.
- Debuggers: 24 pairs of sessions (gdb and lldb × LLVM 19 and 22 × GNU
  ld, lld, mold × two cases) equal: a breakpoint hitting both inlined
  instances, `bt` with inlined frames, locals in them, `step` into and
  `finish` out of an inlined call.
- Corpus (`corpus_survey`, before at a2ef577 and after): the 12 DWARF 5
  `-g` modules unchanged, 8 written (28 functions), none with inlined
  code at `-O0`; the `-O2` module stops at `debug:subprogram-attribute`
  (`isOptimized`). `stl.cpp` (clang 19, clang 22, kiln++; 2,035 functions)
  with every function stubbed: before, refused whole by
  `debug:local-static`; after, by WP-059's `debug:specification`, and,
  with member functions made free ones, by `debug:local-type-scope`
  (WP-057's early local types). So no per-function figure inside
  `stl.cpp` yet; the module is also refused by `a64:atomic`.
- Throughput (release): 195 to 286 ns per DIE; N to 2N ratio 1.00 to 1.10.
- Size: `.debug_info` 1.05 to 1.07 × `llc` 22's.
- Mutations caught (made, run, reverted): `call_column` one off, the
  call's file taken from the callee, nested inlining flattened
  (`inline_trees_match_llc`; the last also `refusals_are_named`).

## Decisions

- **The abstract origin is decided by the writer**, not when the DIE is
  made, as `llc` decides at the end of the unit.
- **`#dbg_value` stays refused**, on the census of §6.9: no `-O0` input
  holds one, so no form would be exercised.
- **Member functions' abstract instances** are written with
  `specification` when the declaration is among the class's elements;
  those whose declaration is WP-059's wait for it
  (`debug:inlined-member`).
