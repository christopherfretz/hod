# WP-040 — the AArch64 `Mem`-class group

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/aggr.rs`; `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1
"Mem-class values (WP-040)"; the support-matrix rows of the group; the
differential harness's `i128` switch.

## What is lowered

On integers wider than 64 bits (`i65` to `i8000` and beyond; `i128`
included), structs (padded, packed, nested, empty), arrays (nested,
zero-length) and `Mem` vectors:

- `add`, `sub`, `mul`, `and`, `or`, `xor`, `shl`, `lshr`, `ashr`
  (constant or variable amount), register or constant on either side;
  `udiv`, `sdiv`, `urem`, `srem` up to 128 bits through `__udivti3`,
  `__divti3`, `__umodti3`, `__modti3`;
- `icmp` with every predicate; `select` (scalar condition), `freeze`,
  `phi` (the glue's copy);
- `trunc`, `zext`, `sext` between every pair of widths that has a wide
  side; `ptrtoint`, `ptrtoaddr`, `inttoptr`; `bitcast` between `Mem`
  types and between a `Mem` value and a `Gpr` or `Fpr` one;
- `load`, `store` (exactly the store size), constants (wide integers,
  `zeroinitializer`, `undef`/`poison`, nested constant aggregates);
- `extractvalue`, `insertvalue` with every field class;
- `llvm.{s,u}{add,sub,mul}.with.overflow` for widths 1 to 64, and the
  add/sub forms wider than 64 bits.

## Refused, by name

- `a64:wide-division` (`aggr::WIDE_DIVISION`): division wider than 128 bits.
- `a64:wide-mul` / `a64:wide-shift`: `mul` and variable shifts wider than
  `aggr::MAX_QUADRATIC_BITS` (4096).
- `a64:wide-mul-overflow`: `{s,u}mul.with.overflow` wider than 64 bits.
- `a64:wide-fp-cast`: conversions between wide integers and floats.
- `a64:mem-vector`: element-wise operations on `Mem` vectors, a vector
  `icmp`, a vector `select` condition, vector `zext`/`sext`/`trunc`.
- `a64:atomic` (the target's name): an atomic `load`/`store` of a `Mem`
  value.
- `a64:mem-binary`, `a64:mem-freeze`, `a64:mem-cast`, `a64:mem-icmp`,
  `a64:mem-load`, `a64:mem-store`, `a64:mem-select`,
  `a64:extractvalue`, `a64:insertvalue`, `a64:overflow-intrinsic`:
  forms the dispatch should never send.

## Evidence

`tests/aggr.rs` with `tests/common/aggr_gen.rs`. Each function is run
from one C driver with hod (plain and `Stress::ALL`) and with `llc -O0
-global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` through a pointer wrapper (its result
stored between 16-byte sentinels; the bytes after each argument's store
size garbage), and, when its parameters and result are integers of at
most 128 bits, also called directly with random bits above the widths.
The expected image is `hod_interp`'s, else `llc`'s (wider than 128 bits,
vectors, aggregate parameters); images compare under masks from the
layouts (padding ignored; hod's integer padding bits must be zero),
and the layouts are checked against `hod_ir`'s.

- binary table (13 operations x 10 widths 65..256): 6,860 cases
  (4,030 against the interpreter, 2,790 against `llc` only);
- consumers of unextended results (`add`/`sub`/`mul`/`shl` feeding every
  consumer that extends): 6,528;
- compares, `select`, `freeze`, `phi`: 4,450;
- casts: 3,068; aggregates and `Mem` vectors (15 layouts): 1,341;
- overflow intrinsics (20 widths): 7,344; 400 random functions: 1,600.
- Of these, 13,923 calls with garbage above the widths in registers.
- `tests/diff.rs` with `i128` on: 645 of 1,000 programs agree, the rest
  skipped by `a64:load-fpr`/`a64:store-fpr` (WP-067); none disagree.
- Mutations caught (each reverted): the middle limbs' `adcs` turned into
  `adds` (4 suites fail); the extension of the top limb before a store
  dropped (6 suites); `asrv` for the limbs of a variable `ashr` (2).

## Not tested here

Calls of user functions with `Mem` arguments or results (WP-038): only
the function's own wide-integer parameters and results, and the runtime
calls, are exercised.
