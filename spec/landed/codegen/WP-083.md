# WP-083 — NEON target intrinsics

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/neon.rs` (the lowering), the encoder families
of `crates/hod_mc_a64/src/neon.rs`, 208 AArch64 members appended to
`hod_ir`'s intrinsic list with their matrix rows, the heading "NEON
intrinsics (WP-083)" in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1, "NEON intrinsics
(WP-083)" in `spec/mc-a64.md`, the AArch64 bullet of `spec/ir.md`'s list.

## What is lowered

201 of the 208 new members (every one but the seven bfloat ones), each
for every signature clang 22 or 19 emits for `<arm_neon.h>` and
`<arm_acle.h>`, and those of the corpus:

- integer and floating-point three same and two-register misc, vector
  and scalar (arithmetic, saturating, halving, rounding, register shifts,
  pairwise, `abs`/`sqabs`/`sqneg`/`cls`, reciprocal estimates, `fmulx`,
  `fabd`, `facge`/`facgt`, `frint32/64{x,z}`, the `fcvt*` conversions,
  also to general registers);
- widening and narrowing: `{s,u}addlp`, `sqxtn`/`uqxtn`/`sqxtun` and
  their scalar forms, `smull`/`umull`/`pmull`/`pmull64`/`sqdmull`,
  `raddhn`/`rsubhn`, `sqdmulls.scalar`, `fcvtxn`, `vcvtfp2hf`/
  `vcvthf2fp`;
- reductions: `{s,u}addv`, `{s,u}addlv`, `{s,u}{max,min}v`, `faddv`,
  `f{max,min}{,nm}v`, the two-element ones by the scalar pairwise forms;
- shifts by immediate: `vsli`/`vsri`, `rshrn`, `{s,u}q{,r}shr{,u}n`,
  `sqshlu`, the fixed-point `vcvtfp2fx*`/`vcvtfx*2fp`;
- `tbl1`..`tbl4`, `tbx1`..`tbx4`; `ld1x2`..`ld1x4`, `ld2`..`ld4`,
  `st1x2`..`st4`, `ld2lane`..`st4lane`, `ld2r`..`ld4r`;
- `sdot`/`udot`/`usdot`, `smmla`/`ummla`/`usmmla`, `fmlal`/`fmlsl`
  (and `2`), `vcmla.rot*`, `vcadd.rot*`;
- AES, SHA1, SHA256, SHA512, `eor3`, `bcax`, `rax1`, `xar`;
- `cls`, `cls64`, `dmb`, `dsb`, `isb`, `hint`.

Target features are not consulted (the `crc32` rule of WP-084).

## Hooks (outside `hod_a64/src/neon.rs`)

- `hod_a64/src/intrinsic.rs`: the last arm of `intrinsic` calls
  `crate::neon::intrinsic` (was `unsupported(INTRINSIC)`); `lib.rs`:
  `pub mod neon` and its row.
- `hod_ir/src/intrinsic.rs`: 208 members appended after
  `Aarch64Crc32cx`; `hod_ir/src/classify/matrix.rs`: their rows, as a
  slice `INTRINSIC_A64` after `INTRINSIC` (one array of both is a
  constant too large for clippy); `docs/status/support.md` blessed.
- `hod_ir/tests/body_llvm.rs`: a declaration per new member in
  `INTRINSICS`, and `UPGRADED_BY_22` for `bfcvt`, `bfcvtn`, `bfcvtn2`,
  which LLVM 22 upgrades away on reading (LLVM 19 keeps them).
- `hod_mc_a64/src/lib.rs`: `pub mod neon`; `tests/l1/harness.rs`:
  `Tools::find_with` and `Batch::check_with` (a major and extra
  features).

## What stays refused, by name

- `intrinsic:llvm.aarch64.neon.{bfcvt,bfcvtn,bfcvtn2,bfdot,bfmlalb,
  bfmlalt,bfmmla}`: the bfloat ones (`type:bfloat` is refused anyway);
  the encodings exist.
- `a64:intrinsic-target`: a signature no instruction takes.
- `a64:intrinsic-immediate`: a non-constant or out-of-range shift
  amount, lane or barrier option.
- Not on the list (`Other`, refused by `classify`): SM3/SM4, FP8,
  `famax`/`famin`, `vluti`, `ldxr`/`stxr` and friends, `clrex`.

## Evidence

- `hod_a64/tests/neon.rs::cases_match_llc`: 1410 functions (one per
  signature, immediate and element type, `tests/data/neon/cases.txt`,
  made by `cases.py` from the declarations clang 22 and 19 emit for
  `<arm_neon.h>`/`<arm_acle.h>` and the corpus), each called three times
  with random inputs from a C driver: llc 22 and llc 19
  (`-O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`) and hod (plain, stress, far) agree
  on all 4230 calls under every linker present. llc 19 sign-extends the
  `saddlv` i32 results of `v8i8`/`v16i8`; llc 22 zero-extends, as hod
  does (`LLVM19_DIFFERS`).
- `hod_mc_a64` L1: every new encoder family swept against llvm-mc 22
  and 19 (about 14 700 encodings).
- Refusals by name: `what_is_not_lowered_is_refused_by_name`,
  `bfloat_intrinsics_are_refused`.
- Mutations, each caught by `cases_match_llc` and reverted: the
  inserting shifts dropping the shifted operand (117 calls differ), the
  struct store not advancing (630), `gpr_size` taking `i64` for `W` (167).

## Corpus

Census, LLVM 22 / 19: compiled 3306 / 3269 before (`docs/status/
reasons.md` at the base), 3321 of 3347 / 3283 of 3308 after; of the
gain, 11 / 12 are this package's, the rest WP-082's `a64:vector-call`.
The AArch64 intrinsic first refusals went from 12 / 13 to the bfloat
ones only (`bfdot` 1 / `bfcvtn` 1); no `a64:intrinsic-target` or
`a64:intrinsic-immediate` anywhere. `corpus verify --strict -j 4` over
the 23 newly compiling modules (kiln `target.neon` 11 + 11, `target.acle`
1 + 1): 33 / 36 results, all PASS, under bfd, lld and mold (wild absent).
