# WP-300 — i386, design 0008 I9: x87 floating point

Landed: `crates/hod_x86/src/x87.rs` (behaviour: `spec/codegen-x86.md`
§11; written as §8, renumbered by the i386 join), its dispatch arms and one field of `FnState` (`x87`, the 80-bit
copies) in `crates/hod_x86/src/target.rs`; run tests
`crates/hod/tests/i386_x87.rs` with `crates/hod/tests/data/i386/x87/`.
`float`, `double`, `x86_fp80`: arithmetic, `fneg`, `freeze`, the sixteen
`fcmp` predicates (with and without `cmov`), `select` (`fcmov` or a
branch), `fpext`/`fptrunc`, conversions from and to integers of at most
32 bits (the control word around `fistp`), `sqrt`, `fabs`, `copysign`,
`fmuladd`, `frem` and the libm intrinsics as calls, constants as `mov`
immediates (no constant pool, so no `x86:pic` case of this package).

Refusals: `x86:float` remains only for a floating argument or result of
a call or a function (I6's ABI); new `x86:float:half`, `:bfloat`,
`:fp128`, `:sse` (a `float` operation with SSE, `double` with SSE2:
I12), `:i64-cast` (I7 with this module), `:intrinsic` (`double`
`minnum`/`maxnum`: `hod_cg` names no `fmin`/`fmax` routine; any other
floating intrinsic), `:cast` (`bitcast` of a floating value).

## The measured rule of `llc`

`llc` 19 and 22, `-O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`, `float` and
`double`, CPUs `generic` (no `cmov`) and `pentiumpro`, run under
`qemu-i386` with inputs where `a*b + c` is `2^-2k` at 80 bits and 0
rounded (`shapes.c`). 19 and 22 agree on every shape. A computed result
keeps 80 bits for every use in its block before the next barrier (any
number of uses, order, distance: `twice`, `nonlifo`, `far_int`,
`far_fp`, `sqrt_between`, `chain_use_twice`, `cmp_twice`); after a call
(`call`, `call_between`, `frem_between`) or in another block (`block`,
`block_and_local`, `phi_use`) it is rounded. Barriers found besides
calls and terminators: `memcpy`/`memmove` of more than 32 bytes and
`memset` of more than 64 (`memcpy_big`, `memset_big`; 32 and 64 are
inline: `memcpy_small`, `memset_small`), and without `cmov` a `select`
of variable operands (`intsel_between`; one of two integer constants is
arithmetic: `intsel_const`; `select_var`). Passing 80 bits on:
`freeze_use`, `fpext_use`, `fptrunc_use` (the DAG folds `fptrunc (fpext
x)`), `select_use` (`select i1 true`, folded) and `select_var` with
`fcmov`; `fneg_use`, `sitofp_var`, `fmuladd`, `stored`, `stored_after`,
`call_before`, `single`, `twice_sep` equal `llc` as well. Constant
operands are folded by the DAG in the type's precision (`sitofp_use`,
`fold_const`), and a folded `frem` is no call (`frem_const_between`);
probed by hand: `floor`, `ceil`, `trunc`, `minnum`, `maxnum`, `fma`,
`fmuladd`, `fabs`, `copysign` fold, `sqrt`, `sin`, `pow`, `exp2`, `rint`,
`round`, `nearbyint`, `roundeven` do not.

## hod's rule

One mechanism replaces the earlier "carried result" (a value left on the
x87 stack under window, depth and LIFO limits, which rounded 140 of 296
shape lines where `llc` does not): a computed result that is not folded
and has an instruction of its run (up to the next barrier) after it is
stored at 80 bits into a 12-byte slot besides its rounded home; uses in
the run load the slot. The x87 stack is empty between instructions.

## Divergence (documented, compared with a model)

`many6_lifo`, `many7_fifo`, `many7_lifo`, `many8_fifo`, `many8_lifo`,
`many9_fifo`, `many9_lifo`, `many12_fifo`, `many12_lifo`: six to twelve
products live at once; `llc` rounds some uses as its fast allocator
spills them (the first product always; then the first and last ones in
FIFO order, the middle ones in LIFO order), hod keeps all at 80 bits.
They compare with `llc` building the same arithmetic at `x86_fp80`
rounded at the store; every one of the 72 lines differs from `llc`'s.
`many6_fifo` equals `llc`. Not measured apart, stated in §11: a folded
`double` operation that hod computes at run time rounds twice.

## Remaining

- `double` `minnum`/`maxnum`: a `hod_cg` runtime routine entry
  (`fmin`/`fmax`, `crates/hod_cg/src/sym.rs`).
- `hod --mcpu=` does not reach `X86Features::of_function` (only the
  function's `target-cpu` attribute does).
- SSE (`x86:float:sse`, I12); floating arguments and results with I6's
  ABI; `x86:float:i64-cast` with I7's 64-bit integers.
- The barriers of instructions `hod_x86` does not lower yet (integer
  `min`/`max`, `ctlz`/`cttz`, saturating arithmetic: branches without
  `cmov`), added to `x87::barrier` by the package that lowers them.
