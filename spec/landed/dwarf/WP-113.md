# WP-113: call-site entries

The contract is `spec/dwarf.md` §6.19. Families 96 to 103 are appended
to §6.1's table (`CODE_END` 11471), §6.7 gains the call-site
normalization, and `spec/codegen.md` §9.5 gains `debug_calls`. This file
records what the work package did, what it measured and what it left.

## What `llc` writes (19 and 22, `-O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`)

Measured on clang 19, clang 22 (`-g -O1 -Xclang -disable-llvm-passes`,
then `opt -passes=always-inline`) and kiln++ (`-g -O0`, the flag added
to every definition) IR, on AArch64 and x86-64:

- `DW_AT_call_all_calls` after `frame_base`/`object_pointer`; the call
  sites last among the subprogram's children, in instruction order;
- direct: `call_origin`, `call_return_pc`; indirect:
  `call_target_clobbered` (`llc` 19: `call_target`) `DW_OP_reg<n>`;
  `musttail`: the origin or target, `call_tail_call`, `call_pc`;
- no `DW_TAG_call_site_parameter` at `-O0`;
- no site for a callee without a `DISubprogram`, an alias, an external
  symbol (libcalls, `__cxa_begin_catch`, `__tls_get_addr`);
- the origin is the callee's concrete DIE, made at the call if new (a
  member definition: its declaration in the class first); a declared
  callee's declaration DIE is made in its scope at the call (a namespace
  made then). An `always_inline` callee that is also called: the
  concrete DIE (the abstract instance is never the origin), identical in
  19 and 22;
- `LineTablesOnly` (`-gmlt -O1`): no DIE without inlined code; with
  inlined code the flag and call sites under the minimal subprograms and
  full declarations at the unit's level (refused,
  `debug:call-sites-line-tables`);
- DWARF 4: the GNU forms (refused, `debug:call-sites`).

## What landed

- `crates/hod_dwarf/src/call_sites.rs` (new): the plan's sites, the
  callee DIEs (`call_origin`, `Inline::mark_early`), the C declaration
  family, the writer of family 102, and the refusals.
- `crates/hod_cg/src/debug_calls.rs` (new): `DebugCallRow` in the
  chunk (`record.rs`), armed by `call.rs` `place_and_call`, completed by
  the targets, checked at `driver.rs` `finish_function`, turned into
  `hod_dwarf::CallSiteInput`s by the bridge (`emit/lines.rs`).
- One hook per target and kind: `hod_a64/src/target.rs` and
  `hod_x64/src/target.rs` `emit_call` (`call_returns_here`), and
  `hod_a64/src/frame.rs` and `hod_x64/src/frame.rs`, the tail-exit loop
  (`tail_branch_here`).
- A tail exit of a describing function gets a line row with the
  `musttail` call's location (`debug_calls.rs` `tail_branch_here`):
  lldb's artificial tail-call frame was at line 0.
- `debug:call-sites` is left only for a unit that is not DWARF 5.

## Evidence

- `crates/hod/tests/dwarf_call_sites.rs` `trees_equal_llc`: 18 trees
  (`calls.c`, `calls.cpp`, `tail.c` by clang 19, clang 22 and kiln++, on
  both targets) equal to `llc`'s after §6.7, every object `--verify`
  clean; each `call_return_pc` of BOTH writers is the end of a call
  instruction of the origin (by relocation) or through the target
  register, each `call_pc` a branch to it.
- `line_tables_only`, `split_dwarf` (as many sites in hod's `.dwo` as in
  `llc`'s), `synthetic_sites`.
- `crates/hod_a64/tests/dies_label.rs` `optimized_unit_matches_llc`:
  the flagged unit builds with its call site.
- `cargo xtask debug --linker bfd`: `tests/debug/call_sites.c`
  (AArch64; `bt` through `musttail`, `finish`): PASS clang 19 gdb and
  lldb, clang 22 lldb (lldb's artificial `hop` frame equal to `llc`'s),
  XFAIL clang 22 gdb (key instructions, below); `call_sites_direct.c`
  (clang 22, `-gno-key-instructions`): PASS on AArch64 and x86-64 under
  gdb and lldb; `optimized_unit.c` (no longer XFAIL): PASS, 4 of 4 on
  each target.
- Three mutations, each caught by `dwarf_call_sites`: a return address
  one instruction late (not a call ends there), the origin made the
  caller (the relocation names another function), and the families
  without the flag (the abbreviation no longer matches: `--verify`).
- Throughput (`throughput`, ignored; release, AArch64, best of 5): 2000
  functions of 8 calls, 16000 sites: 7.42 ms against 4.98 ms without the
  flag, 153 ns per call-site DIE; 4000 functions, 32000 sites: 15.59 ms
  against 9.77 ms, 182 ns. Linear within noise (2.1x for 2x).
- The corpus: the only modules with the flag are kiln's
  `lifetime-markers-O2-g-fpic-run.cpp` (LLVM 19 and 22); their next
  refusal is `debug:dbg-value` (an `-O2` unit).

## Found elsewhere

- clang 22's `-O1` IR has `keyInstructions: true`; `llc` 22 then sets
  `is_stmt` from `atomGroup`/`atomRank` (a row at each return address
  whose store is a key instruction). hod ignores them, so gdb's `finish`
  stops mid-line (`0x? in middle`). With `-gno-key-instructions` the
  line tables have the same `is_stmt` rows (12 and 12).
- The facade declines every `musttail` call on x86-64
  (`musttail:stack-args-unknown`, `crates/hod/src/session.rs`
  `classifier_for`: no x86-64 stack-argument rule), so tail calls on
  x86-64 are tested through `hod_x64::compile_to_object` only.
- `xtask/src/debug/transcript.rs:188` slices a line at a byte index that
  can fall inside a multi-byte character (a `§` in a test's source line
  shown by gdb): the harness panics. The new tests' sources are ASCII.

## Left

- `DW_AT_call_target` of a call through memory (`llc` writes a
  `DW_OP_breg` location for a call through a stack slot): hod's indirect
  calls always go through a register, so it does not arise.
- `debug:call-sites-line-tables`: writing the minimal subprograms'
  sites and the unit-level declarations.
- DWARF 4's GNU call sites after WP-114.
