# WP-151 — x86-64 text size, short frame displacements

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/common.rs` (`bias_local_sites` writes a
local's displacement that fits a signed byte as `disp8` and cuts the
three bytes through the shrink pass), doc comments in `common.rs` and
`frame.rs`; `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.17 (new) and the sentences of §5.6
(frame places) and §5.6.15 (`FnCx::cut`) that changed. Tests:
`crates/hod_x64/tests/text_size.rs` (the frame shape in
`check_shapes`, `frame_operands_at_the_disp8_boundary_agree_with_llc`).
`hod_cg` is unchanged: the cuts are ordinary `FnCx::cut` ranges.

## Size

The corpus `/Volumes/AgentWork/hod-helper/.build/corpus-x64`: its 2,897
single-module LLVM 22 text entries, of which hod builds 2,881 under
`--strict` with each entry's `llc_args`; text is every `SHF_EXECINSTR`
section, hod over `llc` 22 (5,897,575 bytes), as WP-146 measured it.

| | hod bytes | overall | ratio p10 / p50 / p90 / p99 | hod bytes p10 / p50 / p90 / p99 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| before (main 3a98566) | 9,024,531 | 1.530 | 1.42 / 1.68 / 2.00 / 3.30 | 122 / 350 / 1,606 / 10,502 |
| 1. disp8 | 8,261,887 | 1.401 | 1.23 / 1.44 / 1.70 / 2.82 | 109 / 294 / 1,354 / 9,699 |

Cut 1 takes 762,644 bytes (8.5%); per module the text is 0.78 / 0.85 /
0.96 of what it was (p10 / p50 / p90). It saves a little more than 3
bytes per site: branches over frame accesses that reached only once
those were short are relaxed too (§5.6.15).

## Speed and memory

hod's CPU time (user + system, `wait4`) and peak RSS, one process per
module, `HOD_THREADS=1`, 4 at once, shared machine:

| | CPU, all 2,881 modules | max RSS | CPU, 20 largest (min of 5) | max RSS there |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| before | 9.9 s | 77,376 KB | 0.657 s | 80,804 KB |
| cut 1 | 9.8 s | 76,572 KB | 0.663 s | 78,820 KB |

No measurable cost on the corpus (the whole-corpus CPU varies by more
between runs of one binary). The largest module
(`kiln/llvm22/stress/cleanup-scopes-30k`, one function of 3.2 MB of
code) has 26 cuts and 30,000 relaxable branches: its locals are far
from `rbp`. A synthetic function of N local accesses (16 `i64` slots
near `rbp`, blocks of four stores and four loads ending in a
conditional branch three or twenty blocks ahead; 118,742 cuts and
12,499 relaxable branches at 100k, 9,375 of them relaxed):

| | 100k accesses | 200k | 200k / 100k |
|---|---|---|---|
| before, CPU (min of 5) | 0.0652 s | 0.1269 s | 1.95 |
| cut 1, CPU | 0.0693 s | 0.1339 s | 1.93 |
| before, peak RSS | 16,564 KB | 32,688 KB | |
| cut 1, peak RSS | 18,696 KB | 36,952 KB | |

The pass stays linear: a binary search per mapped offset, the ranges
merged once per pass. Its cost on a function that is nothing but frame
accesses is 5 to 6% of hod's time and about 20 bytes per cut (the
`(at, len)` of the cut, the `Edits` range and its prefix sum).
Relaxation passes (`MAX_PASSES` 8) on the corpus after the cut: 9,884
functions take 1, 14,119 take 2, 154 take 3, 9 take 4, none more; the
largest function takes 2.

## Dropped

1. **A use-ordered frame layout.** Upper bound from the census below
   (per function, each displacement's uses, the most used packed first
   below the save area at their widest access): 5,564 `disp32`
   operands would become `disp8` and 2,423 `disp8` ones `disp32`, at
   most 9,423 bytes, 0.74% of the sample's text. It needs a layout pass
   in `hod_cg` (slot sizes, alignment, the variable rows); not worth it.
2. **Store then reload.** Pairs `mov [rbp - d], r` · `mov`/`movzx r',
   [rbp - d]` of the same width: 6,659 in the sample, of which 2,874
   (43%) have the load as a branch target. Of the other 3,785, the load
   could go (same register, 8 bytes: 836) or become a register move;
   6,881 bytes at most, 0.54%. The x86-64 lowering cannot tell whether
   a label is bound between the two without a new query on `hod_cg`'s
   labels, and `spec/codegen.md` §4 is not changed for one target.

## What is left

The census (`llvm-objdump` per instruction, every third module: 961
modules, 1,267,758 bytes after the cut, 1,461,085 before) by
normalized shape (`R` a register, `rip` included; `I` a number;
`[R-D]` a frame operand), share of hod's text: `lea R, [R]`
(RIP-relative, relocated) 10.9%, `mov R, dword [rbp - d]` 6.9%,
`mov R, R` 6.7%, `call` 6.6%, `cmp R, I` 4.5%, `mov R, qword [rbp - d]`
3.8%, `lea R, [rbp - d]` 3.7%, stores of registers to slots 5.9%,
`movzx R, R` 3.1%, `movsxd R, R` 2.7%, `ud2` 2.6%, `push`/`pop` 4.4%,
stores of immediates to slots 2.1%. Of the frame operands 8,805 still
need `disp32` (12% of 72,921). The remaining gap to `llc` (1.401) is
the value-location policy's: every value goes through its slot and a
register move, and extensions are explicit.
