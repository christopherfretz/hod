# WP-075 — assembly that needs no assembler

Landed:
- `spec/ir.md` §2.3 "Assembly text that needs no assembler": the reader
  of module assembly made of symbol directives and the empty-template
  test (`crates/hod_ir/src/asm_text.rs`); `classify` rows
  `module:asm-symbols` and `call:inline-asm-empty` (AArch64 on).
- `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1 "Assembly that needs no assembler (WP-075)":
  `hod_a64::barrier`, empty-template inline assembly lowered by its
  constraints as `llc -O0` does.
- `spec/codegen.md` §9.7.1: `emit_module` writes the directives' symbol
  effects (`crates/hod_cg/src/emit/asm.rs`).

## Implemented

| what | where |
|---|---|
| `.globl`/`.global`, `.weak`, `.hidden`, `.protected`, `.type` (`@`/`%`/`STT_` function, object, notype), comma lists, `;`, comments, blank lines | `hod_ir::read_module_asm` |
| each name checked against the module (table in `spec/ir.md` §2.3): new undefined symbols, binding of declarations, visibility unless the module's wins, type of declarations, `retain` of named declarations | `hod_ir::module_asm_effects`, `hod_cg::emit::asm` |
| empty templates (no `$`, only blank statements and comments) | `hod_ir::asm_template_is_empty` |
| barriers: clobbers of memory, flags and named registers; `r`/`m`/`i`/`n`/`w`/`g`/`X`/`{reg}`/digit codes; `\|` alternatives by LLVM's weights; tied direct output = the input; tied register-class indirect output = a store of the input; memory-only indirect output = nothing; all four flags; `invoke` | `hod_a64::barrier` |

## Refused, by name

- `module:asm`: labels, instructions, `.set`/`=`, `.size`, `.symver`
  (no real input uses either), `.internal` (no field in `hod_obj`),
  section directives, quoted names, `.L` names, `.globl`+`.weak` of one
  name, two types, and what `llc` warns about, fails on, or settles by
  precedence (`.weak` of a strong definition; `.globl` of a weak,
  `linkonce` or `extern_weak` symbol; any directive on a local symbol;
  `.type` that disagrees with a definition or names a TLS variable;
  `.weak`/`.globl` of a common variable).
- `call:inline-asm`: a template that is not empty (`nop`, `$$`).
- `a64:inline-asm-output` (an untied output, or an untied mixed
  register/memory indirect output), `a64:inline-asm-outputs` (two
  effects, a struct result), `a64:inline-asm-constraint` (`!i`, `Q`,
  tied outputs, misordered operands), `a64:inline-asm-clobber` (`sp`,
  `x31`, x86 names), `a64:inline-asm-operand` (`i` of a non-constant,
  type mismatches), `a64:inline-asm-bundle`.

## Evidence

- `crates/hod_cg/tests/emit_asm.rs`: 37 directive modules, sections,
  symbols and relocations equal to `llc` 19 and 22 (which compile each
  without a diagnostic); 20 modules refused as `module:asm` by
  `emit_module` and by `classify`.
- `crates/hod_a64/tests/barrier.rs`: 30 functions of every corpus
  constraint form and each alternative/tie rule measured from `llc`,
  built by hod and by `llc` 19 and 22, linked with a C driver under
  bfd, lld and mold, dynamic and `-static`: `checked, 0 failures`; 14
  refusals by name. `hod_interp` refuses `call:inline-asm`, so it is not
  an oracle here.
- `crates/hod_cli/tests/kiln_e2e.rs`: `iostream-init` (a static
  constructor that prints through `std::cout`, which needs libstdc++'s
  `.globl _ZSt21ios_base_library_initv`) and `weak-hook` (`asm(".weak
  ...")` of an undefined function) through the kiln shim under every
  linker, and `-static`. Every iostream program of the test now goes
  through hod.
- `classify`: an `elementtype` on an inline-assembly argument is no
  `attr:elementtype` row (`classify/walk.rs`, `param_attrs_slow`);
  `tests/barrier.rs` `classify_accepts_every_barrier`.
- Corpus (`cargo xtask corpus reasons`, strict, native bitcode), first
  refusal before (main) and after: compiled 3068 -> 3138 (LLVM 22) and
  3034 -> 3104 (LLVM 19); `call:inline-asm` 74 -> 2, `module:asm` 4 -> 3
  per major; 79 modules per major have an empty template, 69 of them
  compile, the rest stop at other constructs. Left: `call:inline-asm`
  (`dcl.asm/run.cpp`, real templates), `a64:inline-asm-operand`
  (`stkalign.c`: `=*imr` tied to a `ptr` input, an `i32` output),
  `module:asm` (real assembly). `corpus census` is lexical and does not
  change.
- `cargo xtask corpus verify --strict -j 4`: every module that newly
  compiles passes under bfd, lld and mold; the only non-decline failure
  of the corpus (`pr79286.c`, a link error, no assembly) fails on other
  branches too.
- 24 iostream/library programs (libstdc++ and libc++, -O0 and -O1, 96
  translation units) through `hod` strict: 77 compile; the next
  refusals are `inst:atomicrmw` 6, `attr:convergent` 5,
  `mem:atomic-load` 4, `operand:int-of-addr` 3,
  `operand:address-in-aggregate` 1.

## Mutations (each caught, reverted)

1. `emit/asm.rs`: a `.weak` binding written as global — `emit_asm`
   fails on every weak case.
2. `barrier.rs`: alternative choice disabled (always the first) —
   `barrier` fails `t_rm`, `t_rm_0i`.
3. `barrier.rs`: a direct result taken from the first argument instead
   of the tied input — `barrier` fails `d_mid`.
