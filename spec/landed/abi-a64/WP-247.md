# WP-247: `win64cc` and `aarch64_vector_pcs` on AArch64 Linux

Landed: `win64cc` (`__attribute__((ms_abi))`) and `aarch64_vector_pcs`
definitions, direct and indirect calls and `invoke`s on AArch64 Linux,
in both directions with the other conventions, as LLVM's
`CC_AArch64_Win64PCS`, `CC_AArch64_Win64_VarArg`, `CSR_AArch64_AAPCS_X18`
and `CSR_AArch64_AAVPCS` say for a Linux triple and `llc` 19 and 22
confirm (`spec/abi-a64.md` §13). Eight constructs refused by name
(§13.6). With WP-246 (x86-64), `cc:win64cc` is supported on both targets.

## What it did

- `hod_a64::abi::assign`: both conventions are assigned as `ccc`, but a
  variadic `win64cc` call, whose `float`s and `double`s (fixed arguments
  too) travel as integers of their bits: a lone one as a one-lane part
  `vcall::lanes_out` moves with `umov`, an aggregate's members as `W`
  and `X` pieces. `nest` in `win64cc` and `half`/`bfloat` in a variadic
  `win64cc` call are refused.
- `hod_a64::cconv` (new): the save plan of the conventions that keep
  more than AAPCS64 (`win64cc`: `x18`; `aarch64_vector_pcs`: `q8`..`q23`
  whole; `preserve_allcc`'s, moved from `tail3`), stored after the frame
  record with CFI rows, reloaded in every exit; the function-level
  refusals (variadic definitions, `musttail`, coroutine bodies).
- `hod_ir::classify`: `cc:win64cc` is `supported` on AArch64 (was
  `not-implemented`), `cc:aarch64_vector_pcs` `supported` (was `todo`);
  `docs/status/support.md` blessed.

## Test evidence

- The ABI grid (`tests/abi`): both conventions' cases in
  `conventions()`, among them five variadic `win64cc` signatures, in
  the four configurations (`llc` 19 and 22, `-O0` and `-O2`), hod
  calling and LLVM calling, executed: 4430 signatures. With the
  variadic rule off, 25 checks fail.
- `tests/cconv.rs`, every linker (bfd, lld, mold, wild):
  `callees_keep_what_llc_keeps` (callees by hod plain, stressed and far
  and by `llc` 22 and 19; `x18`..`x27` and `q8`..`q31` checked after a
  `bl`, `x18` through the unwinder, CFI register sets equal to `llc`'s;
  saving `x17` in place of `x18` crashes the program);
  `calls_in_both_directions` (13 pairings of hod's and `llc`'s callers
  and callees, `llc`'s variadic `v_sum` and `v_sum2`);
  `exceptions_through_such_frames` (15 pairings);
  `corpus_module_runs` (`dcl.attr.cconv/cc-run.cpp`'s module unedited,
  by hod and `llc` 22: `pm 21 pa 3.5 ms 60 msr 44 v 7 l 9` / `t 42`);
  `refusals_are_named`. `coro_body`'s refusal table names the two
  coroutine refusals.
