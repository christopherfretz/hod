# WP-297 (design 0008 I6, the i386 ABI): parked after session 1

## Done (committed on `wp/297-i386-abi`)

- `crates/hod_x86/src/abi.rs`: `assign` for C, `coldcc` (= C on i386,
  llc probe) and `fastcc` without floating point: 4-byte stack slots
  (`double` 8 at alignment 4, `x86_fp80` 12 with a 10-byte part), `i64`
  as two words, IR aggregate arguments leaf by leaf (one slot per leaf,
  contiguous parts merged), `inreg` (`eax`, `edx`, `ecx`, a word at a
  time: an `i64` splits `ecx` + stack and later `inreg` go to the stack),
  `fastcc` (`ecx`, `edx`, leaves of aggregates too, `i64` split `edx` +
  stack), `nest` (`ecx`; `eax` under `fastcc`), `byval` at its alignment
  (max 4, refused above 16; llc 19 and 22 DO move the offset to 16 for
  `align 16`: `spec/abi-x86.md` §3.2 says otherwise and must be fixed),
  `sret` (param 0 only; stack: callee `ret $4` and `mov eax, [ebp+8]`;
  `inreg`: `eax`, saved in a slot by `frame::begin`), results in `eax`,
  `edx:eax`, `eax`/`edx`/`ecx` for aggregates of up to three words,
  `st0` for one floating-point leaf (`abi::ST0` = PReg 16, glue
  `fld`/`fstp` in `abi::load_st0`/`store_st0`), larger results and
  `i128`/`fp128` returned in memory (caller side only; a DEFINITION that
  returns in memory stays `x86:aggregate`, I11). Varargs calls: all on
  the stack (inreg refused in a variadic signature).
- Callee pop for the caller: `abi::last_call()` (thread-local set by
  `assign`, read by `frame::emit_call`, which emits `lea esp,[esp-4]`
  after the call). Reason: `CallEmit` carries nothing about the callee
  pop and `hod_cg` is not ours; the proper hook is a `callee_pop` field
  in `Assignment`/`CallEmit` (hand-back item). An indirect call with an
  argument in `edx` (the callee register) is refused `x86:abi:indirect-edx`.
- New refusal names: `x86:abi:wide-arg` (i128/fp128 arguments, 19/22
  differ), `x86:abi:x87-pair` (two FP leaves in a result),
  `x86:abi:indirect-edx`; `x86:abi:cc` remains for every other
  convention and for `fastcc` with floating point / sret / in-memory
  results; `x86:abi:attr` for inalloca, preallocated, alignstack, Swift,
  sret not first, inreg on FP/aggregate/variadic, byval align > 16.
- Classifier: `SupportRow` has an `i386` field (x86-64's by default,
  `on_i386` overrides): `cc:coldcc`, `attr:inreg`, `attr:nest` are ON for
  i386 (`crates/hod_ir/src/classify/matrix.rs`). The doc
  (`docs/status/support.md`, `classify/report.rs`) does NOT show the
  column yet.
- Grid `crates/hod/tests/i386_abi.rs`: 103 signatures (96 generated by
  a fixed LCG + 7 fixed rows), forwarders compiled by hod (facade and
  `Stress::ALL`) between an llc `main` and llc checkers, llc 19 and 22:
  4 runs, all print `ok`. Both directions per signature.

## Decisions and reasons

- Per-word register rule reproduces llc's split of an `i64` (probe in
  `.build/target-wp-297/probe/p.ll`, f1/f2/f8).
- Two FP leaves refused rather than ordered: the call sequence moves
  result parts in one order on both sides, the x87 stack needs opposite
  ones.
- Edits outside `abi.rs`: `frame.rs` (`FrameState::sret_slot`, `begin`,
  `end`, `emit_call`: functions the table gives I6), `target.rs`
  `load`/`store` (one `ST0` arm each), `classify/matrix.rs` (the column).
  One source edit was made with `sed` (a test constant in abi.rs) before
  noticing the rule; no others.
