# WP-210 — libc++ through hod with `-g`, and the refusals it named

Spec: `spec/dwarf.md` §6.28 (a static member's value wider than 64 bits)
and §6.29 (an inlined subprogram's retained variables). Abbreviation
families taken: 115 (`fam::STATIC_VARIABLE_WIDE`, 16 codes from 14598)
and 116 (`fam::STATIC_MEMBER_WIDE`, 16 codes from 14614); `CODE_END`
14630. Measurement: `docs/status/libcxx-hod-debug.md` and
`docs/status/libcxx-hod-debug-x64.md`.

## What was built

- `hod_dwarf` `types.rs`: a static member's `i128` or `long double`
  constant is a `block1` of its bytes, least significant first, as
  `llc`'s `addIntAsBlock` writes it (`StaticValue::Block`), in families
  115 (DWARF 5, `DW_TAG_variable`) and 116 (DWARF 4, `DW_TAG_member`),
  whose slots are 54's and 55's; `DW_FORM_block1` (`form::BLOCK1`).
  Before, `debug:die-unsupported` ("a static member's value wider than
  64 bits").
- `hod_dwarf` `inline.rs`: `retained_entities` makes, before the
  abstract instances are sorted, the abstract entity of every retained
  variable (class by argument number, else in retained order) and label
  of an inlined subprogram's scopes, in `llc` 22's
  `endFunctionImpl` order. A function also placed out of line writes the
  concrete DIE of a retained variable as an `abstract_origin` only
  (families 111 and 112, mask 0; `retained_of_abstract`). Still
  `debug:retained-nodes`: an import scoped in a block of an inlined
  subprogram, and a retained import or label of one also placed out of
  line. `import.rs` imports only `DIImportedEntity` nodes of an abstract
  instance; `dwarf4.rs` checks 111 and 112 with mask 0, and 115 and 116,
  against `llc`'s version 4 output.
- `xtask` `debug trees`: a tool's output past the 16 MiB capture cap is
  an error, not a truncated tree; DIE trees go through files
  (`llvm-dwarfdump -o`) and relocations are listed from
  `.rela.debug_info` only.
- `scripts/`: `libcxx-debug-kiln.sh` (adds `-g` in front of a compiler
  chain), `libcxx-hod-debug.sh` (control, hybrid, strict and shard merge,
  both targets), `libcxx-debug-refused.sh` (a run's refused modules with
  hod's detail), `libcxx-debug-sample.sh` (hod-built modules sampled for
  `debug trees`).

## Evidence

- `crates/hod/tests/debug_libcxx_wp210.rs`: wide static members (`i128`,
  `unsigned __int128`, `long double`) on both targets, DWARF 5 and 4,
  against `llc` 22's DIE trees; retained parameters and variables of an
  inlined function, only inlined (with a retained label) and also out of
  line (before or after its caller), both targets, DWARF 5 and 4,
  against `llc` 22.
- The two refusal tests that named the removed cases now name cases that
  stay refused (`debug_real_projects_wp195.rs`
  `other_retained_nodes_of_inlined_functions_are_refused`,
  `hod_a64` `dies_inline.rs` `refusals_are_named`); no host-conditional
  expectation named them.
- `cargo xtask debug trees` with hod at `dd90c23a`: twelve retained-node
  variants (only inlined, function first, caller first; DWARF 4 and 5;
  both targets) 12 same; the 55 libc++ modules with a wide member 1 same,
  54 permitted, 0 differ; 240 sampled libc++ modules 26 same, 213
  permitted, 1 differs (WP-221), every object `--verify` clean.
- libc++ `-g` native: all 55 wide-member modules and the retained-nodes
  module build strict; the measurement is the status page above.
- libc++ `-g` x86-64 (`docs/status/libcxx-hod-debug-x64.md`), hod
  `3b14f8f`: a compile-only census of the whole suite builds 7224 of 7226
  modules by hod (refused: PR28469's `debug:dbg-value-fragment`, written
  by WP-225, PR28469 then passing strict under qemu with hod `46076a3`,
  and kiln's `malformed-input` in `not_fn.nttp`); shard 1/20
  under qemu, control, hybrid and strict, has 0 differences test by test;
  240 sampled modules under `debug trees`: 175 same, 64 permitted, 1
  differs (`llc`'s: LLVM merges the same-named members of a pack
  capture), every object `--verify` clean.
