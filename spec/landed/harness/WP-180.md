# WP-180 — LLVM for x86-64 through kiln and hod, cross-built on AArch64

Landed: LLVM 19.1.7 built for `x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu` on the AArch64
development host by kiln with hod at `-O0` (assertions, no `-g`, static
libraries, `X86;AArch64`), every translation unit of `ninja all` and of
the unit tests compiled, the refusal census written, and the unit tests
and three `check-llvm` slices run under `qemu-x86_64` against the same
tree built by kiln with `llc`. The recipe is `docs/VALIDATION.md` "Real
projects for x86-64 on the AArch64 host"; the scripts are
`scripts/oss/llvm-x64/` (WP-178's `scripts/oss/llvm/` with the cross
settings). Measured with hod at `1bc3210` (wave 11 with WP-173) and kiln
at the workspace's pin.

## Census (hod tree, fallback allowed, `HOD_CENSUS=1`)

| stage | targets | TUs compiled (cumulative) | built by hod | declined | not offered |

| s2 | `llc`, `opt`, `FileCheck`, `not`, `count`, the lit tools | 1827 | 1818 | 4 | 5 |

| s4 | every object of `all` (compile only) | 2799 | 2788 | 6 | 5 |

Not offered: the four BLAKE3 `.S` files (assembled by kiln) and
`EntryExitInstrumenter.cpp` (kiln's statistics trigger
`instrument-entry`: kiln keeps that job on its LLVM back end).

Declined, by first reason (every reason of the module in brackets):

| reason | modules | where |
|---|---:|---|
| `call:inline-asm` | 4 (6) | `cpuid`/`xgetbv` in BLAKE3's dispatcher and `TargetParser/Host.cpp`, valgrind.h's client request in `Support/Valgrind.cpp`, `leaq sym@tpoff` in `llvm-rtdyld.cpp`; also `int3` in gtest and `fwait` in llvm-exegesis |
| `attr:disable-tail-calls` | 1 (1) | `gtest-all.cc` (WP-179) |
| `intrinsic:llvm.x86.fxsave64` | 1 | llvm-exegesis `X86/Target.cpp`, also `fxrstor64`, `flags.read.u64`, `flags.write.u64` |

Every module is saved under `.build/findings/oss/llvm-x64/<reason>/`
with its command line and hod's message, with a smallest reproducer per
new name (`cpuid-min.ll`, `x86-state-min.ll`, `disable-tail-calls-min.ll`).
Against WP-178's native AArch64 census, the x86-64 build adds the
x86-specific inline assembly and the four exegesis intrinsics; nothing
else.

## Cost

Disk: 4.7 GB (hod tree), 3.2 GB (control), 25 MB (frozen host tools).
Links of `all` were not run (about 100 static -O0 executables of up to
240 MB each would pass the 20 GB budget); only the tools and unit tests
above were linked.
