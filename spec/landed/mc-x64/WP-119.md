# WP-119 — x87 forms and the byte-register REX test

Landed: `spec/mc-x64.md` section "x87" (design 0006 section 5, row E1).

## What it did

- `src/x87.rs` (new): `St`, `X87Size` and 55 constructors (5 with a
  checked twin: `fst_m`, `fild_m`, `fist_m`, `fistp_m`, `fcmov`): `fld`,
  `fst`, `fstp` on memory of the three formats and on `st(i)`; `fild`,
  `fist`, `fistp`; the six arithmetic operations in the m32/m64, `_0i`,
  `_i0` and `p` forms; `fchs`, `fabs`, `fsqrt`, `fprem`, `fprem1`,
  `frndint`, `fxch`, `fldz`, `fld1`; `fucomi`, `fucomip`, `fcomi`,
  `fcomip`, `fucom`, `fucomp`, `fucompp`, `fnstsw_ax`; `fcmov`;
  `fnstcw_m`, `fldcw_m`, `fnclex`, `fninit`. `lib.rs`: one appended
  `pub mod`/`pub use` block.
- `tests/l1`: groups `x87`, `x87-memory-operands`, `x87-llc-text`; the
  `byte_regs` tests; 55 rows in `table.rs`.

## Evidence

- 54,534 new encodings (930 + 53,560 + 44) equal `llvm-mc`'s bytes and
  disassemble as the table says, at LLVM 22 and 19; the crate's sweeps
  total 321,050 encodings. The 39 new rejections all have text and all
  are errors to `llvm-mc` at both majors.
- Every x87 instruction of `docs/design/0006-data/abi/03-x87.llc*.s`, and
  those `llc -O0` writes for the other `x86_fp80` operations, assembled
  from `llc`'s own AT&T text, equals hod's bytes.
- `byte_regs`: 820 byte-register instructions with an operand numbered 4
  to 7 all start (after any `66`) with exactly `40`; the 0-to-3 forms
  have no REX; LLVM 22 and 19 disassemble every one with `spl`..`dil`
  and none with `ah`..`bh`. No form of the crate was wrong.
- Mutations, each caught and reverted: `fsubp` encoded as `DE E0+i`
  (16 disagreements in `x87`, 2 in `x87-llc-text`); `rdi` dropped from
  the registers that need REX as a byte (both `byte_regs` tests: `add
  bh, 0x1`).

## Decisions, and why

- **Names and the table follow the SDM's meaning**, which LLVM's Intel
  syntax (assembler and disassembler) shares; the spec states what each
  form computes, and the AT&T swap of `fsub`/`fsubr` and `fdiv`/`fdivr`
  in the `st(i)`-destination forms is documented and pinned by the
  `x87-llc-text` group, which uses `llc`'s AT&T text verbatim.
- **`_i0` with `St0` writes the `D8` form**, which computes the same and
  is what LLVM assembles for that text, so every encoding still equals
  LLVM's.
- **The width of a real memory operand is `X87Size`**, a new type,
  because `FpSize` has no 80-bit member and the arithmetic forms (which
  have none either) keep `FpSize`; integer widths are `Size`, checked.
- **`fcmov` takes a `Cond`**, checked to the eight that exist, as
  `setcc_r` and `cmovcc_rr` do.
- **`fisttp` is left out**: it is SSE3, outside the baseline, and `llc`
  does not use it.

## Gaps

- In the section's "Not provided": integer-operand arithmetic,
  memory-operand `fcom`, `fnstsw m16`, the environment and transcendental
  instructions, the waiting forms.
- `hod_x64` does not use the module yet (WP-131).
