# WP-350: coroutines in the corpus

Landed: `corpus capture --route hod`, which keeps the module kiln gives
hod on its own route (before `opt`, coroutines unsplit) as an `input:
pre-opt` entry; a suite of coroutine programs for it
(`tests/corpus-coro/hod.coro/`); a coroutine corpus captured with it for
both targets (`.build/corpus-coro/`, not committed); eight unsplit
coroutine seeds in `tests/ir/coro/`.

## Why the corpora had no unsplit coroutine

The capture stands a script where `llc` stands (`kiln.rs`,
`CAPTURE_SCRIPT`) and keeps what kiln gives `llc`. For a module that
declares an `llvm.coro.*` intrinsic, kiln's driver runs `opt`
(`coro-early,cgscc(coro-split),coro-cleanup,globaldce`;
`kiln_driver/src/codegen_cmd.rs`, `ir_passes`, `needs_coro_lowering`)
and gives `llc` bitcode, which the capture writes as text with
`llvm-dis` (`; delivered: bitcode`). On the hod route (`-fkiln-backend=hod`)
kiln gives hod the module before `opt` (`hod_takes`: the triggers
`alwaysinline` and `coro` are hod's), so hod lowers coroutines itself
(`spec/codegen.md` §7.6), and no corpus module showed that.

Counts (kiln 8f93304e, the corpora's revision): 51 files under kiln's
three capture roots use `co_await`/`co_yield`/`co_return`; 27 are `run`
tests (17 `compile-fail`, 1 `compile-ok`, 1 `ir`, 5 without `expect`).
Of the 27, 22 are in each corpus (44 entries per target, LLVM 19 and
22), all `; delivered: bitcode`, split; 4 are skipped `needs-env`, 1
`marked-xfail` (`gro-throws-frame-slots-asan-run.cpp`). None was refused
at admission. The AArch64 corpus has 210 bitcode entries in all (coroutines
and `always_inline`).

## The hod route of the capture

`capture_hod_script` (`xtask/src/corpus/kiln.rs`): kiln++ runs it as hod
(`KILN_HOD`); it saves the arguments without `--strict` and the standard
input into `llc-<pid>` with a marker file `hod`, then runs the group's
`opt -passes=default<O0>` and the real `llc` with the oracle's selector:
the oracle's build of an `input: pre-opt` test, so the program whose
behaviour becomes the expectations is the reference's. A module kiln's
fallback gave `llc` is `hod-route-declined`. Entries: `Delivered::PreOpt`
(`; delivered: pre-opt`), `Recipe::pre_opt` writes `;@ input: pre-opt`.
Contract: `spec/harness.md` "The hod route".

## The coroutine corpus

Suite: kiln's 27 coroutine `run` tests (copied with their `Inputs/`) and
30 programs of `tests/corpus-coro/hod.coro/`: six programs (generator
with by-value, by-address and reference parameters; tasks with symmetric
transfer; exceptions out of and inside a coroutine; coroutine lambdas;
a frame with arrays and an escaped address; more arguments than argument
registers) in five variants (plain PIC, `-g`,
`-fstack-protector-strong`, both, `-fno-pic -no-pie`).
