# WP-204 — x86-64 assembly left refused

Landed: module-level assembly on x86-64 (`crates/hod_asm/src/module.rs`
`assemble_module_x64`, `crates/hod_cg/src/emit/module_asm.rs`, `asm::check`
in `crates/hod_cg/src/emit/asm.rs`), `.symver` on x86-64 (AArch64 still
refuses it, its objects unchanged), calls and
branches to a symbol (`crates/hod_asm/src/x64/mod.rs` `jump_kind`), `ret`,
`leave` and the x87 instructions without operands (`x64/encode.rs`, their
encoders in `crates/hod_mc_x64/src/x87.rs`), the x87 operands `{st}` and
`{st(1)}` and empty templates through the template lowering
(`crates/hod_x64/src/barrier/template.rs`, `barrier.rs`), the classify row
`module:asm` on for x86-64 (`crates/hod_ir/src/classify/matrix.rs`,
`docs/status/support.md`), `spec/asm.md` §14 (§13 in counts). Tests:
`crates/hod_asm/tests/llvm_mc_x64.rs` (the branch rows, sorted relocation
sets), `crates/hod_cg/tests/emit_module_asm.rs` (the x86-64 `llc`
comparison), `crates/hod_cg/tests/emit_asm.rs` and
`crates/hod_ir/tests/classify_matrix.rs` (x86-64 rows),
`crates/hod_x64/tests/inline_asm.rs` (x87 and call runs,
refusals), `crates/hod_x64/tests/barrier.rs`, `crates/hod_mc_x64/tests/l1`.

## Step 0: the refusals

The x86-64 corpus, `hod --strict`, at the base (`031a3d5`):
`module:asm` 2 modules, `x64:inline-asm` 2 (`asm goto`),
`x64:inline-asm-operand` 4 (`stkalign` 2, `run.cpp` 2),
`asm:constraint/st` 2 (`990413-2`); the rest of the failures (10,
"metadata defined twice") are not assembly. At the head every one of
these modules but the two `asm goto` ones compiles strict and passes
irdiff against LLVM 22 (bfd, lld, mold) and LLVM 19.

The census (`census_x64.rs`): 194 of 220 bodies before, 197 after; with
the 4 x87 templates (`{st}`/`{st(1)}`, which the census's extraction does
not compile), 201 of 220.

## Evidence

- `llvm_mc_x64.rs`: every branch row's bytes and relocations equal
  `llvm-mc` 19 and 22 (`call S`, `S@PLT`, `S+16`, `S+0`, `S@PLT+4`, `jcc`,
  local labels in and out of the section).
- `emit_module_asm.rs`: 19 x86-64 modules (data, code, sections, symbols,
  `.set`, relaxation, numeric labels, a module function called from IR,
  `.symver` with `@`, `@@`, `@@@`, defined and undefined): symbols (as
  sets), sections and relocations equal `llc` 19 and 22.
- `inline_asm.rs` under qemu, linked with bfd and lld, plain and
  stressed: `fsqrt` (float, double, long double), `fpatan`, `fprem` (a
  loop on C2), `fxam`, `fsincos` (two outputs), `fldpi`, `fscale`; a
  template `call`/`jmp` to a C function that checks its stack alignment.
- Every 10th x86-64 corpus entry (646, `--strict`, PIC): 603 identical
  objects, 2 built only at the head (`dcl.asm/run.cpp`,
  `extension-asm.cpp`), 41 malformed input at both (metadata or a type
  defined twice).
- `hod_a64` unchanged and passing; AArch64 module assembly byte-identical.

## Stays refused

§14's table names each form with its count. `.cfi_*` in a template (15
census templates, valgrind's `CALL_FN_*`) needs, outside the assembler:
remember/restore state in `hod_eh`'s CFI program
(`crates/hod_eh/src/lib.rs:59`, `Op`), and the x86-64 prologue's
callee-saved rows written in order (`crates/hod_x64/src/frame.rs:372`
writes them after the body, which `crates/hod_cg/src/cx.rs:599-603`
refuses). `asm goto` with a template on x86-64 (`x64:inline-asm`, 2
corpus modules) is not built.
