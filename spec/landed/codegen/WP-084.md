# WP-084 — the short tail

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/tail.rs`, the hooks below,
`crates/hod_a64/tests/tail.rs`, `tests/ir/tail_wide.ll`,
`tests/ir/tail_intrinsics.ll`, `SysReg::CntvctEl0` and `crc32` in
`hod_mc_a64`, the heading "The short tail (WP-084)" in `spec/codegen.md`
§5.5.1, and the support-matrix rows.

## What is lowered

- `extractvalue` / `insertvalue` of `half` and `fp128` fields (matrix
  only: the aggregate code already moved them as bytes).
- `smul.with.overflow` / `umul.with.overflow` of 65 .. 2048 bits: both
  operands extended to 2N bits, the 2N-bit product by `aggr::multiply`,
  the flag from its upper N bits (unsigned: any bit set; signed: any bit
  different from bit N-1). Wider is `a64:wide-mul-overflow`.
- `fptosi` / `fptoui` to, and `sitofp` / `uitofp` from, integers of 65 ..
  128 bits, from and to `half`, `float`, `double`, `fp128`, through
  compiler-rt's `__fix[uns]{h,s,d,t}fti` and `__float[un]ti{s,d,t}f`, as
  `llc` (a `half` result: the `float` call then `fcvt`). Wider is
  `a64:wide-fp-cast`.
- `switch` on integers wider than 64 bits: a constant condition is a
  branch; otherwise one `cmp`/`ccmp` chain per case over the limbs.
- `llvm.readcyclecounter`: `mrs CNTVCT_EL0` (`llc`'s).
- `llvm.aarch64.crc32{b,h,w,x,cb,ch,cw,cx}`: one instruction (`llc`
  refuses them without `+crc`; hod emits them regardless).
- `sshl.sat` / `ushl.sat` up to 64 bits; wider is `a64:intrinsic-wide`.
- `is.constant`: an aggregate literal (and a constant expression of
  constants) is manifest, as LLVM's `isManifestConstant`.
- `is.constant` / `objectsize`: the operand chain `llc` deletes is not
  emitted. A pre-pass runs only in functions calling one of them: per
  block, in reverse, an instruction with a result, no effect, and no use
  left but folded calls is dropped (a value used by a phi or in another
  block stays, as SelectionDAG exports it). Other functions pay one scan
  of their calls.
- `invoke` with an odd-width integer result: `hod_cg` clears the leaves
  above the width in the result's home after the call (the landing pad
  path never sees the value).

## Hooks (outside `hod_a64/src/tail.rs`)

- `hod_cg/src/call.rs`, `FnCx::call` after `finish`: `clear_odd_leaves`
  of an `invoke`'s `Mem` result.
- `hod_cg/src/sym.rs`: the 14 compiler-rt functions appended to
  `runtime_fns!`.
- `hod_a64/src/aggr.rs`: `cast` (FP arm), `overflow_intrinsic`, and eight
  helpers made `pub(crate)`.
- `hod_a64/src/control.rs` (switch), `intrinsic.rs` (readcyclecounter,
  is.constant, the shl.sat and crc32 arm), `call.rs` (the
  `a64:invoke-odd-int-result` refusal removed), `target.rs` (`FnState::tail`,
  `tail::begin`, the dropped check at the top of `lower`), `lib.rs`.

## What stays refused, by name

- `const-expr:trunc`: the corpus' two are truncated differences of
  `blockaddress`es; a label-difference constant form is needed in
  `hod_ir`, both readers and emission.
- `special-global:llvm.global.annotations`: the readers report it, and it
  has appending linkage; dropping it is a reader change (`hod_ll/src/
  globals.rs`, `hod_bc/src/module.rs`). With the global removed, both
  corpus modules compile.
- `type:bfloat`: a whole type class (ABI, conversions, vectors).
- Integers wider than 128 bits in FP conversions (`a64:wide-fp-cast`),
  `smul`/`umul.with.overflow` wider than 2048 bits, `sshl.sat` wider than
  64 bits.

## Evidence

- `crates/hod_a64/tests/tail.rs`, 14 tests, under bfd, lld and mold, hod
  plain, `Stress::ALL` and far, against `llc -O0 -global-isel=0
  -fast-isel=0` and `hod_interp` where it models the types: wide multiply
  overflow 680 calls (65 .. 256 bits); conversions to wide integers 288
  (+144 interpreter), from wide integers 576 (+288); wide switch 260;
  saturating shifts 540 against a Rust model; crc32 96 against `llc`
  (`+crc`); fp128 / half aggregate fields; readcyclecounter monotone;
  is.constant of aggregates; folded operand chains (a null dereference
  not evaluated, as `llc`); an odd-width `invoke` result; the refusals.
- `tests/ir/tail_wide.ll`, `tests/ir/tail_intrinsics.ll` through
  `cargo xtask irdiff`: PASS under bfd, lld and mold.
  Neither holds `llvm.aarch64.crc32*` (llc for x86_64 aborts on it, and
  every seed test is also built for the other target) nor a signed
  multiply-overflow wider than 64 bits (llc 19 calls `__muloti4`, which
  only compiler-rt has); those two are covered by the tests above.
- Mutations, each caught and reverted: (1) the signed flag without the
  `eor` of the sign (wide multiply); (2) the `ccmp` fallback NZCV made Z
  (wide switch); (3) the pre-pass disabled (the folded null dereference
  crashes); (4) the `invoke` clear removed (1535 for 1407).
