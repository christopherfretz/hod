## What was checked outside hod

- **`byval` aligned above 16** (`llc` 19 and 22 agree): the caller
  realigns its frame (`and rsp, -64` in the prologue), copies to
  `rsp + O` with O rounded to the alignment, and the callee reads
  `rbp + 16 + O`. The pointer passed must have the `byval` alignment:
  `llc` copies the pointee with aligned `movaps` (a test that passed an
  8-aligned pointer with `align 128` crashed `llc`'s own program).
- **Vectors of `i128`**: `llc` 22 passes `<N x i128>` as 2N ordinary
  8-byte pieces (one may sit in `r9`, the next on the stack, 8-aligned);
  `llc` 19 keeps a `<1 x i128>` a pair and splits longer vectors in yet
  another way. Results: `<1 x i128>` in `rax`:`rdx`, longer in memory,
  the same in both.
- **Vectors of `x86_fp80`**: both pass each element in a 16-byte stack
  slot, return one or two elements in `st0`/`st1` and three or more in
  memory, and lay the vector out packed (`fstpt 10(%rdi)` for lane 1).
- **`x64:sjlj-live-value`**: not lowered; what is missing is in
  `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.18 (4).
- **kiln**: of the 35 modules, `simd.class/simd_unary.pass.cpp` does not
  compile with the current `kiln++` (a vector `!` converting `char`
  lanes); it was not measured.
