# WP-104 — the coroutine plan (design 0005, package C1)

Landed: `spec/xform.md` §1 to §5, and `hod_xform::coro` that implements
§3. Design 0005 §3.4 is updated in the same commit: `heap_values` is a
`BitSet<InstId>` (not `ValueId`, which `hod_ir` does not have), the plan
gains `begin_value_copies`, and the extensions kiln's maintainers asked
for (values before `coro.begin`, parameter copies, arguments after
`coro.end`, dynamic `alloca`, wrappers always present) are written down.
`hod_cg` and `hod_a64` are untouched; classify still refuses
`attr:presplitcoroutine`.

## What it did

- `crates/hod_xform/src/bitset.rs`: `BitSet<I: Idx>` (§2).
- `crates/hod_xform/src/coro/mod.rs`: `plan(&Module, &Body) ->
  Result<Option<CoroPlan>, Refusal>`, `analyze` (the plan with its slot
  classes and the violations found, for tests and the census),
  `CoroPlan`, `Suspend`, `SlotClass`, `Region`, `Violation`, and the 11
  `coro:*` refusals as `pub const` plus `REFUSALS`.
- `crates/hod_xform/src/coro/analysis.rs`: header recognition (both

  aliases (sorted table), rules 1 to 5 as one forward dataflow per track,
  SSA heap values, begin copies, the suspended region, the safety
  conditions of §3.6 as checked properties (`Violation`s, refused as
  `coro:frame-access-after-suspend`), and the plan's invariants

- Reader support for lifetime markers and `!coro.outside.frame`
  (`spec/ir.md` §5.2, §5.5), outside ownership:
  - `crates/hod_ir/src/body.rs:170,175-187,209-222,479-480,517,548,756-765,808,835`

  - `crates/hod_ir/tests/debug_body.rs:669-671` (`Body` is 568 bytes, was 544);

## Evidence (`spec/xform.md` §4)

- Gate, pre-opt corpus (50 modules × 2 majors, 134 coroutines): LLVM's
  frame allocas 687 (47 exempt by rule 3), spills 4, all inside hod's
  heap slots ∪ begin copies; hod has 2 heap slots LLVM does not. Refused:
  12 coroutines in exactly the 6 contract-postcondition modules, all
  `coro:frame-access-after-suspend`. The `-g` set gives the same result.
- libc++: 25 rebuilt modules, 10 with coroutines, 17 coroutines; 89 frame
  allocas (2 exempt), 0 refusals, superset holds.
- Begin copies: 5 slots in 2 modules per major, as the design predicts.

## Speed

- Synthetic body, release: 100k instructions 32.2 ns/inst, 200k
  32.9 ns/inst; the 2N/N time ratio is 2.04.
- Largest corpus body (208 instructions): 12.1 µs, 58 ns/inst. Largest
  libc++ body (172 instructions): 57 ns/inst.
