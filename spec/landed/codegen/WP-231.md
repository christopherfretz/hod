# WP-231: under-aligned atomics on x86-64; `.ident` in module assembly

Spec: `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.10 ("Below its size's alignment");
`spec/asm.md` §9 (`.ident`).

Two refusals met in real code, each of which `llc` builds.

## What was built

- `crates/hod_x64/src/atomic.rs`: an atomic access whose alignment is
  below its size calls libatomic's generic, size-parameterized routine,
  as `llc`'s `AtomicExpand` does (`expandAtomicOpToLibcall`): a load
  `__atomic_load(size, ptr, &ret, order)`, a store
  `__atomic_store(size, ptr, &value, order)`, `atomicrmw xchg`
  `__atomic_exchange(size, ptr, &value, &ret, order)`, `cmpxchg`
  `__atomic_compare_exchange(size, ptr, &expected, &desired, success,
  failure)`. Values travel through frame slots aligned to their size;
  the orders are C's `memory_order` numbers; a `cmpxchg` keeps the
  expected value in its result's slot, where the routine writes the
  cell's value on failure, and stores the returned `bool` as the flag.
  An operand's register is given back once the value is in its slot, so
  the size and ordering arguments fit a small register pool.
- `crates/hod_cg/src/sym.rs`: the four routines in `RuntimeFn`.
- `crates/hod_asm/src/module.rs`: `.ident "string"` is read on both
  targets and collected in `ModuleCode::idents`. The bytes are the ones
  between the quotes as written: LLVM reads no escape in this directive.
- `crates/hod_cg/src/emit/module_asm.rs`: the strings are written to
  `.comment` as LLVM's ELF streamer writes them: `SHT_PROGBITS`,
  `SHF_MERGE | SHF_STRINGS`, entries of one byte, a NUL, then each
  string with its NUL.

## Evidence

- `crates/hod_x64/tests/leftovers.rs`
  `under_aligned_atomics_run_against_libatomic`: 52 functions (load,
  store, `xchg` and, for the integers and the pointer, strong and weak
  `cmpxchg`, over `i16`, `i32`, `i64`, `ptr`, `half`, `float`, `double`
  and `i128` at alignments below their size). hod plain and under every
  stress option, `llc` 22 and `llc` 19 are each linked with `-latomic`
  by bfd, lld and mold and run: 208 calls a leg on cells placed at the
  access's alignment past a 64-byte boundary. The driver works out the
  cell and the result by copying bytes and checks the bytes around the
  cell; no leg has a wrong call.
- `crates/hod_x64/tests/atomic.rs`
  `under_aligned_atomics_call_libatomic_generic`: 13 forms, plain and
  stressed, name the routine in the object; `refused_atomics_by_name`
  keeps the remaining refusals.
- `crates/hod_cg/tests/emit_module_asm.rs`, the `ident` case on both
  targets: the whole object, `.comment` included, is what `llc` 22 and
  19 write for two `.ident` directives, the second with `\t`, `\x41` and
  `\"` (kept as written).
- `crates/hod_asm/src/module.rs` `ident_strings`: order, the raw bytes,
  the forms refused (no operand, two operands, not a string, a quote
  inside, a backslash before the last quote).

## What stays refused

- `x64:atomic-unaligned`: an under-aligned `atomicrmw` other than
  `xchg` (`llc` builds a `__atomic_compare_exchange` loop), and an
  under-aligned store, `xchg` or `cmpxchg` whose value operand is its
  own pointer operand.
- `a64:atomic-unaligned`: AArch64 still refuses every under-aligned
  atomic access (WP-232).
- `!llvm.ident` is still dropped: with both it and `.ident`, hod's
  `.comment` is the first part of `llc`'s.

## Notes

- `crates/hod_cli/tests/kiln_e2e.rs` used `.ident` as the directive hod
  declines, to keep the fallback path tested; it now uses `.uleb128`
  (`asm:directive/uleb128`).
