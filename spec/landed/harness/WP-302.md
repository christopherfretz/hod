# WP-302 — `tests/ir` at i686

A harness package: no change under `crates/`, nothing hod emits moves.
Landed: the `kept-off` directive (`xtask/src/irdiff/directives.rs`,
`KEPT_OFF_REASONS`; the SKIP and its summary line in `engine.rs` and
`report.rs`), 38 seeds kept off i686, three seeds made valid for every
target, the i686 quick-tier run in both tiers of the gate
(`xtask::ci::irdiff_runs`), and `spec/harness.md` "Kept off a target",
"Test directives", "Summary" and "In the gate".

## Why

WP-293's first `cargo xtask irdiff --target i686` exited 3: every ERROR
row was the oracle's (`llc` for i686 and the i686 toolchain), from seeds
written for the 64-bit targets. With them the run could not be a gate
step.

## The ERROR rows, by cause (eb7ae0d2, linkers bfd, lld, mold)

Before: LLVM 22 77 rows (27 seeds), LLVM 19 78 rows (28 seeds); the quick
tier 15 rows (5 seeds) with each. None was hod's: hod declined every
test with a clean reason.

| cause | seeds | the oracle's error |
|---|---|---|
| C++ ABI of LP64 (`_Znwm`, `_ZdlPvm`) | 10 per dialect | `undefined symbol: operator new(unsigned long)` |
| `long`/`size_t` as `i64` (`%ld`, `malloc(i64)`) | `coro/params-before-begin-*`, `const_operands`, `varargs_caller` | output `new 3 4 \x00`; `same=0`; SIGSEGV |
| `pthread_t` as `i64` | `tls`, `tls_desc` | SIGSEGV (`pthread_join(i64, ptr)`) |

| x87 excess precision | `exit_float` (19, 22) | exit 103 for 7: `6.5 / -0.375` compared unrounded |

## What was done

- **Made valid for every target (3):** `const_operands.ll` (`%ld` to
  `%lld` for its two `i64` values), `frame_intrinsics.ll` and
  `coro/noop.ll` (`getelementptr i8, ptr %p, i64 8` to
  `getelementptr ptr, ptr %p, i64 1`). hod's objects of the old and the
  new text, for AArch64 and x86-64: `frame_intrinsics` and `noop`
  byte-identical; `const_operands` differs only in `.rodata` (the format
  string is two bytes longer), `.text` and relocations identical.
- **Kept off i686 (38 files):** `lp64-abi` 28 (the 24 clang-written
  coroutine seeds that call `_Znwm` or print `long`, `gro-throws-uaf-*`
  among them although its oracle is skipped; `coro/handles`, `tls`,
  `tls_desc`, `varargs_caller`), `va-list-abi` 2, `runtime-routine` 4,
  `llc-crash` 2, `llc-abi` 1 (LLVM 19 only), `x87-precision` 1. Per
  run: 25 with LLVM 22, 26 with LLVM 19 (the other dialect's twins are
  SKIP by dialect first).
- **Oracle defects** (not 64-bit assumptions): `llc-crash` (2 seeds,
  both majors), `llc-abi` (LLVM 19), `x87-precision`, LLVM 19's bfloat
  return, and libgcc's SSE2 half/bfloat routines against an SSE-less
  runner (a toolchain/runner mismatch). They are kept off under their
  own classes, so they stay visible.
