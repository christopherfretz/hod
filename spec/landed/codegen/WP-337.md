# WP-337: i386 `llvm.is.fpclass`, `x86_fp80` / `i80` bit casts, `llvm.modf`

Spec: `spec/codegen-x86.md` §21 (appended as "§21", numbered §21 at the join of wave 45). Module:
`crates/hod_x86/src/fpclass.rs` (one `target.rs` arm, before the SSE and
x87 arms). Tests: `crates/hod/tests/i386_fpclass.rs`.

## Where it comes from

A sample of 1,026 libc++ tests built for i686 by clang 22 (clang's
default i686 CPU: `cmov`, no SSE) left 31 tests with a module hod
refuses; `is.fpclass` (`x86:intrinsic`, 20 tests), `bitcast x86_fp80 to
i80` (`x86:float:fp80-cast`, 1), `llvm.modf.f64` (1) and an under-aligned
8-byte `cmpxchg` (`x86:atomic:libatomic`, 2) were 24 of them.

## What landed

1. `is.fpclass` of `float`, `double`, `x86_fp80`, x87 and SSE functions:
   integer tests of the home (the value rounded to its type, a rounding
   point as in `llc`). No barrier: `llc` 19 and 22 never branch for it
   (all 1024 masks, three types, CPU `generic` and `i686`, an operand
   computed in the block). The `x86_fp80` odd encodings as `llc` answers
   (§21); pseudo-denormals as `llc` 22.
2. `bitcast` `x86_fp80` <-> `i80` (10 bytes between homes) and `icmp` of
   an `i80` with zero (every predicate): clang's `signbit(long double)`.
   Other `i80` operations stay `x86:i64:wide`.
3. `llvm.modf` of `float`, `double`, `x86_fp80` on i386: `modff` / `modf`
   / `modfl`, an x87 barrier. New: `Intrinsic::Modf` (`hod_ir`),
   `RuntimeFn::{Modff, Modf, Modfl}` (`hod_cg`), the classifier row
   `intrinsic:llvm.modf` (i386 supported; x86-64 and AArch64 `todo`).
4. `x86:atomic:libatomic` stays (llc calls `__atomic_compare_exchange`).

## Measurements

- clang 22 writes `llvm.modf.*` for `modf` / `modff` / `modfl` on i686,
  x86-64 and AArch64; clang 19 calls the routines; kiln calls the
  routines (no module of the kiln corpora calls `llvm.modf`). `llc` 22 at
  `-O0` lowers it to the plain call on all three targets; LLVM 19 does
  not know it (it calls a function named `llvm.modf.f64`).
- `cmpxchg i64 ... align 4` on i686: `llc` 19 and 22 call
  `__atomic_compare_exchange` at `generic`, `pentium`, `i686`.
- `x86_fp80` classes: see §21 (the masks `llc` compares, the
  inverted tests, 19 against 22).
- Evidence at landing: every `i386*` binary of `-p hod` passes plain and
  under `Stress::ALL`; irdiff i686 132 PASS, 0 FAIL, 0 ERROR at LLVM 22
  and LLVM 19; compare-binaries against 8e6e852c: 14114 comparisons, 0
  different, 0 changed outcome.

## Divergence to decide

x86-64's `is.fpclass` of a pseudo-denormal (`spec/codegen.md` §5.6.12,
`crates/hod_x64/src/x87.rs` `fp_class`, test
`crates/hod_x64/tests/leftovers2.rs:915`) answers "subnormal" for every
mask (as `fxam`); i386 now answers as `llc` 22, as this package was
asked. They differ at 459 masks. One rule should hold on both targets.
