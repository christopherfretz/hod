## Discarded `available_externally` bodies (item 6)

Not changed. The pre-opt form of `re.alg/regex-match-libstdcxx.cpp`
(LLVM 22, the WP-021 unit with the most `std::` functions) has 1929
definitions in 49,794 body lines; 5 are `available_externally`, all
`alwaysinline` (held, 46 lines), none discarded. Over the 1331 LLVM 22
conformance modules of the corpus and the 150 pre-opt modules at hand, no discarded body
either: clang and kiln emit `available_externally` at `-O0` only for
`alwaysinline` functions. Skipping their parse would save nothing
measurable and would let a malformed one through (today
`a_malformed_discarded_body_is_still_malformed` holds).
