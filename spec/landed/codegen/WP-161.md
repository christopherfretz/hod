# WP-161 — x86-64 arithmetic on `i128` and `x86_fp80` lanes; `pause`

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/vector.rs` (arithmetic on `i128` lanes:
`wide_binary`, `wide_cast`, `lane_amount`; `x86_fp80` lanes:
`fp80_binary`, `wide_fneg`), `crates/hod_x64/src/mask.rs` (`fcmp` of
`x86_fp80` lanes, `wide_fcmp`; `select` of `i128` and `x86_fp80` lanes,
`wide_select`), `crates/hod_x64/src/target.rs` (the three hooks),
`crates/hod_x64/src/aggr.rs` (`limbwise`, `shift_const`, `shift_var`
public for the lanes), `crates/hod_x64/src/intrinsic.rs` and
`hod_ir`'s `Intrinsic::X86Sse2Pause` with its matrix row
(`never(X64_ONLY)`); `docs/status/support.md`; `spec/codegen.md`
§5.6.19. Tests: `crates/hod_x64/tests/wide_arith.rs`, and
`leftovers3.rs`'s `wide_lane_refusals_by_name` (the `add` and `zext`
cases that now compile replaced by a conversion to `half` lanes),
`vector.rs`'s `what_is_refused_is_refused_by_name` (`add` of `i128`
lanes replaced by `llvm.umin` of them), and the new intrinsic's row in
`hod_ir/tests/body_llvm.rs`.

## Evidence

Every leg runs under `qemu-x86_64`; hod plain and hod under every stress
option are both legs, beside `llc -O0 -fast-isel=0
-relocation-model=pic` 22 and 19. "text" is hod's `.text` over `llc`
22's (mostly the hashing that reads the lanes back).

Mutations (each caught, then reverted):

| item | mutation | result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | the lane offset of an `i128` binary operator `w.offset(lane / 2 * 2)` | 10,724 disagreements |
| 2 | `sext`'s high half `sar 62` instead of `sar 63` | 51 disagreements |
| 3 | `fcmp` of `x86_fp80` lanes with its operands pushed the other way round | 1,920 disagreements |

Differences from `llc`: none in results. The division routines are
called once per lane, as `llc`'s scalarized code does; a shift by a
variable amount of 128 or more gives a value other than `llc`'s
(poison, not compared).
