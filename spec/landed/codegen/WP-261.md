# WP-261: the blocks `llc` deletes after folding `is.constant`

Spec: `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.8 "Folded constants and the blocks they
remove", §5.5.1 "WP-084" (the dropped operands of a folded call).

hod folded `llvm.is.constant` to false and still emitted the arm the
fold made dead, so an object referenced what only that arm calls. A C++
library's way of making a misuse fail to link (a call of a function that
is declared and never defined, under `if (__builtin_constant_p(x))`)
then failed to link with hod and linked with `llc -O0`: `llc`'s IR pass
`LowerConstantIntrinsics` (LLVM 22: inside `pre-isel-intrinsic-lowering`)
replaces the call by its constant, simplifies the users, rewrites a
`br` on the result and deletes the blocks no longer reached. hod now does
the same on both targets.

## Evidence: what `llc` 19 and 22 do

Experiments under `.build/findings/wp-261/exp/` (not committed), each
built by `llc-19` and `llc-22` `-O0` for x86-64 and for AArch64
(`-global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0`); the undefined symbols compared with
`llvm-nm -u` (`cmp.sh`). LLVM 19 and 22 agree in every case.

- `bcp.ll` (GCC `bcp-1.c` shapes), `seed.ll` (the reproducer):
  the dead arm and everything only it reaches are gone.
- `simp.ll`: what the recursive simplification folds: `xor` with
  `true`, `zext`/`icmp ne 0`, `and false, %p`, `select` on the result,
  `freeze`, a `phi` of the result and the same constant, `icmp ugt 0,
  %y`. `llvm.expect` of the result is not folded by `llc` (hod's reader
  drops `expect`, so hod folds it: hod references less; none in the
  corpus).
- `mab.ll`: only when a folded `br` left a block without predecessors
  does `llc` run `removeUnreachableBlocks`: then a block ends after a
  `noreturn` call and a `switch` on the constant folds; otherwise a
  `switch` on the folded value keeps both arms.
- `edge.ll`: `blockaddress` of a deleted block (`llc` writes `1`, hod
  the function's trap, as for every unreachable address-taken block,
  §9.6; no symbol differs), `indirectbr` and `callbr` targets in a
  deleted block, `musttail` of a `noreturn` callee (does not end the
  block).
- `eh.ll`: an `invoke` in the dead arm; its landing pad goes with it
  when nothing else unwinds there, stays when a live `invoke` shares it.
- `os.ll`: `objectsize` of an `alloca [16 x i8]` is 16 and of a global
  `[10 x i8]` 10 in `llc` (its evaluator); hod gives `-1`. hod folds
  with its own value (the value its program computes): a known gap,
  recorded for a follow-up object-size evaluator.
- `fa.ll` / `fb.ll`: an `alloca` used only in a deleted block: hod's
  `.text` equals the one for the IR with the block deleted by hand.
- `bcpg.cpp`, `dv.cpp` (clang-22 `-g`, then `llc-22` and hod):
  `llvm-dwarfdump --verify` clean on both targets; no line row for the
  deleted arm's line (hod {2,3,4,7,9,11}, `llc` {0,2,3,4,7,9,11});
  gdb steps 4, 7, 9; a variable declared in the deleted arm has no DIE
  in either writer's output, nor its lexical block.

## What was built

- `crates/hod_cg/src/const_fold.rs`: `Lci::plan` simulates the pass per
  function (reverse post-order of the calls, the recursive
  simplification with hod's set, the `br` rewrite with
  `removePredecessor`, then `markAliveBlocks` or the plain walk), and
  gives each instruction an action: emit, drop (SelectionDAG's dead
  nodes, the WP-084 rule, moved here from both targets and kept exact
  for a function in which nothing folds), jump (a rewritten
  terminator), or unreachable (the end of a block ended early). Four
  named refusals for what `llc` rewrites and hod does not model.
- `crates/hod_cg/src/driver.rs`: `mark_reachable` plans and clears the
  deleted blocks; `terminator_phi_stores` stores only the kept edge.
- `crates/hod_x64/src/target.rs`, `crates/hod_a64/src/target.rs`:
  `lower` follows the action. The per-target copies of the WP-084
  decision (`intrinsic::begin`, `tail::begin`) are gone.

## Tests

- `tests/ir/is_constant_dead_blocks.ll`: each simplification, the
  `objectsize` fold, the `switch` and `noreturn` cases of
  `markAliveBlocks`, a folded branch in a loop; every deleted block
  calls a function no object defines (link and run, both targets).
- `tests/ir/is_constant_dead_invoke.ll`: an `invoke` in the dead arm,
  its own landing pad and one shared with a live `invoke` (C++
  runtime, link and run).
- `crates/hod/tests/const_fold_blocks.rs`: undefined symbols equal to
  `llc` 19 and 22 on both targets, the refusals, a folded call that
  feeds no branch changes nothing.
- `crates/hod_x64/tests/intrinsic.rs`: the dropped operands, through
  `hod_cg::const_fold::analyse`.

## Survey

`.build/findings/wp-261/survey.py` over the saved corpus modules that
call `is.constant` or `objectsize` (108 modules): hod refuses none, and
no undefined symbol of hod's is missing from `llc`'s, before and after
(the corpus has no dead arm that references an undefined name; the
reproducer is the test). None of the four refusals fires. 478
`objectsize` calls, 2 of them directly on an `alloca` or a global (one
module, built by LLVM 19 and by 22): the known gap.
