# WP-131 — `x86_fp80` on x86-64 (the x87)

Landed: `crates/hod_x64/src/x87.rs`; `crates/hod_x64/tests/x87.rs` with
`tests/common/x87_gen.rs`; the `type:x86_fp80` row and the x86-64
`x86_fp80` cells of 20 instruction rows and 47 intrinsic rows on
(`hod_ir` `classify/matrix.rs`, `tests/classify_matrix.rs`
`x87_rows_of_wp_131`, `docs/status/support.md`); `tests/ir/x86_fp80.ll`
runs on x86-64; `spec/codegen.md` §5.6.12.

## Hooks into other groups' files

- `crates/hod_x64/src/target.rs` `load`/`store`: a part in `st0`/`st1`
  goes to `x87::load_part`/`store_part`.
- `crates/hod_x64/src/call.rs` `guard`: no longer refuses the x87.
- `crates/hod_x64/src/float.rs` `cond_br`: a deferred `x86_fp80` compare
  goes to `x87::cond_br`.
- `crates/hod_x64/src/aggr.rs` `select`: a fused `x86_fp80` compare goes
  to `x87::select`.
- `crates/hod_x64/src/fpintr.rs` `intrinsic`: an intrinsic whose first
  operand is `x86_fp80` goes to `x87::intrinsic`.

## Refused, by name

- `x64:x87-bfloat`: `fptrunc x86_fp80` to `bfloat` (`llc` 19 cannot
  select it; 22 calls `__truncxfbf2`, which the runtime table lacks).
- `x64:x87-intrinsic`: a non-constant `is.fpclass` mask, a `powi`/`ldexp`
  exponent other than `i32`, an integer result wider than 64 bits.
- `x64:x87-cast`: an opcode that is no conversion (invalid IR);
  `x64:wide-fp-cast`: an integer wider than 128 bits.

## Evidence (`crates/hod_x64/tests/x87.rs`)

Every function compiled plainly and under every stress option, run under
`qemu-x86_64` against `hod_interp` (`NanPolicy::Host`, exact; NaN for
NaN) and bit for bit against `llc -O0 -fast-isel=0` 22 and 19:

- binary (`fadd` .. `frem`, `fneg`, a chain), every compare (16
  predicates as a value, fused into `select` and `br`, used twice),
  `fpext`/`fptrunc` with `half`, `bfloat`, `float`, `double`, `fp128`,
  `fptosi`/`fptoui`/`sitofp`/`uitofp` of 11 widths from 1 to 128,
  `select`/`phi` (a loop, a swap)/`freeze`/constants/`load`/`store`
  (aligned and packed), 72 intrinsic functions, `is.fpclass` with all
  1024 masks on 18 values. Edge values: zeros, denormals,
  pseudo-denormals, unnormals, pseudo-zeros, infinities,
  pseudo-infinities, quiet and signalling NaNs with payloads,
  pseudo-NaNs, `2^k` and neighbours for `k` in 7, 15, 31, 32, 63, 64.
- Calls: `x86_fp80` arguments in memory among register ones, results in
  `st0`, `{x86_fp80, x86_fp80}` in `st0`/`st1`, `{x86_fp80, double}`,
  eleven arguments, a value live across calls, a variadic call to C,
  C callees (`_Complex long double`); the callees also compiled apart and
  linked hod-with-`llc` both ways.
- Disassembly: the flags rule (only `fstp`, `fld`, `fxch` and moves
  between `fucomip` and its readers), the stack empty at every call and
  between instructions, no signalling compare.
- Mutations caught: `fsubp` as `fsubrp` (binary: 12591 disagreements);
  no truncation in the control word (integer conversions: 7095); no
  `fxch` for a part in `st1` (calls: 756).

Where `llc` is not compared: `minimum`/`maximum` (it cannot select
them; the interpreter is the oracle, glibc's `fminimuml`); a
pseudo-denormal into `is.fpclass` (19 and 22 differ) or `x * 1.0`
(folded); invalid encodings into `minimumnum`/`maximumnum` (22 inline
passes them through); a NaN's sign through `fadd (fneg x), y` (folded
to `fsub`). The interpreter is not compared where the C library computes
on invalid encodings or signalling NaNs as integers.
