# WP-297 — i386 I6: the ABI (design 0008)

Landed: `hod_x86::abi::assign`, the i386 System V convention as `llc` 19
and 22 implement it, normative in `spec/abi-x86.md` (status, §1.2 to
§12 filled in, §3.2's `byval` alignment corrected from measurement);
the callee pop of a hidden or `sret` pointer; `st0` results; `musttail`
tail exits; the classifier's i386 column; the ABI grid.

## What compiles

C, `coldcc` (= C) and `fastcc` without floating point; stack slots of 4
(`i64`, `double` 8, `x86_fp80` 12, all at alignment 4); IR aggregate
arguments leaf by leaf; `inreg` (`eax`, `edx`, `ecx`, a word at a time);
`nest`; `byval` at its alignment up to 16; `byref` as a pointer; `sret`
plain (callee `ret $4`) and `inreg`; results in `eax`, `edx:eax`, up to
three words in `eax`/`edx`/`ecx`, one floating leaf in `st0`, larger ones
through a hidden pointer the callee pops (caller side; a definition
returning in memory is still `x86:aggregate`, I11); variadic calls, all
on the stack; `musttail` (direct and indirect, stack arguments rewritten
in place, `sret` and callee pop included).

## Decisions

- `hod_cg::abi::Assignment::callee_pop` and `hod_cg::CallEmit::callee_pop`
  (zero default; AArch64 and x86-64 never set it): the bytes the callee
  removes, which `frame::emit_call` puts back with `lea esp, [esp - n]`.
  Replaces a thread-local of the first session.
- An indirect call with an argument in `edx` is refused
  (`x86:abi:indirect-edx`) by `abi::check_call`, called from
  `call::check` in place of the old `musttail` refusal. `llc` takes a free
  register (`eax`, else `esi`); hod's callee register is fixed per target
  (`RegInfo::callee`), so choosing one per call is a `hod_cg` change,
  not made for a `-mregparm` shape.
- `musttail` with stack arguments and an argument in `ecx` is refused
  (`x86:abi:musttail`): the call sequence copies the staged stack
  arguments into the incoming area after placing register arguments,
  and the target's `copy` glue uses `ecx`.
- Two floating-point result leaves are refused (`x86:abi:x87-pair`)
  rather than ordered: the call sequence moves result parts in one order
  on both sides, the x87 stack needs opposite ones.
- `i128`/`fp128` arguments are refused (`x86:abi:wide-arg`): `llc` 19 and
  22 align them differently.
- Classifier: `SupportRow::i386` (x86-64's cell unless `on_i386`); ON on
  i386: `cc:coldcc`, `attr:inreg`, `attr:nest`; not-implemented on i386
  (`x86:abi:cc` in the back end): `cc:x86_stdcallcc`,
  `cc:x86_fastcallcc`, `cc:x86_thiscallcc`, `cc:preserve_mostcc`,
  `cc:preserve_allcc`, `cc:x86_64_sysvcc`, `cc:win64cc`. The session
  gives the i386 classifier `TargetStackArgs::new(&X86)`, so `musttail`
  is no longer `musttail:stack-args-unknown` there.
  `docs/status/support.md` gains an `i386` section (counts and the rows
  that differ from x86_64); the existing tables are unchanged.

## Evidence

`crates/hod/tests/i386_abi.rs`: the grid (103 signatures, both
directions, hod plain and `Stress::ALL` against `llc` 19 and 22: 4 runs)
and the fixed rows (dropped x87 results, a variadic call, results in
memory, `byref`, `musttail` a million deep in five shapes, an indirect
`inreg` call; GNU ld, lld, mold: 12 runs), every run `ok`.

## Edits outside the crate

- `crates/hod_cg/src/abi.rs` (`Assignment::callee_pop`, `clear`),
  `crates/hod_cg/src/target.rs` (`CallEmit::callee_pop`),
  `crates/hod_cg/src/call.rs` (the `CallEmit` built with it).
- `crates/hod_x64/tests/abi/table.rs`, `crates/hod_a64/tests/abi/table.rs`:
  `callee_pop: 0` in the expected `Assignment`.
- `crates/hod/src/session.rs`: `X86_STACK_ARGS`.
- `crates/hod_ir/src/classify/matrix.rs` (the column),
  `crates/hod_ir/src/classify/report.rs` (the doc section).
- `crates/hod_cli/tests/cli.rs`: the i686 coverage-log case now uses an
  `i128` argument (`x86:abi:wide-arg`); the `i64` identity compiles.

## Left open

`fastcc` floating point (I12), two-leaf x87 results (I9), definitions
returning in memory (I11), `tailcc`/`stdcall`/`fastcall`/`thiscall` (no
package), a per-call callee register (`x86:abi:indirect-edx`).
