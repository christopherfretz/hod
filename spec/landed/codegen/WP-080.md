# WP-080 — vectors, the lane loop (design 0003, V3)

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/lanes.rs` (one unrolled loop over the lanes
for every element-wise operation), the per-lane seams of the scalar
lowerings, the hooks below, `crates/hod_a64/tests/lanes.rs`,
`tests/ir/vector_lanes.ll`, the heading "Vectors, the lane loop
(WP-080)" in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1, and the support-matrix rows.

## The seam (the scalar kernels, one lane in registers)

- `int::lane_binary(cx, op, bits, ra, rb)`, `int::lane_cast(cx, op,
  from, to, r)`.
- `float::lane_binary`, `float::lane_frem` (a call of `fmod`/`fmodf`),
  `float::lane_fneg`, `float::lane_convert`.
- `intrinsic::lane_unary`, `lane_min_max`, `lane_funnel`,
  `lane_saturating`, `lane_three_way`.
- `fpintr::lane_plan` → `LaneFp::{Inline, Call}`, `fpintr::lane_inline`,
  `fpintr::lane_call` (`half` widened to `float` for the call, the
  result narrowed).

`lanes.rs` sets the operands up once (an `Fpr` vector spilled to a
scratch image, a `Mem` vector used in place, equal operands sharing an
image), then for each lane loads the lane(s), calls the kernel, stores
the result lane; an `Fpr` result is reloaded once at the end.

- `crates/hod_a64/src/target.rs:185` (`fneg` of class `Mem`; it fell to

- `crates/hod_a64/src/intrinsic.rs:114` (first line of `intrinsic`).
- `pub mod lanes;` in `lib.rs`.

## What is lowered

On vectors of `i8`, `i16`, `i32`, `i64`, `ptr`, `half`, `float`,
`double`, 1 to 256 lanes, both classes: `add` .. `xor`, the shifts,
`udiv`, `sdiv`, `urem`, `srem`; `fadd` .. `fdiv`, `frem`, `fneg`; `trunc`,
`zext`, `sext`, `fptrunc`, `fpext`, `fptoui`, `fptosi`, `uitofp`,
`sitofp`, `ptrtoint`, `ptrtoaddr`, `inttoptr` (any change of class);
the vector forms of `bswap`, `bitreverse`, `ctpop`, `ctlz`, `cttz`,
`fshl`, `fshr`, `abs`, `smax`, `smin`, `umax`, `umin`, `scmp`, `ucmp`, the
four `*.sat`, `ptrmask`, and of WP-070's floating-point intrinsics but
`frexp` and `is.fpclass`. Matrix: `Vector` added to 72 rows: `inst:udiv`,
`sdiv`, `urem`, `srem`, `frem`, `ptrtoint`, `ptrtoaddr`, `inttoptr`, and
the 64 rows of the intrinsics above.

## What is refused, by name

`a64:vector-length`, `a64:vector-wide-element` (`i128`, `fp128` lanes),
`a64:vector-odd-element`, `a64:vector-bfloat`; lanes of `i1` stay
`a64:mem-vector` / `a64:intrinsic-vector` (WP-081); `frexp` and
`is.fpclass` of a vector stay `a64:intrinsic-vector`.

## Evidence

- `tests/lanes.rs`, five suites, 2,646 functions, 34,334 lane
  comparisons with a Rust model (exp10 against `llc` 22), poison lanes
  excluded: integer operations 364 / 5,720, floating-point operations
  126 / 1,772, casts 567 / 8,132, integer intrinsics 518 / 6,216,
  floating-point intrinsics 1,071 / 12,494. Legs: hod plain under bfd,
  lld and mold, hod under `Stress::ALL`, `llc -O0 -global-isel=0
  -fast-isel=0` of LLVM 22 and 19, clang `ext_vector_type` and gcc
  `vector_size` C (operations and casts). Zero differences.
- Oracles left out where they are wrong or crash (`Func::in_llc`):
  LLVM 19 and 22 store a `<3 x i8>` truncated from 64-bit lanes into
  the wrong bytes (lane 2 to byte 1, byte 2 never written; clang with
  them); both crash on `ldexp` of `<1 x half>`; 22 cannot select
  `canonicalize` of `<1 x double>`; 19 refuses vector `lround` ..
  `llrint`, cannot select vector `canonicalize`, nor scalarize
  `fptosi.sat`/`fptoui.sat` of `<1 x half>`.
- `tests/ir/vector_lanes.ll` through `cargo xtask irdiff`: PASS under
  bfd, lld, mold.
- Cost (`the_code_is_linear_in_the_lanes`, 16 .. 256 lanes, equal bytes
  per lane asserted): `add <N x i32>` 22 bytes per lane, `udiv i64` 28,
  `fadd half` 31, `sitofp` to double 21, `fshl i8` 46; about 0.8 to
  1.3 µs per lane at 256 lanes in a debug build (not asserted). Nothing
  is allocated per lane.
- Mutations, each caught and reverted: (1) the reload of an `Fpr`
  result dropped (`lanes::done`): 4,567 differences over the five
  suites; (2) `sext` as `zext` in `int::lane_cast`: 240; (3) the narrowing
  of a `half` call result dropped (`fpintr::lane_call`): 1,020.
