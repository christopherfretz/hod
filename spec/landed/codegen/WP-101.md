# WP-101 — the short tail, third part

Landed: `crates/hod_a64/src/tail3.rs`, the hooks below,
`crates/hod_a64/tests/tail3.rs`, the heading "The short tail, third part
(WP-101)" in `spec/codegen.md` §5.5.1, `spec/ir.md` §4.2,
`spec/abi-a64.md` §3.3, §4.3, §10, §12, and the support-matrix rows.

## What is lowered

- Vectors of `i128` and `fp128` lanes: `<1 x i128>`/`<1 x fp128>` in a
  `Q` register, longer ones in memory; integer arithmetic limb by limb,
  `mul`, `icmp`/`fcmp` into masks, `select`, `fneg`, the `fp128` runtime
  calls per lane, casts between masks or narrower integer lanes and
  `i128` lanes, `insertelement`, `extractelement`, `shufflevector`. Across
  calls each `i128` element travels as an `i128`, where `llc` 19 and 22
  agree (`[n x i128]` rules of `spec/abi-a64.md` §3.3).
- Arrays of `bfloat` (HFAs, `[n x bfloat]`) when `NSRN + n <= 8`.
- Vectors of `bfloat` without `+bf16`: arithmetic, `frem` (`fmodf`),
  `fneg`, `fcmp`, `fpext` to `float`/`double`, `fptrunc` from `float`
  and `double`, lane by lane as the scalar.
- The NEON intrinsics `bfcvt`, `bfcvtn`, `bfcvtn2`, `bfdot`, `bfmlalb`,
  `bfmlalt`, `bfmmla`.
- A number minus an address as an operand (`sub (i64 k, ptrtoint @s)`
  and its `add`/`sub` variants): `ConstPool::neg_addr`, row
  `operand:neg-addr`; the address, then `neg`.
- `preserve_allcc` as caller and callee: `x9`..`x15` and `q8`..`q31`
  saved after the frame record with `DW_CFA_offset` rows, reloaded with
  `DW_CFA_restore` in every exit.
- `saddlv` of bytes sign-extends its 16-bit sum to `i32` (`smov`), as
  `llc` 19; `llc` 22 zero-extends it.

## What stays refused, by name

- `a64:vector-i128-op`: shifts and divisions of `i128` lanes (`llc` calls
  `__ashlti3`.. per lane; no corpus module).
- `a64:vector-wide-cast`: other casts with an `i128`/`fp128` lane side.
- `a64:vector-bfloat-cast`: other casts of `bfloat` lanes.
- `abi:vector-element`: a lone `<1 x i128>` argument or result (a `Q`
  value in two general registers).
- `abi:wide-int-array`: `[n x i128]` placements the selectors disagree on.
- `abi:bfloat-array`: a `bfloat` array that does not fit the `H`
  registers left (`llc` 22 and 19 place the stack pieces apart).
- `init:neg-addr`: a number minus an address in data or an aggregate
  operand (no relocation; `llc` refuses it too).

## Known `llc` defects

- `llc -O0` 19 and 22 round vector `bfloat` arithmetic from the
  operand's image (`ushr v1.4s, v0.4s, #16` of the loaded operand): its
  vector results differ from its scalar ones. hod follows the scalar; the
  tests' reference is `llc`'s build of the scalarized program, which 19
  and 22 agree on.
- `llc` 22 zero-extends `saddlv` of bytes.

  and far, against `llc -O0 -global-isel=0 -fast-isel=0` of LLVM 22 and
  19, linked under bfd, lld and mold: `i128` lanes (512 calls, also
  against `hod_interp`), `fp128` lanes, `i128` lanes across calls (9
  builds including hod caller with `llc` callee and the reverse),
  `bfloat` arrays across calls, `bfloat` lanes (576 calls against the
  scalarized `llc` program, NaNs canonicalized), the bf16 intrinsics (84
  calls), numbers minus addresses (direct, through the GOT, with
  offsets), `preserve_allcc` (C inline assembly puts sentinels in
  `x9`..`x15`, `q8`..`q31` around a `bl`; the callee calls a C function
  that clobbers them and walks the stack with `_Unwind_Backtrace`, which
  must find the caller's `x9`..`x15`; the CFI register set equals
  `llc`'s), and the refusals by name. `tests/neon.rs` checks `saddlv`.
- Mutations, each caught and reverted: (1) `q30`, `q31` not saved
  (`preserve_all_functions_keep_every_register_llc_keeps`: "q30
  changed"); (2) the `x9`..`x15` CFI rows one slot off (same test: "the
  unwinder finds other x9..x15"); (3) `saddlv` of bytes zero-extended
  again (`tests/neon.rs`: 2 calls differ).
- The 17 libc++ `simd` tests built by hod strict and run: 17 of 17
  pass, as with `llc`.
